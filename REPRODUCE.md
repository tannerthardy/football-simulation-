## Failure receipts and interpretation

Initial collector attempts hit a missing cueStart binding at the first controller
call, before a World tick. failed-collector-0.log preserves that failure. Early
failed absolute-path launch receipts were overwritten by their successful rerun;
there is no invented failed-run timing. Successful discovery receipts were copied
from the launcher's nested absolute-path destination without altering bytes.

perimeter-focused.json preserves the first focused failure: a new test forbade
all see calls during physical acquisition, including the defender's legitimate
key read. Only the test was corrected to forbid a stalker movement query; physical
acquisition is still required. perimeter-final-focused.json passes 38 checks and
perimeter-typecheck.json passes on the same production runtime. Integration owns
combined verification; no full suite was run here.

Broad moving-center and moving-surface policies lose useful H/LB near-side work.
They are rejected and archived. Continuity and composed arms retain a named H
target across a fleeting private engagement, but still fail to restore his F01
load. They are diagnostic evidence, not additional production corrections.
