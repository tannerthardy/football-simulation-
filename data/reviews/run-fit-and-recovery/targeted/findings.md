## Packet and limits

`targeted-comparison.json` retains all24 paired outcomes, first fit decisions with the consumed possession cue, first OL requests and early stance, first contact/wrap/tackle events, stats and every stuff transition. `targeted-{baseline,final}.json.gz` preserve complete endpoint signatures and initial profiles. `targeted-{baseline,final}-film.json.gz` preserve full native frames and telemetry for all48 clips. Raw tick traces preserve actual World movement/contact and every forwarded perception request; saved-only delta compression may package these without changing the expanded JSON. `targeted-receipt.json` pins the exact source/tools/cohort/output files.

The cohort is intentionally small. The fixed ordinary162-run and96-pass guards cover broader transfer separately. Changed gains cannot be assigned uniquely to one repaired component in this combined source, and none of these outcomes justify fitting force or an outcome bonus. Facing is body orientation, not eyes; the possession cue remains a delayed historical held-carrier channel without line-of-sight occlusion. Treat the improvement as a bounded cue/technique consistency repair, with adverse Draw execution and full-game tails still open.
