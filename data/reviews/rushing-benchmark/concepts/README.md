## Labels, operators and missing data

`MAN` is not established Duo. `OUTSIDE ZONE` does not distinguish our Wide Zone/Toss installations. Exact compound secondary annotations are disjoint raw values; the individual `SPLIT`, `PITCH`, `LEAD`, `READ OPTION` margins overlap. They cannot be summed as mutually exclusive concepts. A split label alone says nothing about the slice player's motion, kick/log read or block quality.

Main `yardsGained` and `prePenaltyYardsGained` are kept independently in every cell; missing penalty yards do not imply no penalty. Provider positive EPA is separate from yard-based success. The yard-conversion operator does not promise possession after a lost fumble. Recorded first-down/TD fields are not present and are not guessed from descriptions.

A short touchdown has limited room for a 20-yard gain. `fieldEligibleExplosive*plus` excludes only starts lacking enough known field, retaining touchdowns and reporting its own denominator. Conditional tails remain selected scheme/team/situation observations.

Motion flags come from exact offense-player rows. An observed TRUE is positive. A negative label requires all 11 offense flags known FALSE; otherwise it is unknown, never no motion. Position-count composition is a roster-label proxy, not a charted personnel package. No box count or named front exists here. Coverage labels are context, not an independent run-fit description.

Every cell exposes distinct games, teams and known carriers plus the largest game/team contribution. The central 95% game-block intervals use 1,500 shared deterministic draws, supported only for at least 100 plays and 20 games. They retain both sides of a game together but cannot cover repeated-player/team dependence between games, unobserved seasons or label ambiguity. They are diagnostic intervals rather than a synthetic-roster acceptance band.

## How to use it in the engine

1. Compare an RB/FB non-sneak cohort to RB/FB references with similar down, field and score/clock composition; keep QB/sneak roles separate. Preserve concept, formation, generated roster and defensive call frequencies before comparing means.
2. Inspect the full distribution: median, stuffs, success and tails together. A low median plus excessive 20+/30+ frequency suggests specific breakaway failures; a correct mean can hide both too many stuffs and too many explosives. Show top-five-percent yard contribution as an additional diagnostic.
3. Treat Inside Zone and Outside Zone labels as broad observational guards. Verify visible aiming point, front assignment, contact, combo transfer and support/pursuit cues before blaming a difference on physics. Keep Wide Zone/Toss and Duo as simulator-specific groups until a defensible external label mapping exists.
4. Separate physics stress tests from outcome benchmarks: mirrored support/load/momentum and repeatable sustained-contact behavior validate physical coherence. Scheme-specific outcomes require controller/assignment/vision evidence; never repair an apparent concept advantage with a hidden force multiplier.
5. Use the detailed primary-plus-situation cells to choose film witnesses and uncertainty checks, not to force every cell to a precise target. Larger whole-season PBP references own multi-season context, while this packet owns exact provider scheme/modifier labels.

## Mechanism hypotheses to test, not conclusions from these yards

- Zone: first-step angle and leverage, whether a covered partner can retain the down defender before the uncovered blocker climbs, RB press/read/cut timing, and backside fit pursuit. The paired zone rates can motivate these tests but do not identify which one caused an outcome.
- Power/Counter/Pull Lead: down-block feasibility, puller interception speed/base, kick versus log read, wrap arrival and penetration timing. Diagnose whether higher tails came from roster/call selection, free defenders, broken tackles or correct lanes before altering a physical capability.
- Trap/Draw: keep their smaller exposure visible; defensive pass/run recognition and willingness to rush upfield are likely controller levers. Do not infer a universal strength or explosive bonus from a high small-sample mean.
- Split/Read Option/Pitch: isolate modifier-positive film and preserve compound labels. Slice support and motion momentum, option cue ownership, edge conflict and runner timing are meaningful tests, while overlapping annotations cannot be treated as extra independent plays.
