What this version contains

Run stack through #52: observer-owned teammate information, attainable opening forecasts, stretch helper/moving-fit footwork, local DL block response, moving exchange and attached TE base. Minimal stalk #29, live-aim assessment #13, and Stick-only agreement are included. Accepted moving-catch behavior is retained.

Excluded: blocked coverage #34, rejected gather/departure trials, disabled attention pilot, seam/post, QB-pressure policy, experimental grip solvers, rating changes and new call-menu entries.

Interpretation limits

  • Exploratory four-pair checkpoint; no separate estimate of seed variation or league realism.
  • Matched whole-game seeds share initial teams/settings, but histories, fatigue and calls can diverge. This is not a same-state causal comparison.
  • Clean rates exclude every flagged foul, including declined ones; official scores and boxes retain normal rules.
  • Body-contact intervals/impulse describe physical contact, not automatically an awareness or blocking defect.
  • RB contact labels identify the RB-position player; they do not establish that he carried the ball at that instant. The OL/TE/FB breakdown excludes QB mesh contact.
  • Standard film has jobs, requests and engagement links; it does not contain full perception or exact contact-force traces.
  • Historical NFL context is a separate 2022–23 clean sample, not matched talent/scheme/game-state calibration.

Body-facing ticks show torso direction, not measured gaze. Interpolated film uses saved native positions and events. Select a player in the full film room for recorded assignments, requests and engagement links.
