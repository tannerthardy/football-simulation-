## What the retained mechanical evidence can establish

The loaded-helper screen requires an actual helper role, a live acquired hand
joint, positive evaluated helper support, nonzero achieved hand force and
measured arm impulse. Mere hand presence, `push=1`, proximity or a climb label
does not satisfy it. Records are sampled lower bounds from existing
first-contact and combination-read logs, not whole-play contact frequencies.
Pushing, steering and holding can all create nonzero force; delivered load
alone does not certify the coach's desired lane.

Power/Over/left/S3 retains a useful first request witness. At .70 s LG helps
LT on DT_S, with actual hand normal/tangent force zero in both sources.
The baseline requests `(+.132,+.357)` and final requests
`(−1.646,+1.869)` yd/s to recover its working surface. The first retained
native position difference is only .0034 yd. A later final sampled LG/DT_S
hand load at 2.35 s has positive helper support and actual bounded normal/
tangent forces about `62.05/−511.50`, versus the parent's earliest retained
positive sample at 3.90. The 2.35 record is triggered by a collision with
owner LT, **not** a new DT body strike. This is a candidate for a complete
native contact/support trace, rather than proof that useful hands first
acquired at those sampled timestamps. Gain changes .68→3.07; no automatic
climb is added.
