## Recommendation

strength. A live helper hand relationship currently invokes the same driving
request even when that helper is on a surface from which its hands cannot
contribute useful directional force. The clearest bounded next experiment is
recovering a working foot position while keeping surviving hands, then driving
from achieved geometry. It is not mandatory release or a new contact bonus.

## Coaching basis and limits

`docs/football-handbook/trenches.md` mechanisms T1/T4/T5 distinguish supported
arrival, Draw presentation, attainable helper surfaces and physical transfers.
Janak's public Duo installation describes inside/up helper movement and
restoring the base; Penree distinguishes regaining the base against an even
defender from running with a widening one. Grabowski's brief-post Inside Zone
instruction warns against burying the helper's hips and delaying his backer
response. These justify conditional movement from achieved geometry, not a
universal clinic sequence, stronger bodies or an automatic climb timer.

The next acceptance is therefore mechanical: the same bounded capabilities
recover the working surface under attainable ordinary/adverse conditions,
preserve actual hands and surviving keeper load, and converge under unchanged
resolution. Native gains and full-game tails stay descriptive guardrails.

No additional positively demonstrated invalid OL request is promoted by this audit.
Healthy-target abandonment, premature combination release and a blanket DL
slowdown remain hypotheses for other exact native witnesses. Existing good
contact and blocked-carrier paths can legitimately produce early tackles.
