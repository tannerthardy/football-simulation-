## Validation and evidence limits

Every baseline/promoted pair has identical saved initial physical profiles, complete bodies and snap minds/cadence. Initial hashes recompute exactly from the saved JSON. Every source archive and collector fingerprint is pinned. Observational native execution matches an unrecorded control at each tick, including all bodies, submitted/executed intents, retained mind Maps, perception, joints, reports and RNG. Final result and copied next-RNG signatures also match.

The collector wraps each existing `World.pairImpulse` once, records requestedx/y multiplied by its returned achieved work fraction, and sums signed actual hand-channel impulses by physical pair. It neither calls extra controllers nor inserts physical actions. Scalar mean normal/tangent force and exact vector impulses are retained separately. The geometry dot is descriptive; it is not used to infer zero directional load.
