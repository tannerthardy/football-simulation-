Run baseline diagnostics in a separate checkout at `16e87d8`, copying the same
current diagnostic scripts into it. Keep source and diagnostic files frozen
through measured runs. Re-exporting paired film requires both sources' raw
`*-film.json` files, generated by the run panel. The committed offline HTML
already embeds their frames and full inspection state.

Observers check body/intent/result/RNG equivalence in seven recorded run inputs
and six mechanical fixtures. Zero-capacity contacts and mirror pairs are exact.
Newer unheld command effort is inferred from actual allocated speed and the
nominal recovery ramp; older missing allocation is labelled unknown.

## Cost and comparison limits

The dedicated cost reports are benchmark-only processes, with identical
22-body inputs, construction excluded, explicit GC after fixture allocation,
three excluded warm blocks and seven blocks of 400 ticks. Both sources were
measured twice. Serialized command metadata omits the `--expose-gc` startup
flag; `allocationGcBeforeBenchmark=true` records its use.

Ordinary fixture timings vary substantially between separate runs. Multi-grip
CPU medians span approximately 302–307 ms baseline and 290–326 ms candidate.
Do not infer a precise speedup or overhead from these measurements. All raw
blocks remain in the reports; [cost-comparison.json](cost-comparison.json)
retains the repeat spread. The previous increment's stress cost remains a
separate historical issue, not a proved saving here. Do not substitute the
embedded, differently game-warmed benchmarks for this isolated comparison.

Run-panel gain-after-contact includes official forward progress; it is not
the NFL reference's selected contact-to-tackle ball displacement. The repaired
anatomy tool uses carrier positions, rejects missed reaches/ambiguous possession
chains, and separates physical endpoints from official gain. A QB's earlier
canonical contact can suppress a later carrier segment's contact record, so
those samples are excluded rather than inferred. The controlled run panel has
all 144 contacts after transfer. No NFL grip strength or recovery stiffness is
claimed; full 3D support remains paused.
