## Remaining football interpretation

Both changes are coherent executions of the new attention rule, with intact
physical eligibility and real movement/contact response. They are not proof
that these choices are optimal. Split Bear exposes an especially useful future
review question: a .05-second teammate picture versus .25-second opponent
picture can make a mechanically adequate C keeper look marginal to LG.
Investigate exact observed ages/geometry and coached inference if that becomes
systematically overcautious; do not widen the bound to recover these yards.

The adverse evidence supports retaining genuinely independent readers and
reviewing football technique/communication later. No source, tests, physics
parameters, native inputs or published balanced evidence changed for this audit.
