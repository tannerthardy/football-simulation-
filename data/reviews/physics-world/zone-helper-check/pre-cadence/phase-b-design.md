# Phase B: optional near-level check before climbing

## Installation and responsibility

Only actual insideZone/splitZone combinations install
`helpPolicy: {kind:'check',homeY,side}`. `homeY` is the original uncovered
helper's y at installation; `side` is the sign from original owner to helper.
Absence is the existing sustained-double policy. Duo/gap/OZ/WZ/Draw preserve
their original installation and reader. No physical capacity changes.

Only original members[1] may use the unjoined path. Original members[0] must
actually own the original DL and retain this exact combination plan. Owner
updates return before advancing shared nextRead whenever this check member
does not share the DL engagement. This leaves the helper's own finite cadence
usable without introducing a second cadence. `checkSince` belongs only to
that helper; owner secureSince cannot satisfy it. A real joined double resets
checkSince and uses the existing sustained release reader.

## Physical admissibility (not knowledge attributed to the helper)

The owner must have genuine World hands linking the correct bodies, its own
support availability >.60, be upright, carry a run engagement and have no
outside helper. The original helper must be upright, unengaged and free of
other World joints. Both still hold this exact installed job.

Existing secure-control/urgency, baseB>.60 and |phi|<.55 checks remain. The
candidate keeper uses the existing |phi|<.65, live own hands, actual support
>.60 and ordinary helper-contact eligibility. A handsless helper cannot
become keeper. No velocities, bodies, forces or hand ownership are fabricated.

## Helper's consumed cues

The helper reads the owner's delayed pose/location and the DL's delayed
pose/location/movement. Both must appear up. Observed owner-vs-DL geometry
uses the installed drive frame and the existing .55 quarter-turn leverage
limit. Their observed center distance cannot exceed body radii plus the
owner's measured full arm. This is a coarse visible contact envelope, not
the shorter fresh-strike range demanded for reacquisition.

The DL must be on the owner's far lateral half relative to the installed
helper side: `side*(DL.y-owner.y)<0`. Zero is the exact center plane, not a
new fitted margin. Lateral velocity alone does not control the decision:
the native Split/Tite/right witness has a slightly returning lateral DL
velocity while that far surface remains clear and truly carried.

Local replacing work is read through helper sim.see. Protect BOTH original
homeY and current helper y with the existing .9yd collection-lane width.
The existing first-level boundary is LOS+2; bodies more than1yd behind the
helper are outside this forward collection. Original DL and designated
backer are already this pair's responsibilities. A real adjacent live hand
pair can account for an upfield body; an observed body past LOS-.25 still
blocks departure even with that pair. Relationship labels without genuine
hands do not count. Actual current contact is an admissibility check, not
perfect visual location or a statement that the helper knows exact forces.

Only after a supported, visually clear check persists for the existing .15s
sampled stability dwell does the helper consume its designated backer/RB cue.
The existing backer-distance<4.2yd or observed runner x>LOS-2.8 cue remains;
there is no scripted handoff/mesh clock.

The observed DL must also clear the body-radius corridor from helper to
observed backer now and at the helper's next decision. The projection uses
observed DL and backer velocities over decisionInterval; it is a short visual
flow estimate, not a physical reachability certificate or a new timer. World
and the existing second-level approach still determine actual execution.

## Transition and observability

Reuse the existing keeper transfer/solo restoration and vertical climb
approach. `block_climb` detail begins `check`; helper receives ordinary
combo-climb plus combo-check-climb. Preserve the owner's actual hand object,
acquired surface, control, start, penalties and physical load budgets.

The existing collector sees scheduled check reads through combo.nextRead and
records actual keeper state, consumed helper pictures, changed targets and
subsequent World loads. It does not serialize helpPolicy/checkSince directly;
tests establish policy/private-dwell controls. Event count is permitted
departure, not successful second-level engagement or improved rushing.

## Required controls

- Native Split/Tite/right/S3 and physical mirror; observe earlier helper
  opportunity, same owner hands and later achieved movement/contact.
- Prime a real eligible helper read and then wait to its next scheduled read
  before adding unsupported/missing owner contact, stale down owner/DL,
  closing near hip, replacing penetration or blocked backer corridor. This
  avoids false negative tests that merely hit the initial .15s dwell.
- Distant LB plus far-back RB; hidden current fresh cues cannot override old
  helper images. Owner secureSince alone cannot bypass helper stability.
- Foreign helper contact and original owner departure to a handsless helper.
- Real joined doubles and all non-check concept installations stay controls.
- Same actual bodies/capacities with stronger recognition can read sooner;
  no promised yards, perfect assignments or strength bonus.

Remaining independent member attention for actual doubles stays a separate
task. This path does not generally fix shared observer order or infer a new
call-familiarity mechanic.
