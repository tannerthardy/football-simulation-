A shared post/check/leave rule should ask what support must remain, which real blocker is in position to supply it, what this player's delayed picture says about the filling backer, and whether leaving is physically executable. It should not require an already unhelpful helper to establish a useful push before it can go somewhere useful. It should preserve uncertainty from finite observation cadence, so good recognition/training means more timely information and execution, not clairvoyant assignments.
