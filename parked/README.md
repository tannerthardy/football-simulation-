# Parked experiments (patches)

Three experiments that existed only as local commits, saved here so they are
not lost. Each patch records its base commit (`base-commit:` at the end);
apply it there with `git am`, or reapply its hunks onto a current branch.
The ideas are catalogued, with the other parked flags, in `docs/PARKED.md`.

| Patch | Flag | Base | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `force-lead.patch` | `FORCE_LEAD` | `2c98bed9` | The force defender's fit leverages the lateral track he has acquired. |
| `force-flow.patch` | `FORCE_FLOW` | `0b2addbe` | The force boundary follows the pre-possession flow outward. |
| `lead-in.patch` | `LEAD_IN` | `1903df6a` | The power/counter lead blocker wraps on the kick-out he sees. |
