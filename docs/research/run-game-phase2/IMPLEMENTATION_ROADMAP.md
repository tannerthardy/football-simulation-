**Recommended intervention.** Pass actual traction×availability into yaw allocation. After existing gait/lean allocation, enforce the bound on the actual attained net vector and scale its component/action accounting consistently. The1e−9 rounding tolerance is a numerical guard, not a football parameter. Keep force–velocity calibration, contact geometry and combo policies out of this unit.

**Competing approaches.** Yaw-only repair fails13/44 retained inputs because the second defect remains. Merely adding substeps fails. A new FV curve does not repair zero-budget residual force. A more elaborate allocator is unnecessary unless the minimal projection fails an accounting/control requirement.

**Dependencies and preserved behavior.** Same public capacities, normal-load model, existing lean release, legal body/hand contacts and support model. Preserve free movement where contact has not changed the normal-load allowance. Retain strong-blocker wins and strong-defender wins over weak doubles. Consult the shared owner before touching allocation signatures.

**Adverse controls and interpretation.** P019 accepted retains both loaded edges through throw; repair loses LT before3.05. Do not delete/reselect the fixture or restore contact by awarding a force bonus. Determine whether the changed geometric shed is a defect in the repair or exposure of another mechanism. P002's missing4-second pursuit opportunity is mediated by launch/RNG phase; do not count its setup-censored six assertions as passes, nor call it a pursuit-law regression. ## 3. Run/shared information: separate defender arrival from designed-track exit

**Problem and football mechanism.** A nearby acquired teammate currently adds an unsupported .25/.7-second retention duration. The modified utility score also controls whether the carrier exits its designed track. Removing false protection can therefore keep the runner on a blocked designed route, even when the selected open heading is unchanged. The native S12-P011 bidirectional intervention isolates this coupling exactly. S11-P029 prevents a universal early-exit answer.

**Owner and evidence.** Existing run/G2 carrier owner and shared G10 information owner; attach to the ongoing acquired-information work rather than reopening merged #292 or creating a duplicate private-win fix. Current pin,208 development/304 heldout evaluator packets,24 development and24 fresh heldout constructors, three nominated transition boundaries and six native interventions are preserved in `information/`.

**Recommended intervention.** Keep acquired defender-arrival evidence and the decision to abandon the designed route as separate explicit contracts. Use route blockage, feasible acquired alternatives and commitment costs to justify a mode change; do not inherit an arbitrary utility scale from a protection bonus. Model A's narrow removal is a useful starting direction, not a finished combined patch. Preserve observed slowing, body obstruction, recovery cues and real contact evidence. Any future protection forecast must earn its extra delay through observable constraints.

**Competing approaches.** A removes the allowance; C's geometric body-detour changes no development continuation beyond A; B's history inference does not rescue either adverse Duo, and the continuity extension does not rescue the one Duo it tested, S12-P011. Retire those implementations as fixes. Compare any new transition rule against a no-bonus baseline and a variant changing only the transition contract. Do not use the diagnostic event times as inputs, force all early exits, or tune scores to recovered yards. A responsibility-aware model is conditional later work, not an untested winner.

**Dependencies and preserved behavior.** Full acquired packets and ordinary evaluation cadence, stable player/body profiles, exact constructor pairing. Existing `live-gaps.ts` private-membership sensitivity remains a separate dependency for whole-lane claims: current evaluator invariance does not certify the entire runner path. Coordinate a narrow acquired-surface treatment with its existing owner; do not silently mix it into the transition experiment.

**Required scenarios.** Constructed incidental teammate beyond defender, wrong-side teammate, real interposition, wash-away, drive-back, scrape/escape, stale/down/unknown blocker and mirrored cases. Native S12-P011 must distinguish actual Y restraint from decisive weak DT_W credit. Preserve S11-P029 as a required adverse and S11-P022 Counter as a competing route. Retain heldout S14-P054 Counter, S14-P023 Trap and S14-P043 Draw. Test mode choice separately from heading and attained running speed.

**Independent review.** Hold acquired pictures fixed while varying true opponent positions, private win and membership; require invariance at the evaluator boundary. Verify actual command ownership and first divergence, not only final yards. A separate reviewer must challenge whether a mode exit is justified by what that runner could see, and whether a valid commitment is abandoned too cheaply.

**Completion.** A coherent acquired-only transition explanation, preserved valid commitment cases, no invented future restraint, no hidden-state response, and understood adverse controls. Freeze before a fresh roster/seed/front/concept panel; the existing heldout set is now an adverse development asset and must not be called fresh again. **Stop/revert.** A candidate that wins only by an arbitrary hold-duration/score offset, always exits early, needs true future motion, or fixes two witnesses while leaving the same unexplained mechanism in S11 must stop. After two failed variants of the same rationale, return to the first divergence rather than continuing a threshold sweep.
