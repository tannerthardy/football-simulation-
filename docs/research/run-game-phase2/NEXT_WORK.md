2. **Mechanism gate:** a frozen physical or decision island distinguishes the proposed cause from at least one serious alternative. Native continuation then verifies its relevance. Neither island nor native outcome alone substitutes for the other.
3. **Adverse gate:** retain P019, S11-P029, legitimate defender wins, valid runner commitments and false-protection negatives. A changed result is traced to its first divergence and mechanism rather than automatically restored.
