# B — What a successful block means

The unit is a **responsibility interval**, attached to identified players, a current task, and a live football window. An initial job is the sampling unit for the original census; it is not an immutable obligation. A contact, a loaded hand joint, an arrival, and a contribution are different observations. This framework supports uncertainty explicitly. It does not manufacture a complete success label from incomplete evidence.

## Six separate questions

| Dimension | Operational evidence | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Responsibility | Installed assignment, combination members/down man, acquired reread, native release/retarget events, updated plan | Coaching intent in NFL tracking; correctness of a reassignment |
| Opportunity | A relevant defender, an actionable time window, an observable route, feasible movement/contact, and a viable remaining first-level obligation | Free-space distance alone; permission to abandon a dangerous down man |
| Contact | Physical torso contact and hand acquisition, identity, time, direction and load | Leverage, sustained restriction or protection |
| Control | Defender motion/access constrained by body placement, traction and contact; opposing-force and retention evidence | A future hold duration from present proximity or present joint membership |
| Timely access restriction | Defender's access angle, path or arrival is changed during the actual carrier's relevant live window | That any late load helped; that first contact always ends usefulness |
| Contribution | A usable corridor is preserved/created and the carrier has an opportunity to use it, with first-level costs and other defenders included | Final yards as a block grade; exclusive credit for a multi-player outcome |

Use `observed`, `supported conditional inference`, `constructively demonstrated`, `contradicted`, and `unknown` at each dimension. Missing observations are not zeroes. Correlation of load and a successful run does not move a case up this ladder.

## A testable useful-opportunity claim

For a nominated historical job, specify the current responsibility at decision time, a live corridor, the target threat and its deadline, what the blocker already saw, and the allowed intervention. A constructive missed-opportunity witness must demonstrate all of the following:

1. The target could threaten relevant access during a live window. A recorded tackle interaction is strong retrospective relevance evidence, but is not the only possible threat and does not prove the blocker knew it.
2. A legal controller can reach and acquire usable leverage with the same ordinary body, ratings and information. A realistic better-player profile is an alternative explicitly specified intervention; unrestricted acceleration or hindsight pursuit is not.
3. The remaining blocker can meet the first-level obligation without a newly exposed earlier threat. A hand joint merely staying loaded is a check, not sufficient proof of secure leverage.
4. The intervention restricts the target's timely access, beyond merely touching him. Controls must separate block effect from a different runner route or pursuit task.
5. The improvement survives a legitimate defender response and opposing scenes. It must not borrow hidden velocity, another player's assignment, an outcome-selected target or a frozen defender policy without labeling that limitation.

A native closed-loop replay estimates the total effect of that controller in that exact input. It does not isolate block contribution after every other actor changes. A frozen-command World island isolates a physical mechanism, at the price of removing adaptation. Use both, and name the intervention each supports. Neither final rushing yards nor a universal contact threshold is the estimand.

The natural counterfactual is a trajectory/access difference under an identified intervention. Define the target's relevant envelope from body reach and carrier geometry, an observation horizon, and how absent arrival is censored. Report the complete time series and sensitivity to the envelope. If the treated defender reacts to a different runner, comparing that defender to the original runner path is a **hybrid geometric reference**, not a controlled causal access effect. That limitation disqualifies a tempting claim about S7-P025 in this study.

## Timing, censoring and thresholds

Native decisions/events use update-start time; end snapshots use the following endpoint. Thus S9's1.25 event appears in the1.30 endpoint plan. Never silently subtract these clocks or claim a50 ms response discrepancy. Actual carrier identity is taken from possession, not the scheduled RB. First carrier contact is an event timestamp, possibly shared by multiple defenders; it is not a unique first tackler or the universal end of useful blocking.

The factual census uses resultant member-hand load >1 kg·yd/s² at20 Hz endpoints. It measures load presence, not a force threshold for success. Contact can begin within a physics step; endpoint classification does not prove substep ordering against an event with the same timestamp. The scalar threat screen uses a1.5-second hindsight corridor and simplified free locomotion. Its29 nominations are not29 real opportunities. The reach islands use original body/profile/support but remove traffic and sustained contact, use future target positions, and start when an alternate member has any nonzero down-man hand load. That permissive load criterion is weaker than the census's >1 threshold. Their positive outcomes are not legal strike or secure-keeper certificates.

The study does not fit a useful-control duration to a desired block-success percentage. Threshold sensitivity is meaningful only after the metric's causal meaning is correct. Forty fixed constructors and their repeated variants are not independent draws from NFL football; no confidence interval or league prevalence claim is justified here.

## What is validated, and what remains unvalidated

The native responsibility/contact/load observations reproduce independently, and passive observation preserves exact reachable state and results. Physical positive/negative islands reject load-equals-protection. Counterfactual transition interventions distinguish information from downstream mode choice. These are validated components.

A fully validated scalar block-success classifier and a complete useful-missed-opportunity count are **not** delivered. The package instead provides every original job with auditable facts and explicit unresolved judgments. A convincing count requires constructive, duty-aware, legal-contact witnesses and control of the runner/pursuit task. Replacing those requirements with an empty-space travel estimate would yield53 relaxed positives here and a misleading answer.
