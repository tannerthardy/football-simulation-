## Brief A: responsibility and contribution evidence

### Narrow implementation that is ready to start

Promote the smallest passive interface from Phase II `observer.mts` into the existing diagnostic tooling. Produce two linked views: an immutable initial-job census and time-indexed current responsibility. The latter must record actor, current target, plan kind, release/keeper identities, change reason, acquired-evidence references, raw and rewritten commands, final World intent, possession, physical contacts, hand-force vectors and relevant time intervals. Diagnostic reasons belong in the trace, not new football events or player logic.

Inspect every target mutation path. `block_threat` alone misses `availableTarget/findWork`, and a reread can change `climb` into `drive` or `reach`. Phase II's nine rows with later threat events and six rows with another target while remaining a climb have only three rows in common. Both selectors follow the departing/direct actor; neither counts every responsibility change. Preserve that distinction rather than adjusting counts until they agree.

Keep “contact,” “load,” “timely access restriction,” and “useful contribution” separate. Load presence uses the retained >1 native force-unit definition for continuity, with signed vectors available for interpretation. It is not a success threshold. Record native event time, load endpoint time and sampled positional crossing intervals separately. Preserve multiple defenders at a tied first-contact timestamp, actual QB keeps, unknowns and right-censoring. A current-duty recorder can ship without a validated scalar contribution classifier.

## Brief B: separate defender-arrival evidence from the runner's mode choice

### What is ready, and what is not

The ready implementation task is a diagnostic/refactoring boundary that exposes three separate products without changing behavior: acquired defender-arrival evidence, candidate-route assessment, and the designed-track stay/exit decision with its evidence and commitment cost. The current utility score serves all three purposes. An explicit boundary is useful only if its baseline implementation is exactly equivalent; do not slip a changed policy into a readability refactor.

Removing the automatic .25/.7-second proximity allowance is the simplest information-repair direction, but Model A is not ready unchanged. Model C's geometric detour changes no development continuation beyond A. Model B fails both adverse Duos; its continuity extension was tested only on S12-P011 and fails there. Neither forecast is an integration candidate. Valid additional restraint forecasting remains open; observed slowing, body obstruction and acquired recovery cues must remain represented.

Coordinate the separate `live-gaps.ts` private-membership seam with its existing owner. Same-picture evaluator invariance does not certify the whole route consumer. Treat a surface correction as a separately pinned factor; do not silently combine it with the first transition experiment or recreate a parallel private-win cleanup already merged in #292.

**B1 — Decompose the first changed decision, including the adverse Duo.** On the 24 development constructors and the former 24 heldout constructors, passively record acquired threat inputs, per-heading route evidence, selected heading, aim blockage, utility components, commitment state, exit-gate operands and final command ownership. **B2 — Compare two explicit exit rationales with the arrival model held fixed.** Keep A's no-extra-credit arrival model in every treatment. Retain the original gate as control. Predeclare at most two transition hypotheses: (i) a blocked designed continuation may yield to an acquired unblocked alternative that is reachable under current motion; (ii) the same alternative must additionally repay an explicit turning/braking commitment cost over the existing decision horizon. The first tests whether mere blockage/alternative evidence suffices; the second tests whether S11 exposes omitted commitment cost. Derive costs from current motion and existing public locomotor capacities, not a fitted protection duration, case ID, wall-clock timestamp or desired yardage. Document the precise equations and horizon before examining outputs.

Use frozen packets first, including same-heading/different-score cases, real open designed routes, incidental/wrong-side teammates, interposition, wash-away, driven-back, scrape/escape and stale/down/unknown pictures, with mirrors. Run private-win, true-position and membership shadows behind the same acquired packets. A transition must not change merely because an irrelevant future-restraint bonus shifts a utility scale. Reject an always-exit or always-stay rule. Reject a rule requiring an inaccessible exact contact duration. If neither hypothesis explains the positive and opposing decisions, stop and rediagnose; do not start a threshold sweep.

**B3 — Install the surviving rule and test mode, heading and movement separately.** Use a small factorial: accepted behavior; A alone; transition-only on the accepted arrival evaluator; and A plus the explicit transition rule.
