# OFFENSIVE FOOTBALL REFERENCE LEXICON

# PART VI — MASTER GLOSSARY OF MODERN OFFENSIVE FOOTBALL

This is a **broad working lexicon**, covering common NFL and major-college concepts and the variants most likely to appear in film study. It is intentionally more useful than a purported single 'official playbook dictionary': there is **no universal complete set** of formations, motions, tags and concept names. Team-specific calls are unlimited; terms below describe common interpretations, and any particular installation can differ. Where a label is not standardized, a clear functional definition is more important than the word itself.

| Term | Working definition / film-room use |
|:--|:--|

| **20 personnel** | Two backs, zero TEs, three WR. |
| **21 personnel** | Two backs, one TE, two WR. |
| **22 personnel** | Two backs, two TEs, one WR. |
| **23 personnel** | Two backs, three TEs, zero WR; heavy short-yardage configuration. |

| **H** | Often slot, wing, second TE or hybrid back; team-specific. |
| **U** | Common label for move TE or second TE in two-TE systems. |
| **X** | Often backside or line-of-scrimmage WR, but role depends on installation. |

| **Y** | Often primary TE or inside eligible receiver. |
| **FLEX** | A TE/RB aligned in receiving space or a movable hybrid receiver. |

| **Strength** | Declared run/pass/field side; multiple strength concepts may differ. |
| **Run strength** | Side with greater blocking surface or insert potential. |
| **Pass strength** | Side with greater receiver distribution or passing threats. |

| **Unbalanced line** | Offensive line distribution with unequal on-line players on either side, subject to eligibility rules. |
| **Tackle-over** | Additional tackle aligned to one side, creating an unbalanced surface. |
| **Extra offensive lineman / jumbo** | Package that includes sixth eligible/reporting OL or another heavy lineman; rules apply. |
| **Wildcat** | Direct snap to a nontraditional QB, often RB or specialty player. |
