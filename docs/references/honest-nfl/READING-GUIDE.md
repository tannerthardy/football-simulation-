Maestro → Mesh Rail with OTB, Falcon/Sparrow → Flanker Drive variants,

Oakland, Wasp or protection meaning into another sheet without checking it.

## Turn a teaching example into a coherent installation

For later design work, record the formation/personnel, protection, intended receiver distribution, acquired cues, allowed adjustments, defensive ownership and relevant movement/contact constraints. Compare related sheets and explicit alternatives before treating a phrase as a universal rule. Preserve uncertainty and execution limits; the library does not establish what every player knows or physically achieves.

Use the source image for exact wording. OCR and navigation labels can contain errors. Individual teaching claims and source attribution have not been independently authenticated by this import.
