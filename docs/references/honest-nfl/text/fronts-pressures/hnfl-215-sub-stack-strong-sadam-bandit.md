COACHING POINTS

Sack-It can be an empty check for an entire game, or it can be used as Can call.

When Sack-It is called, we will check Sack-It vs any aligned Empty or shift to Empty (not motion). Empty is defined as any formation where there is no one in the

backfield other than the QB. We will not check if there are C-Area players on both sides of the formation.

If they motion out of Empty back into 1-Back, we will keep sack it on. The Safety will pressure rush the RB.

Backers and Tackles will be in the A and B Gaps per front called or game planned. The A and B Gap rushers will take a Heels charge on the OL inside of you, if he

blocks down, keep rushing, if you get the face, then drop out.

Ends will align on the edge of the tackle — Both ends will rush and stay in the rush regardless of the blocking by the tackle. Expect the QB to get flushed out and be

ready to keep contain.

Safety — be ready to Frisco with the End vs a Y in the Core.
