IDENTIFYING FRONT STRUCTURE

We feel that Identifying Front Structure is the most important element of defensive

identification, as it allows us to use the context clues given, to then identify the rest of the

defensive structure. We break down front structures into six main categories. Even, Odd, Over,

Under, Jam, and Bear. These categories allow us to identify most fronts presented, outside of

the occasional unsound sub front presentation. Understanding the basic structure is principle in

our identification, so the strongest emphasis is placed on this in our teaching. Once basic

structures are mastered, then we can begin to dig into linebacker, and defensive back

techniques and alignments within the box.
