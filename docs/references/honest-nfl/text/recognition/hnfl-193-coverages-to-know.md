COVERAGES TO KNOW

To sufficiently attack any defense, we must have an understanding of what they’re

doing, how they’re doing it, and how we can exploit it when we see it. The specific

concept menu will be featured in the game plan meeting the night before each game.

.

4/44 (MOD) = Traditional Quarters coverage played with key & match principles.

They play Quarters with outstanding positioning and perhaps the most efficient

key-reading we've seen to date. There is very little waste in their match

coverages. They use their +1 advantage to each side to compress route

combinations and maintain advantageous depth & leverage at all costs.

Corners will typically be in Clue alignment unless they are on the backside of 3x1.

They are fairly passive and will zone it off if in doubt (at 5 yards exactly) using a

“China” call rather than latching on. If #1 goes shallow, they will make an “In”

call and zone their 1/4 of the field from top-down.

Quarter Flat defenders are aggressively re-routing #2 to disrupt our

timing/spacing and give the safety time to read the pattern. They are responsible

for the 1st flat threat or matching the final #2 in the distribution underneath.

They will play this from a Wall alignment if #2 is detached.

Their safeties play Inside Quarters technique with a normal 2-to-1 read from a

Quarters alignment (10-12y over #2), but don’t look to bracket #1 if there is no

seam threat — they will act more like Robber techniques.

Any close splits that present an exchange threat will automatically be zoned off

with a ZORRO adjustment.

Any formation with #1 & #3 in close proximity will be played with a BOX

adjustment to combat exchanges.

Any motion to 3x1 will be handled with a TRIX adjustment to relieve the vertical

stress of #3 on the Midpoint defender.

Rarely will we see MOD against this unit — they value the vision that playing off

allows in order to streamline their key reads and pattern recognition.

Vulnerable in all the ways that normal Quarters structures are.

Play pass is generally not effective because the LBs think pass and react to run.

They basically only call this in passing situations.

Flat Responsibilities: Apexes (Quarter Flat techniques), but Safeties are primary

force vs. the run.

Vertical Responsibilities: Corners have #1 to their side vs. any structure. Safeties

have #2 to their side. Midpoint (Quarter Hook) carries #3.

They will redistribute pre-snap vs. a Fast #3 only using a “Push” call.

.

4/44 TRIX = Traditional Quarters played to a Trips/Trey side with the Free Safety

keying #3-to-X. This allows the Mike to stay underneath while the Free Safety

overtakes anything up or over by #3.

The Nickel/Sam is re-routing #2 from a Wall alignment.

There will be snaps that look like ZORRO with the Strong Safety handling #3 up

the seam, but it looks as if they’re betting on us following sound spacing rules

with our verticals (which they‘d be right about). We will not send both #2 & #3

up the seam in a 3x formation.
