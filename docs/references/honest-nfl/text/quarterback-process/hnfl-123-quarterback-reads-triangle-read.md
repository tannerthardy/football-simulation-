TRIANGLE READS ARE POST-SNAP READS DESIGNED TO UTILIZE THE ROUTE DISTRIBUTION TO STREAMLINE

THE WAY WE READ THROUGH OUR PROGRESSION AND ALSO HOW WE EXPLOIT ZONE DEFENDERS IN THE

UNDERCOVERAGE.

RATHER THAN JUST USING THREE ROUTES LIKE SPACING AND STRETCH DEFENDERS PURELY ONA

HORIZONTAL AXIS, TRIANGULAR STRETCHES ALLOW US TO PUT ROUTES ON MULTIPLE LEVELS AND UTILIZE

BOTH VERTICAL & HORIZONTAL STRETCH PRINCIPLES.

THERE ARE TWO TYPES OF TRIANGLES: MIDDLE TRIANGLES AND APEX TRIANGLES.

MIDDLE TRIANGLES ARE BUILT OVER THE BALL IN ORDER TO STRETCH HOOK DEFENDERS.

APEX TRIANGLES ARE BUILT WITH AN APEX & CORNER INCLUDED AND THEY STRETCH THE OUTSIDE PORTIONS

OF THE FIELD.

WE WILL ALWAYS READ TRIANGLES FROM TOP-DOWN, INSIDE-OUT (RELATIVE TO FRONTSIDE). THEY MUST

SUFFICIENTLY TAKE AWAY A ROUTE BEFORE WE PROGRESS TO THE NEXT.
