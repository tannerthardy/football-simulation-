CONCEPT FAMILIES & READS (QUARTERBACKS)

Every pass in our system belongs to a specific family of concept and is executed with a specific type of read for the

Quarterback. They attack different parts of the field and aspects of the defense. Concepts in RED denotes a

post-snap process read for the Quarterback. BLUE denotes a process based on pre-snap reads. “BLS” = Best Look

Side concept with the Quarterback picking a side based on defensive presentation.

PURE PROGRESSIONS — The most basic form of a read with the QB working smoothly

through the progression looking for the first open window every time.

PROGRESSIONS + ALERTS — A read with a foundational progression that only

changes if a specific look is presented that can be exploited using the route we have

alerted.

ZONE ATTACKERS— Post-snap movement key read used when we want to runa

“2-on-1” high/low or east/west concept on any underneath zone defender to stretch

them vertically or horizontally. Can be applied to area in the undercoverage in a L-V-B

structure. (ex. “Falcon” - “Spacing” - “Hawk”)

SQUEEZE READS (BLS) — Post-snap movement key read used in Best Look Side

designs where the “squeeze” (horizontal commitment to frontside or backside)

dictates the primary side the QB will work. We always work away from the squeeze.

TRIANGLE READS — A progression consisting of three routes that form a triangle to

stress zone coverage defenders both vertically & horizontally. (ex. “Maestro” -

“Piano” - “Falcon” - “Shark” - “Stallion”)

VOID ATTACKERS — A concept designed to create voids within the coverage structure

using a route as another one works to exploit the void. Used to manipulate zone

defenders working within various types of coverage mechanisms. Mostly utilized (not

exclusively) against outside-leveraged Man-Free, Halves, Tampa, and Quarters.

CLOUD ATTACKERS — A concept designed to attack the technique of the Corner and

Safety who make up a Cloud coverage. The Cloud Corner is a primary force and flat

defender we look to stretch vertically while the Cloud Safety rolls over top to handle

verticals by reading outside-in.

THREE-LEVEL VERTICAL STRETCHES — A concept designed to attack coverages by

flooding it on three levels in order to stretch the deep & flat defenders on a vertical

axis. We typically teach these as flat defender key reads with the ability to alert the

clearout element if we deem there to be some sort of advantage gained by the time

the QB teaches the top of the drop. Otherwise, we key the flat defender’s depth and

work off that. If he sinks to take away the high element, check it down to the low

element. If he squats on the low element, rip the high element once you come to

balance. (ex. “Sail” - “Detroit” - “Flutie”)

DEEP HORIZONTAL STRETCHES — A concept designed to attack the deep defenders by

stretching them on a horizontal axis and keying on their “squeeze” while reading it

inside-out. This is most commonly used in the context of our “All Go” family of

vertical routes with “2-on-1” reads when keying the deep defenders.

RUN/PASS OPTIONS — An “advantage” read based on key reads that are executed

post-snap. We incorporate a wide variety of initial reads that determine whether the

QB hands the ball off or pulls it to throw, then add in a second key read to determine

which route the ball will be thrown to.
