“RAIDER” FAMILY (VERTICALS) — Package of vertical-based plays built using our

“PIRATE” FAMILY — Package of plays built using our “PIRATE” & “BUCCANEER”

concepts and their variants. Used primarily as underneath zone attackers, but has

answers for M/M and Key & Match coverages in the form of sharp, sudden route

adjustments. Various designs provide the QB with a few ways to attack, typically as

Pure Progressions, Progressions + Alerts, structure reads, alignment reads, or Mike

Squeeze reads.

“IN” FAMILY — Package of plays built using our “GUPPIE” & “OCEAN” concepts and

their variants. Used primarily as underneath zone attackers with inside-out stretches

and triangle reads. (Fish = Hooks/Curls with a Shallow element)

“DBL” FAMILY — Package of plays built using mirrored routes outside in conjunction

with either a Middle Read or Thru route inside. Used primarily as “Best Look Side”

reads based on matchup, cushion, leverage, rotation, coverage count, or field space.

Can be read top-down, inside-out, bottom-up, based on structure or alignment.

“BAY AREA” FAMILY — Package of plays built using our “49er” & “FRISCO” concepts

and their variants. The overwhelming majority of these designs are from 3x1/Bunch

formation structures that allow for a frontside combo that attacks various coverages

and a backside combo competent enough to complement the front in a half-field read

scenario. Used mostly in the form of Progression + Alert reads, structure reads, or

alignment reads.

“WEATHER” FAMILY (NEGATIVES) — Package of plays built using our “ALL THUNDER”

& “ALL LIGHTNING” concept variants that operate on well-timed rhythm throws to

“negative break” routes with double moves mixed in.

“PROJECTILE” FAMILY — Package of plays built using our “MISSILE” & “TORPEDO”

concepts as the foundation. These concepts are typically Pure Progression reads with

the occasional alert, but we utilize them most as “Kill” components to each other.

“HORSE” FAMILY — Package of plays built using our “COLT” & “STALLION” concepts

as the foundation. These concepts are built to stretch underneath zone defenders by

creating a Triangle Stretch.
