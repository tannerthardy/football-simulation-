```text
ADVANTAGES — A pre-snap read based on the number of box defenders, the coverage

count to a side, or anything that we deem to be too advantageous to pass up. Similar

to Run/Pass Option, but executed purely on pre-snap assessment.

MAN/ZONE READS — A pre-snap half-field man/zone alignment read designed to

streamline the post-snap process by utilizing pre-snap context clues to make sure the

QB's eyes and feet get to the right spot as soon as possible. In theory, one side of the

design is a concept to defeat man coverage and the other side has a concept to stress

zone coverage.

SINGLE/SPLIT SAFETY READS — A pre-snap Single Safety/Split Safety structure read

designed to streamline the post-snap process by utilizing pre-snap context clues to

make sure the QB’s eyes and feet get to the right spot as soon as possible. In theory,

one side of the design is a concept to defeat Single Safety zone coverage structures

and the other side has a concept to stress Split Safety zone coverage structures.

(MFO/MFC READS = Post-snap version of a coverage structure read)

MIDDLE OF FIELD OPEN/CLOSED READS — A post-snap Middle Open/Closed structure

read designed in a similar manner to Single/Split Safety reads, but we allow for

post-snap safety rotation to take place so that we can exploit any advantages in our

final assessment numbers, space, and/or leverage using our 2nd Wide & Narrow

visions.

LEVERAGE READS (BLS) — A type of read we use in our “Best Look Side” concepts

that are based strictly on pre-snap presentation. Here, we are simply looking for the

most favorable leverage positioning to throw the corresponding route (ex. 200 Jet

Omaha, we want to go to the slot receiver with the outside leverage on the Apex if it’s

presented).

“FLORIDA” FAMILY — Package of GVA plays built using our “TAMPA” (Snag) concept

and its variants. Used to design a variety of reads for the QB that includes Pure

Progressions, structure reads (Half-Field Single/Split Safety, Open/Closed Middle),

alignment reads (Half-Field Man/Zone), coverage count reads, leverage reads,

matchup reads, and Mike Squeeze reads.

“DAGGER” FAMILY — Package of plays built using deep in-breakers designed to attack

a void or stretch a zone defender. Primarily built on “DAGGER” and “HAMMER”

concepts to attack MFO zone coverages.

“MUSIC” FAMILY (MESHES) — Package of GVA plays built using our “MAESTRO”

(Mesh—Rail—OTB) & “PIANO” (Escapes—Rail—OTB) concepts and their variants. Used

primarily as M/M beaters with zone answers via triangle reads to stretch defenders.

“MARINE” FAMILY (SHALLOW CROSSES) — Package of GVA plays built using our

“SHARK” (Shallow Cross) & “SHRIMP” (Shallow Cross Swing) concepts and their

variants. Used primarily as M/M beaters with zone answers coming in the form of

triangle reads.

“SNAKE” FAMILY — Package of plays designed to attack deep areas or defenders that

are built using our “RATTLER” & “SNAKE” concepts and their variants. Used primarily

as Void Attackers against Quarters, Deep Horizontal Stretches against Cover 2, or

3-Level Vertical Stretches.

“BIRD” FAMILY (DRIVES) — Package of GVA plays built using our “FALCON” (Flanker

Drive) & “HAWK” (Bingo Shallow) concepts and their variants. Used primarily as M/M
```
