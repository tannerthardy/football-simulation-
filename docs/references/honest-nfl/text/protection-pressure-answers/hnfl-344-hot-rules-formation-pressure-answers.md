```text
“HOT RULES” = A mechanism built into every formation structure in our system that we use to

be able to exploit pressure concepts with 5+ rushers. “Hot” describes any play in which the

defense brings one or more LBs/DBs that are unaccounted for to their side of the protection. It

refers to the QB being responsible for negating the free runner(s) by getting the ball out quickly

(like a hot potato) to a designated route built into almost all of our 5- or 6-man dropback

protections (Quick protections don’t apply here as the ball is designed to be out quick anyway).

We will always be on alert (“ALERT HOT” G& @ ) for potential Hot situations by using our

Pressure ID toolbox and simple arithmetic. If the called play is a 5- or 6-man protection with no

Hot built in, our audible system has options built into every formation structure we can

seamlessly check into. This ensures snaps will not go to waste. Our “Hot” system is typically

reserved for man pressure coverages like Cover 0 variants or Cover 1 overloads.

“SIGHT ADJUSTMENTS” = A mechanism we can utilize on any snap where the defense blitzes

using position players or areas of the field normally reserved for coverage (mainly Replacement

Zone Dogs/Blitzes and Free Access Cover 0). The actual adjustment on the route will be based

on where the vacated space is and who has the leverage to exploi'

it.

“PEEK RULE” = A nonnegotiable rule we teach to our skill position players that requires them to

“peek” back to the QB within 4 yards if the defender aligned over you blitzes. In the same ilk as

a Sight Adjustment, but not in such a defined way as we teach with S.A.’s.

™Q’ CALL” = Alerts the innermost eligible receiver to be ready for a quick throw if the QB is hot

by utilizing the aforementioned “Peek Rule.”
```
