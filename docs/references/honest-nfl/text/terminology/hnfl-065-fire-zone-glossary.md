FIRE ZONE GLOSSARY

TIGHT 1/3 (CB TECHNIQUE) = A match technique in which the Corner is responsible for #1 unless they go shallow

across the No Cover Zone and an “In” call is made. Can be played from a “Clue” or “MOD” alignment.

CLUE 1/3 (CB TECHNIQUE) = A vision zone technique in which the Corner is responsible for both #1 & #2 vertical by

utilizing a “2-to-1” key read. If only #1 is vertical, he will cap it and play top-down on it. If only #2 is vertical, he will

squeeze it top-down, outside-in using his post help deep to constrict any possible seam window. If both #1 & #2 are

vertical, he will midpoint them, get vision to the QB, then squeeze to where the QB’s eyes & body language lead him

(‘Vision & Squeeze” technique). Can be played from a “Clue” or “MOD” alignment.

SEAM/FLAT (APEX TECHNIQUE) = An undercoverage technique in which the Apex plays #2 using “Match-Carry-Deliver”

principles. They will match anything that crosses their face, carry seams, and deliver anything inside to the Midpoint (unless

#3 is away).

#3 RECEIVER HOOK (MIDPOINT TECHNIQUE) = An undercoverage technique in which the Midpoint is responsible for

relating to the final #3 in the distribution. Possible “Push” to new #3 if original #3 is fast to the flat.

KEY & MATCH (ZONE PRINCIPLE) = A form of zone coverage with M/M principles that utilizes their key reads to

streamline pattern recognition and leverage routes properly. Defenders are matching routes that break into their leverage

side and delivering routes that break away from them into their help.

VISION & BREAK (ZONE PRINCIPLE) = A form of zone coverage in which defenders drop to landmarks, play with

vision on the QB while feeling routes & receivers, and break to the catch point using the QB's body language as a trigger.

MAN MATCH = A form of coverage that plays like man-to-man unless their receiver releases into their help, then they

zone off and pattern read based on the concept played. It’s man-to-man with zone principles built in when necessary.

2-3-2 PRINCIPLE = An undercoverage principles in which the Apexes match the final #2 to their side and the Midpoint

matches the final #3 in the distribution. Also applies to Quarters.

#3 AWAY = Denotes the backside of the coverage in which #3 occupies the Midpoint. As a result, the “Seam/Flat”

defender is also responsible for taking #2 back across the formation (no delivery).

v CLUE = An adjustment of route distribution principles made when there is a threat of an immediate exchange — stacks,

bunches, dirty bunches, reduced formations, etc. It turns the coverage into one with “vision & break” zone principles.

®. “CHINA” CALL = A live coverage adjustment made by the outer defender when the receiver they align over runs a

route that doesn’t clear the No Cover Zone. They will zone it off in order to combat Smash concepts.

®. “IN” CALL = A live coverage adjustment made by the outer defender when the receiver they align over runs shallow

across the No Cover Zone. They will zone it off to combat Hi/Low concepts with Shallow routes.

®. “PUSH” CALL = A live coverage adjustment made between the Midpoint & Apex defenders when #3 releases fast to

the flat, resulting in a quick exchange. “Push” call is the defense’s way to re-distribute that exchange while properly

leveraging the routes involved in the exchange. Apex matches the flat (new #2) while Midpoint pushes to take the new #3.

FINAL #1-2-3-4 = Denotes receiving threats from outermost to innermost within the pattern after distribution.

SQUEEZE ALIGNMENT = A pre-snap coverage alignment taken by an Apex or Midpoint defender in which they align

outside of the relative receiver in order to force #2/#3 to cross face to the flat. Depth can vary.

CLUE ALIGNMENT = A pre-snap coverage alignment taken by a Corner in which they align 7+ yards deep and outside of

#1 in order to be able to properly read their keys and maintain advantageous positioning on routes.

HEADS ALIGNMENT = A pre-snap coverage alignment taken by any defender in which they align head-up over the

relative receiver. This allows them to disguise intent and prevent us from using the Apex’s alignment as a context clue.

MOD (PRESS-BAIL) ALIGNMENT = A pre-snap coverage alignment taken by a Corner in which they align pressed up to

#1 before bailing for depth (usually late in the cadence -- sometimes post-snap).

ZONE ALIGNMENT (PRESENTATION) = Denotes a pre-snap coverage alignment in which defenders align relative to

their zone responsibility rather than a specific matchup against like talent. Corners align relative to #1, Apexes align relative

to #2, and Midpoint aligns relative to #3. Corners do not follow X/Z across vs. slot. All motions are bumped across.

M-C-D — Denotes “Match-Carry-Deliver” coverage principle played within the undercoverage of match zone coverages.

MATCH - The act of latching onto a route that releases into the defender’s leverage (ex. #2 Flat vs. “Seam/Flat”)

CARRY - The act of running with vertical routes up the field working into their deep help (ex. #2 Seam vs. “Seam/Flat”)

DELIVER -— The act of passing off a route that releases away from the defender’s leverage into their help underneath.
