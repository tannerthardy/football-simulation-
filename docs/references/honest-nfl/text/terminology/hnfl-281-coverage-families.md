**Familiar language:** Pressure shells: fire, burn, deuce, quattro, quiz and hot zone

**Basis:** source-stated — editorial interpretation for navigation; not a corrected source transcription or an exact universal alias. [Confidence and method](../../CONCEPTS.md#how-to-use-these-interpretations).

Five-rush fire/burn differ by sky/buzz; Deuce has two-deep/four-under; Hot has six rushers and three-deep/two-under. Red-zone, bunch and slot are context categories, not one shell.

```text
COVERAGE FAMILIES

FIRE ZONE = A THREE DEEP, THREE UNDER MATCH ZONE PRESSURE COVERAGE PLAYED

BEHIND A 5-MAN CHARGE WITH SKY ROTATION. ALIGNMENTS WILL BE BASED ON THE FRONT

CHARGE AND OUR FORMATION.

BURN ZONE = A THREE DEEP, THREE UNDER MATCH ZONE PRESSURE COVERAGE PLAYED

BEHIND A 5-MAN CHARGE WITH BUZZ ROTATION. ALIGNMENTS WILL BE BASED ON THE

FRONT CHARGE AND OUR FORMATION. (**NEW FOR 2026, BURN ZONE IS THE BUZZ VERSION

OF FIRE ZONE. “MYRTLE” IS DEAD.)

DEUCE ZONE = A TWO DEEP, FOUR UNDER MATCH ZONE PRESSURE COVERAGE PLAYED

BEHIND A 5-MAN CHARGE. CAN BE TAGGED AS “DEUCE ROLL” FOR A SUPER ROTATION,

“DEUCE READ” FOR A READ PRINCIPLE PLAYED TO A DETACHED #2, OR “DEUCE TRAP” FOR A

TRAP PRINCIPLE.

QUATTRO ZONE = A POTENTIAL FOUR DEEP, TWO UNDER MATCH ZONE PRESSURE COVERAGE

PLAYED BEHIND A 5-MAN CHARGE. MOST COMMONLY PLAYED TO STACKS OR AN OVERSPLIT

#1 ADJACENT TO AN ATTACHED #2. THEY ARE PLAYING 3-OVER-2 QUARTERS PRINCIPLES TO

EACH SIDE WITH “#3 AWAY” RULES.

QUIZ ZONE = A COMBINATION PRESSURE COVERAGE THAT PLAYS WITH QUARTERS

PRINCIPLES TO THE NICKEL/SAM SIDE AND COVER 0 PRINCIPLES TO THE WEAK SIDE.

HOT ZONE = A THREE DEEP, TWO UNDER TRUE “VISION & BREAK” ZONE PRESSURE

COVERAGE PLAYED BEHIND A 6-MAN CHARGE.

RED ZONE COVERAGES = COVERAGES DESIGNED SPECIFICALLY TO BE PLAYED WITHIN THE 15

YARD LINE FOR THE DEFENSE. THEY ARE UTILIZING THE BACK OF THE ENDZONE AS A 127H

DEFENDER AND ALLOWING DEEP DEFENDERS TO BE MORE PROACTIVE UNDERNEATH.

BUNCH COVERAGES = COVERAGES DESIGNED SPECIFICALLY TO BE PLAYED WHEN PRESENTED

WITH THE THREAT OF MULTIPLE EXCHANGES. THIS HAPPENS WHEN #1 & #3 ARE ALIGNED

CLOSELY (WITHIN ABOUT 5-6 YARDS) IN A BUNCH (3x) OR DIRTY BUNCH (2x + HB) LOOKS.

SLOT COVERAGES = COVERAGES DESIGNED SPECIFICALLY TO BE PLAYED AGAINST SINGLE-

WIDTH SLOT/NUB FORMATIONS WITH ‘X’ & ‘Z’ ALIGNED TO THE SAME SIDE OF THE

FORMATION.
```
