COVERAGE STRUCTURE TERMINOLOGY

COVER 0 — A NO DEEP MAN-TO-MAN COVERAGE STRUCTURE, TYPICALLY WITH TWO ADJUSTERS IN SOME FORM OF

MAN PRESSURE OR RED ZONE COVERAGE.

COVER 1—A SINGLE SAFETY MAN-TO-MAN COVERAGE STRUCTURE WITH AN ADJUSTER. CAN BE PRESENTED AS A

SINGLE SAFETY COVERAGE OR ROTATED TO FROM A SHELL LOOK. MOST COMMON FORM IS SOME VARIATION OF

OUTSIDE-LEVERAGED MAN WITH INSIDE HELP FROM A ZONE DEFENDER.

COVER 2—A TWO DEEP SPLIT SAFETY ZONE COVERAGE STRUCTURE, TYPICALLY WITH FIVE IN THE UNDERCOVERAGE.

CAN BE PLAYED WITH ZONE (“CLOUD”/” TAMPA”), ZONE MATCH (“WILD”), OR MAN MATCH (“CLAMP”) MECHANICS.

COVER 3 — A THREE DEEP, FOUR UNDER SINGLE SAFETY ZONE COVERAGE STRUCTURE WITH STRONG ROTATION.

STRONGSIDE USES SKY, BUZZ, OR CLOUD ROTATION. CAN BE PLAYED WITH ZONE (“TREY”), ZONE MATCH (“DANGER”),

OR MAN MATCH (“RAIDER”) MECHANICS.

COVER 4—A POTENTIAL FOUR DEEP, THREE UNDER SPLIT SAFETY ZONE COVERAGE STRUCTURE. THIS IS THE MOST

DIVERSE FAMILY IN TERMS OF CONCEPTS AND THE MECHANICS IT CAN BE PLAYED FROM. TYPICALLY USED AS A MATCH

COVERAGE WITH DISTRIBUTION BASED ON KEYING RELEASES AND READING PATTERNS.

COVER 5 — A TWO DEEP, FIVE UNDER SPLIT SAFETY MAN COVERAGE STRUCTURE. CAN BE PLAYED WITH MAN-TO-MAN

OR MAN MATCH COVERAGE MECHANICS FROM A HALVES (“FIST”) OR QUARTERS (“POUNDER”) STRUCTURE.

COVER 6 —A THREE DEEP, FOUR UNDER SPLIT SAFETY ZONE COVERAGE STRUCTURE THAT PLAYS 4-OVER-3 QUARTERS

CONCEPTS STRONG AND 3-OVER-2 HALVES CONCEPTS WEAK. TYPICALLY PLAYED WITH ZONE MATCH MECHANICS

(“STUFF”), BUT NOT EXCLUSIVELY.

COVER 7 — A SPLIT SAFETY MAN MATCH COVERAGE STRUCTURE THAT PLAYS AS “+1” TO EACH SIDE USING ANY FORM

OF MAN TOOLS. TYPICALLY USED AS BRACKET COVERAGES IN THE RED ZONE, BUT NOT EXCLUSIVELY.

COVER 8 — A THREE DEEP, FOUR UNDER SPLIT SAFETY ZONE COVERAGE STRUCTURE THAT PLAYS HALVES CONCEPTS

STRONG AND QUARTERS CONCEPTS WEAK, BOTH BASED ON FORMATIONS & ALIGNMENTS. TYPICALLY PLAYED WITH

ZONE MATCH MECHANICS (“STUFF NICKEL”), BUT NOT EXCLUSIVELY. POSSIBLE 5-OVER-3 STRONG VS. 3x1 IF #3 IS UP OR

OVER (“TRIX”/” TRIP”).

COVER 9 — A THREE DEEP, FOUR UNDER SINGLE SAFETY ZONE COVERAGE STRUCTURE WITH WEAK ROTATION.

WEAKSIDE USES SKY, BUZZ, OR CLOUD ROTATION. CAN BE PLAYED WITH ZONE (“TREY”), ZONE MATCH (“DANGER”) OR

MAN MATCH (“RAIDER”) MECHANICS.

FIRE ZONE — A THREE DEEP, THREE UNDER SINGLE SAFETY ZONE PRESSURE COVERAGE PLAYED BEHIND A FIVE-MAN

CHARGE. TYPICALLY PLAYED WITH MATCH MECHANICS (“FREEZE”), BUT CAN BE PLAYED AS TRUE ZONE (“CLUE”) BASED

ON GAME PLAN, FORMATION, OR ALIGNMENTS.

DEUCE ZONE — A TWO DEEP, FOUR UNDER SPLIT SAFETY ZONE PRESSURE COVERAGE PLAYED BEHIND A FIVE-MAN

CHARGE. TYPICALLY PLAYED WITH MATCH MECHANICS USING “READ” OR “TRAP” PRINCIPLES.

QUATTRO ZONE — A POTENTIAL FOUR DEEP, TWO UNDER SPLIT SAFETY ZONE PRESSURE COVERAGE PLAYED BEHIND A

FIVE-MAN CHARGE. TYPICALLY PLAYED WITH MATCH MECHANICS USING QUARTERS PRINCIPLES.

HOT ZONE — A THREE DEEP, TWO UNDER SINGLE SAFETY ZONE PRESSURE COVERAGE PLAYED BEHIND A SIX-MAN

CHARGE. PLAYED WITH TRUE VISION ZONE PRINCIPLES BASED ON FORMATIONS & ALIGNMENTS.
