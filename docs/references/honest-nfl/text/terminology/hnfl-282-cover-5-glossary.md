COVER 5 GLOSSARY

FIST M/M (CB/APEX TECHNIQUE) = A true man coverage technique in which the defender takes inside leverage on their assignment

and plays trail technique underneath while expecting deep help over the top from outside-in

POUNDER M/M (CB/APEX TECHNIQUE) = A true man coverage technique in which the defender takes head-up or slight outside

leverage on their assignment and plays man technique with the possi

ity of safety help from inside-out.

M/M (TECHNIQUE) = A true man coverage technique played over threats working out of the core. Help varies by call

INSIDE QUARTERS (POUNDER TECHNIQUE) = A variation of Quarters technique played by a safety in which they execute their

normal 2-to-1 read and apply help accordingly. Can play like a “Robber” technique by game plan.

MAN-TO-MAN (COVERAGE PRINCIPLE) = The most basic form of coverage in which defenders are responsible for all of the routes

run by their assignment.

MAN MATCH (COVERAGE PRINCIPLE) = A form of coverage that plays like man-to-man unless their receiver releases into their help,

then they zone off and pattern read based on the concept played. It’s man-to-man with zone principles built in when necessary.

“ZONE OFF” = The act of converting from a man technique to a zone technique with pattern reads to find work.

FIST (COVERAGE) = Cover 5 concept that plays Fist to both sides (aggressive inside-leveraged M/M underneath with Deep 1/2 help).

POUNDER (COVERAGE) = Cover 5 concept that plays Pounder to both side (help-based Man-Match + Inside 1/4's help deep)

INGER (COVERAGE) = Cover 5 concept that plays Fist to the Nickel/Sam side with Pounder away. Possible “Trix” vs. Ttips.

OLEX (COVERAGE) = Cover 5 with Pounder strong and Fist weak (“Rolex” = Roll coverage to the ‘X’). with applicable tools.

RING # (TARGETED COVERAGE TO A TAGGED #) = Cover 5 concept

STRANGLE # (TARGETED COVERAGE TO A TAGGED #) = Our one-word

FIST (CONCEPT)

Denotes inside-leveraged man coverage underneath with a Deep 1/2 safety over the top

POUNDER (CONCEPT)

Denotes man coverage underneath with a Quarters safety deep.

v CHOKE = Adjustment vs. Trips in which the Corner locks #1 with Fist played over #2 & #3

¥ TRAP = A man-match Cover 5 concept in which the Corner, Apex, and Safety play Trap based on #2's release.

v BINDE

= Adjustment vs. Trips in which the Corner locks #1 with Trap played over #2 & #3

v PUSH = Fist adjustment vs. Trips that pushes both safeties strong (like 3 Cloud)

v TRIX (TRIP) = Finger adjustment vs. Trips in which the Free Safety plays Trix (Trip) technique based on #3's route. #3 up/over, the

Free Safety will overtake it and relieve the Midpoint. No #3 threat, Free Safety robs ‘X’ (Trix) or Post (Trip).

¥ THUMBS = A 2-over-1 inverted form of Fist in which the Corner drops as the 1/2 defender while the Safety cuts off #1 from inside

as a Fist M/M defender.

v NAIL = A 3-over-2 inverted form of Fist in which the Corner drops as the 1/2 defender while the Safety & Apex cut off #1 & #2,

respectively, as Fist M/M defenders.

v¥ SWITCH = A 3-over-2 man-match concept that plays as Cut or Cone based on the release of #2

JV BUSTER = A 4-over-3 man-match concept that zones off any exchange by #2/3. Corner is Sink technique and can “steal” #2 by

having #2 awareness and falling off #1 vs. Deep Exchanges (Snakes & Sails). Can apply to bunches.

v MINI FIST = Fist adjustment vs. bunches locks the point man and plays “In & Out” on #1 & #3 with a Deep 1/2 safety.

¥ MINI POUNDER = Pounder adjustment vs. bunches locks the point man and plays “In & Out” on #1 & #3 with a Quarters safety

deep.

STEAL (TECHNIQUE) = A coverage technique played by a deep defender in which they utilize a pattern read to fall off of the clearout

route and trap the live route.

RAIL (TECHNIQUE) = A true man coverage technique played underneath a Deep 1/2 (or Tight 1/2) safety in which the defender

favors the inside hip from a low position in order to properly “deflate” the route in conjunction with the deep help.

“DENY-THE-INSIDE” (LEVERAGE) = A Fist coverage approach taken by a Fist M/M defender with an initial focus on denying all inside

releases by their man in order to funnel the route back outside or up the field where help awaits. Both options force us to make

tougher throws either through layers of defenders or to a shrinking spot on the field.

FIST ALIGNMENT = A pre-snap coverage alignment taken by a Corner or Apex defender in which they align pressed up to their

matchup with inside leverage. They are expecting help over the top from outside-in

POUNDER ALIGNMENT = A pre-snap coverage alignment taken by a Corner or Apex defender in which they align pressed up to their

matchup with head-up or outside leverage. They are expecting help over the top from inside-out.

HEADS ALIGNMENT =A pre-snap coverage alignment taken by any defender in which they align head-up over the relative receiver.

This allows them to disguise intent and prevent us from using the Apex’s alignment as a context clue.

MAN ALIGNMENT (PRESENTATION) = Denotes a pre-snap coverage alignment in which defenders align relative to matchups against

like talent — speed on speed, size on size, skill on skill
