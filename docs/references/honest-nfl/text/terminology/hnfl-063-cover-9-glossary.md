COVER 9 GLOSSARY

TIGHT 1/3 (CB TECHNIQUE) = A match technique in which the Corner is responsible for #1 unless #1 goes shallow and an

“In” call is made. Can be played from a “Clue” or “MOD” alignment.

SEAM/ FLAT (APEX TECHNIQUE) = A match technique in which the Apex plays #2 with “Match-Carry-Deliver” principles.

They are responsible for matching any route by #2 that crosses their face to the outside, carrying any vertical stem up the

seam by #2, or delivering anything inside or shallow and zoning off to find work & pattern read with a seam-to-flat mindset.

#3/HOOK (MIDPOINT TECHNIQUE) = A match technique for balanced formations in which both potential Midpoint

defenders “Combo” #3 and fulfill their subsequent coverage responsibility accordingly. If #3 releases into the pattern, the “#3/

Hook” defender to that side becomes the Midpoint player and matches #3 (or the final #3 if “Push” call is made). The

remaining “#3/Hook” defender away from #3 becomes the Hook defender playing with vision while feeling route patterns.

#3 RECEIVER HOOK (WEAKSIDE HOOK TECHNIQUE) = A match technique played to “x1” formations in which the

weakside Hook defender is responsible for either the final #3 deep, the 1% shallow, or the final #4 in “Flow” concepts.

CLUE 1/3 (CB TECHNIQUE) = A vision & break technique in which the Corner is responsible for vertical threats by #1 & #2

to their side. To streamline their movement efficiency, they utilize a “2-to-1” read and defend accordingly. If #2 is not vertical,

the Corner will lock onto #1 and play top-down. If #2 is vertical, they will midpoint #1 & #2 and then read #1. If #1 is also

vertical, they will play vision & squeeze technique off the QB’s body language. If only #2 is vertical, they will squeeze the seam

from above the outside shoulder and close off the seam window in conjunction with the Post Safety.

HOOK-CURL (MIDPOINT TECHNIQUE) = A vision & break technique in which the defender takes a Hook drop and gets

vision to the QB while feeling route patterns. If the route pattern has no threat to the Hook zone, they will push for width and

close off the Curl window.

CURL-FLAT (APEX TECHNIQUE) = A vision & break technique in which the Apex takes a Curl drop and gets vision to the

QB while feeling route patterns. They will look to hold off any seam threat and will play top-down when stretched. They will

latch onto Wheel routes.

RAIDER (APEX TECHNIQUE) = A man-match technique in which the Apex is in M/M on #2 unless #2 goes shallow and an

“In” call is made. “In” = they zone off and pattern read.

KEY & MATCH (ZONE PRINCIPLE) = A form of zone coverage with M/M principles that utilizes their key reads to streamline

pattern recognition and leverage routes properly. Defenders are matching routes that break into their leverage side and

delivering routes that break away from them into their help. Allows coverages to each side to play like “Lurk” against verticals

& out-breakers or “Trey” against shallows & unders.

VISION & BREAK (ZONE PRINCIPLE) = A form of zone coverage in which defenders drop to landmarks, play with vision

on the QB while feeling routes & receivers, and break to the catch point using the QB’s body language as a trigger.

MAN MATCH = A form of coverage that plays like man-to-man unless their receiver releases into their help, then they zone

off and pattern read based on the concept played. It’s man-to-man with zone principles built in when necessary.

#3 AWAY = Denotes the backside of the coverage in which #3 occupies the Midpoint.

vV DANGER = Cover 9 with match zone mechanics.

¥ RAIDER = Cover 9 with man-match mechanics.

v TREY = Cover 9 with vision & break zone mechanics.

v CLUE = Cover 9 adjustment for stacks, bunches, and reduced splits with match principles from zone pickups.

vV SPOT = Cover 9 adjustment for stacks, bunches, and reduced splits with vision & break principles from zone pickups.

v SITE = Cover 9 adjustment for 3x1 formations in which the form of rotation is based on the release of #2. If #2 weak scat

releases or carries it, the Free Safety is the weak Apex defender (Sky Weak). If #2 weak blocks or is slow to go, the Free

Safety is the “#3 Receiver Hook” defender while the Dime is the Apex (Buzz Weak).

v GRAB = Cover 9 adjustment for 3x1 formations that turns it into 9 Buzz.

®. “CHINA” CALL = A live coverage adjustment made by the outer defender when the receiver they align over runs a route

that doesn’t clear the No Cover Zone. They will zone it off in order to combat Smash concepts.

®. “IN” CALL = A live coverage adjustment made by the outer defender when the receiver they align over runs shallow

across the No Cover Zone. They will zone it off to combat Hi/Low concepts with Shallow routes.

FINAL #1-2-3-4 = Denotes receiving threats from outermost to innermost within the pattern after distribution.

SQUEEZE ALIGNMENT = A pre-snap coverage alignment taken by an Apex or Midpoint defender in which they align outside

of the relative receiver in order to prevent free access to the flat. Depth varies, but typically is about 5 yards deep.

CLUE ALIGNMENT = A pre-snap coverage alignment taken by a Corner in which they align 7+ yards deep and outside of #1

in order to be able to properly read their keys and maintain advantageous positioning on routes.

HEADS ALIGNMENT = A pre-snap coverage alignment taken by any defender in which they align head-up over the relative

receiver. This allows them to disguise intent and prevent us from using the Apex’s alignment as a context clue.

MOD (PRESS-BAIL) ALIGNMENT = A pre-snap coverage alignment taken by a Corner in which they align pressed up to #1

before bailing for depth (usually late in the cadence -- sometimes post-snap).

ZONE ALIGNMENT (PRESENTATION) = Denotes a pre-snap coverage alignment in which defenders align relative to their

zone responsibility rather than a specific matchup against like talent. Corners align relative to #1, Apexes align relative to #2,

and Midpoint aligns relative to #3. Corners do not follow X/Z across vs. slot. All motions are bumped across.
