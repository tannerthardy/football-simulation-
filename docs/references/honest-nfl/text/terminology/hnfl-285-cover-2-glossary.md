COVER 2 GLOSSARY

fig

SQUAT (CB TECHNIQUE) = A flat zone technique played by a Corner (unless “/ Lock”) from a Squat alignment over #1 in

which the defender plays outside-in to funnel #1 inside, then sinks to the necessary depth while keying #2 & #3 for any

potential flat threat. No flat threat = can latch onto #1 and play trail technique.

SMASH (CB TECHNIQUE) = A flat zone technique played by a Corner from a Heads or Fist alignment over #1 in which the

defender plays inside-out to funnel #1 wide outside, then flips their hips to sink to the necessary depth while keying #2 & #3

for any potential flat threat. No flat threat = can latch onto #1 and play trail technique.

WALL (TECHNIQUE) = A match zone technique played by an Apex or Midpoint defender from a Wall alignment in which they

are responsible for matching anything upfield or inside by their assignment. If their assignment releases flat, they will zone off

and look for their new responsibility coming back inside. Applies to Vertical Curl, Vertical Hook, Wall Hook, and Middle Hook

techniques.

MIDDLE HOOK (MIDPOINT TECHNIQUE) = A match zone technique played by the Midpoint in which they are responsible for

relating to the final #3 in the pattern. They are keying #3-to-1 in their pattern read (#2 is occupied by Apex).

VERTICAL CURL (APEX TECHNIQUE) = A match zone technique played by an Apex defender in which they will key #2-to-1 in

order to Wall #2 (or the final #2) or zone the Curl area and find work.

MIDDLE HOOK (MIDPOINT TECHNIQUE) = A match zone technique played by the Midpoint defender in which they will key #3-

to-1 in order to Wall #3 (or the final #3) or zone the Hook area and find work.

VERTICAL HOOK (MIDPOINT TECHNIQUE) = Same as “Vertical Curl,” but played by the Midpoint vs. 3x formations.

DEEP 1/2 (SAFETY TECHNIQUE) = A deep zone technique played by the Safety in which they will read releases from outside-in

to know how much width they'll need, then play top-down based on route concepts. If #1 is vertical, they will squeeze to #1

with awareness of #2/#3. If #1 is not vertical, they will deflate #2 and/or #3 upfield.

TAMPA 1/2 (SAFETY TECHNIQUE) = A deep zone technique played by the Safety in which they can play with more width

because the Middle Run defender will close the post vs. any seam threats. Can look like “Cloud 1/3” technique.

REROUTE CURL (APEX TECHNIQUE) = A vision zone technique played by the Apex defender in which they will look to re-route

#2 before dropping to their Curl window landmark and playing with vision to the QB while feeling routes.

MIDDLE RUN (MIDPOINT TECHNIQUE) = A vision zone technique played by the Midpoint defender in which they will open to

speed and key #2 & #3 to know how much depth they need. They must stay deeper than the deepest of #2 & #3 and close the

middle if a seam threat is presented. Can look like “Middle 1/3” of 3 Db/ Cloud depending on routes.

SINK (CB TECHNIQUE) = A match zone technique played by the Corner in which they are responsible for matching #1 with

awareness of #2. By keying #2-to-1, they can use pattern reads to inform their technique (ex. #2 flat = overplay #1 expecting

some form of Hank) and possibly steal #2 late within a Void Attacker route concept.

BUSTER (APEX TECHNIQUE) = A match zone technique played by an Apex defender in which they utilize a “Me/You” coverage

principle in conjunction with the Middle Read defender to properly leverage the routes of #2 & #3. Responsible for the

outermost threat of #2 & #3.

MIDDLE READ (MIDPOINT “BUSTER” TECHNIQUE) = A match zone technique played by the Midpoint defender in which they

utilize a “Me/You” coverage principle in conjunction with the Buster defender to properly leverage the routes of #2 & #3.

Responsible for the innermost threat of #2 & #3.

TIGHT 1/2 (SAFETY TECHNIQUE) = A deep zone technique played by the Strong Safety in which they play “Deep 1/2"

technique over #2 & #3 only — “Sink” Corner has #1 upfield, so the Safety can key #2-to-3 to play to inside threats upfield.

CLOUD FORCE (RUN SUPPORT) = Denotes a defense in which the Corner is the primary force defender responsible for

leveraging the ball carrier back inside to the help defenders.

SKY FORCE (RUN SUPPORT) = Denotes an inverted form of Cover 2 in which the Safety is the primary force defender

responsible for leveraging the ball carrier back inside to help defenders.

SPILL (RUN FIT) = A mode of play in the run game in which defenders are intent on leveraging the ball carrier out wide while

the deeper layers of the defense to overlap and work from depth. Commonly played with 7-man spacing underneath.

¥ WILD = A form of Halves that utilizes “key & match” zone principles with specific responsibilities based on where #3 is. If

played to Trips, Midpoint becomes a “Vertical Hook” defender and must carry #3 upfield.

v TAMPA = A form of 2 deep, 4 under Halves with a Middle Run defender that utilizes “vision & break” principles with an

emphasis on re-routing to disrupt timing & spacing. Can become a middle-closed coverage vs. any seam threat by #2/#3.

Vv CLOUD = A form of Halves that utilizes true “vision & break” zone principles with an emphasis on dropping to landmarks,

feeling route combinations, and keying the QB to trigger their break to the catch point.

v LOCK = A 4-over-3 form of Halves played to Trips that locks the Corner onto #1 and plays “Wild” inside over #2 & #3.

Vv SQUAT = A 4-over-3 form of Halves played to bunches that uses the Corner-Apex-Midpoint to match the final #1-2-3

underneath, respectively, while the Safety handles upfield threats over the top.

J BUSTER = A 4-over-3 form of Halves played to formations that present an immediate exchange by #2 & #3 (including

bunches) threat that plays like “Buster” or “Box” based on the release of #1. If #1 stays #1, it plays out like “Buster” with tight

matching underneath. If #1 goes under #2 (exchanges), it plays like “Box” would. Apex & Midpoint play in/out on #2 & #3.
