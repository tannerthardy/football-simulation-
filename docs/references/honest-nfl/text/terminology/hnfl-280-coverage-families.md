COVERAGE FAMILIES

fig

COVER 0 = A NO DEEP MAN-TO-MAN COVERAGE STRUCTURE, TYPICALLY WITH TWO ADJUSTERS IN SOME

FORM OF MAN PRESSURE OR ZONE COVERAGE.

COVER 1 =A SINGLE SAFETY MAN-TO-MAN COVERAGE STRUCTURE WITH AN ADJUSTER. CAN BE

PRESENTED AS A SINGLE SAFETY COVERAGE OR ROTATED TO FROM A SHELL LOOK. MOST COMMON

FORM IS SOME VARIATION OF OUTSIDE-LEVERAGED MAN WITH INSIDE HELP FROM A ZONE DEFENDER.

COVER 2 = A TWO DEEP SPLIT SAFETY ZONE COVERAGE STRUCTURE — TYPICALLY WITH FIVE IN THE

UNDERCOVERAGE — THAT PLAYS A HALVES-BASED ZONE CONCEPT TO BOTH SIDES. CAN BE PLAYED WITH

ZONE (“CLOUD”/” TAMPA”), ZONE MATCH (“WILD”) OR MAN MATCH (“CLAMP”) MECHANICS.

COVER 3 = A THREE DEEP, FOUR UNDER TRUE “VISION & BREAK” ZONE COVERAGE WITH “TREY”

PRINCIPLES PLAYED TO ALL NON-“x1” FORMATIONS. CAN BE PLAYED WITH A SKY, BUZZ, OR CLOUD

ROTATION TO EITHER THE STRONG OR WEAK SIDE BASED ON PASSING STRENGTH, FIELD/BOUNDARY, OR

BY GAME PLAN. (**THIS IS NEW FOR 2026. IT IS NO LONGER JUST FOR STRONG-ROTATED 3 DEEP, 4

UNDER COVERAGES)

COVER 4 =A POTENTIAL FOUR DEEP, THREE UNDER SPLIT SAFETY COVERAGE STRUCTURE THAT PLAYS A

QUARTERS-BASED ZONE CONCEPT TO BOTH SIDES. TYPICALLY PLAYED AS A MATCH COVERAGE BASED ON

KEYING RELEASES AND READING PATTERNS TO DETERMINE RESPONSIBILITIES.

COVER 5 = A TWO DEEP, FIVE UNDER SPLIT SAFETY MAN COVERAGE STRUCTURE. CAN BE PLAYED AS

TRUE MAN-TO-MAN OR WITH MAN-MATCH PRINCIPLES FROM A HALVES (“FIST”) OR QUARTERS

(“POUNDER”) STRUCTURE.

COVER 6 = A THREE DEEP, FOUR UNDER SPLIT SAFETY ZONE COVERAGE STRUCTURE THAT PLAYS A

QUARTERS-BASED CONCEPT TO THE STRONG SIDE WITH A HALVES-BASED CONCEPT TO THE WEAK SIDE.

TYPICALLY PLAYED WITH ZONE MATCH MECHANICS (“PACK”), BUT NOT EXCLUSIVELY. (**NEW FOR 2026,

WE NOW CALL SUB COVER 6 “PACK” SO THAT WE CAN RELATE IT DIRECTLY TO OUR SIX-PACK SIGNAL)

COVER 7 = A SPLIT SAFETY MAN-MATCH COVERAGE STRUCTURE THAT PLAYS AS “+1” IN COVERAGE TO

EACH SIDE. TYPICALLY FEATURES SOME FORM OF BRACKET CONCEPT, BUT WE ALLOW IT TO INCLUDE

VARIATIONS OF OTHER CONCEPTS THAT DON’T FIT DIRECTLY INTO ANOTHER FAMILY (“7 MOD/NAIL”). IT

IS UTILIZED ALMOST EXCLUSIVELY FOR CHARTING DISPARATE CONCEPTS WHEN SCOUTING DEFENSES.

COVER 8 = A THREE DEEP, FOUR UNDER SPLIT SAFETY ZONE COVERAGE STRUCTURE THAT PLAYS A

HALVES-BASED CONCEPT TO THE STRONG SIDE WITH A QUARTERS-BASED CONCEPT TO THE WEAK SIDE.

TYPICALLY PLAYED WITH ZONE MATCH MECHANICS (“STUFF”), BUT NOT EXCLUSIVELY. (**NEW FOR 2026,

WE NOW CALL SUB COVER 8 “STUFF” SO THAT WE CAN RELATE IT AS THE INVERSE OF “PACK”)

COVER 9 = A THREE DEEP, FOUR UNDER “KEY & MATCH” ZONE COVERAGE WITH “DANGER” PRINCIPLES

PLAYED TO ALL FORMATIONS WITH NORMAL SPLITS. ONLY AN EXCHANGE THREAT CAN RESULT INA

CHECK TO “TREY” PRINCIPLES. CAN BE PLAYED WITH SKY, BUZZ, OR CLOUD ROTATION TO EITHER THE

STRONG OR WEAK SIDE BASED ON PASSING STRENGTH, FIELD/BOUNDARY, OR BY GAME PLAN. (**THIS IS

NEW FOR 2026. IT IS NO LONGER JUST FOR WEAK-ROTATED 3 DEEP, 4 UNDER COVERAGES.)
