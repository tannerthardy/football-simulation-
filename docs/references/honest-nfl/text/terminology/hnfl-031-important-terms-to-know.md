IMPORTANT TERMS TO KNOW

ADJUSTER- A DEFENDER THAT WITHIN A COVERAGE THAT HAS AN ADJUSTABLE RESPONSIBLILTY (EX. BLITZ, SPY, HOLE, ETC.)

ALLEY - THE AREA BETWEEN THE SINGLE WR AND THE EMOL

BANJO - IN/OUT MATCH PRINCIPLES BETWEEN LINEBACKERS ON TWO THREATS IN THE CORE

BRACKET - A DOUBLE TEAM BY A SAFETY ON A RECIEVER

BUSTER - A FORM OF MATCH ZONE FROM A HALVES STRUCTURE THAT PRIORITIZES TIGHT MATCHES WHILE WALLING OFF THE INSIDE

CARRY - THE ACT OF RUNNING WITH VERTICAL ROUTES UP THE FIELD WORKING INTO THEIR DEEP SQUEEZE HELP.

CAT - A DEFENSIVE BACK INVOLVED IN THE CHARGE

CHARGE - DENOTES THE DEFENDERS INVOLVED IN THE PASS RUSH AND/OR PRESSURE CHARGING AT THE QUARTERBACK

CLOUD CORNER - DENOTES A CORNERBACK LINED AT A DEPTH OF 4-7 YARDS OFF OF #1.

COMBO - TWO DEFENDERS PLAYING IN & OUT ON TWO THREATS BY AREA OF RELEASE

DANGER - THE TERM WE USE TO DESCRIBE BASIC MATCH COVER 3/9 WITH THE CORNER & APEX MATCHING ANYTHING UP OR QUT.

DELIVER - THE ACT OF PASSING OFF A ROUTE THAT RELEASES AWAY FROM THE DEFENDER'S LEVERAGE INTO THEIR HELP UNDERNEATH.

DOG- A PRESSURE FEATURING AN OFF-BALL LINEBACKER WITHIN THE FRONT CHARGE

FINAL #1-2-3-4 ~ DENOTES RECEIVING

REATS FROM OUTERMOST TO INNERMOST WITHIN THE PATTERN AFTER DISTRIBUTION.

FIRE ZONE - A ZONE PRESSURE STRUCTURE FEATURING SOME COMBINATION OF 3 DEEP DEFENDERS AND 3 UNDERNEATH DEFENDERS

FREEZE - THE TERM WE USE TO DESCRIBE BASIC MATCH FIRE ZONE COVERAGE WITH A “TIGHT 1/3" CB & APEX USING "M-C-D" RULES.

HEAVY - DENOTES A 9 MAN BOX

HOLE - AREA BETWEEN THE HOOK AND CURL AREAS

INVERT - A FORM OF POST-SNAP ROTATION THAT INVERTS THE APEX TRIANGLE TO ONE OR BOTH SIDES OF THE FORMATION

LOCK - DENOTES A DEFENDER PLAYING MAN COVERAGE ON ALL ROUTES BY A RECEIVER WITHIN A NON-M/M COVERAGE CONCEP

M/M - DENOTES MAN-TO-MAN COVERAGE

M-C-D - DENOTES MATCH-CARRY-DELIVER COVERAGE PRINCIPLE PLAYED WITHIN MATCH ZONE COVERAGES.

MAN ALIGNMENT- PRE-SNAP M/M PRESENTATION WITH DEFENDERS ALIGNING RELATIVE TO MATCHUPS REGARDLESS OF FORMATION.

SPEED ON SPEED, SIZE ON SIZE, SKILL ON SKILL. ALL SHOW TYPICAL M/M BEHAVIOR (EYES-FEET-HIPS)

MAN MATCH - A FORM OF COVERAGE THAT PLAYS AS MAN-TO-MAN UNLESS THE ROUTE RELEASES INTO HELP, THEN THEY ZONE OFF AND

PATTERN READ BASED ON THE SPECIFIC CONCEP

HEY ARE PLAYING. IT 1S MAN-TO-MAN WITH ZONE PRINCIPLE ("MAN UNLESS..."

MATCH - THE ACT OF LATCHING ONTO A ROUTE THAT RELEASES INTO THE DEFENDER'S LEVERAGE.

MATCH ZONE - A FORM OF COVERAGE PLAYED WITHIN A ZONE STRUCTURE IN WHICH DEFENDERS ARE KEYING ROUTES & RELEASES IN

CONJUNCTION WITH THE OTHER DEFENDERS IN ORDER TO MAINTAIN ADVANTAGEOUS DEPTH & LEVERAGE DEFENDING A ROUTE CONCEPT.

MEG - CORNERBACK TECHNIQUE THAT STANDS FOR “MAN EVERYWHERE HE GOES". THEY'RE RESPONSIBLE FOR ALL ROUTES.

MOD - QUARTERS TECHNIQUE FOR CORNERBACKS THAT WE TEACH AS "MAN OUTSIDE & DEEP" (UNLESS “CHINA” OR “IN” CALL IS MADE)

OFF CORNER - DENOTES A CORNERBACK LINED UP AT A DEPTH GREATER THAN 7 YARDS THAT OFFERS FREE ACCESS INTO THE ROUTE.

PRESS CORNER - DENOTES A CORNERBACK LINED UP WITHIN 3 YARDS OF THE LOS OVER THE RECEIVER THEY'RE ALIGNED TO.

QUADS - THE TERM WE USE TO DESCRIBE BASIC MATCH QUARTERS COVERAGE WITH A CORNER IN “CLUE” ALIGNMENT ("MOD" = CB BAIL)

RAT - A DEFENDER FROM THE LOS DROPPING INTO THE LOW HOLE

ROBBER - STRONG SAFETY DROPPING INTO THE HOLE AREA

SHELL - DENOTES A SPLIT SAFETY STRUCTURE PRESENTATED PRE-SNAP

SINK (COVERAGE TAG) - SOFT BAIL ZONE TECHNIQUE

SINK (CORNER TECHNIQUE) - MATCH ZONE TECHNIQUE IN WHICH THE DEFENDER IS RESPONSIBLE FOR ROUTES BY #1 UPFIELD UNLESS

THERE IS AN IMMEDIATE THREAT 10 THE FLAT. A “SINK” CORNER ALSO HAS THE ABILITY TO FALL OFF #1 AND STEAL #2.

SPY - A TECHNIQUE OF PLAYING THE Q8, FOLLOWING HIM QUT OF THE POCKET. HIS RESPONSIBILTY IS SHEERLY THE OB

SSM - DENOTES A SINGLE SAFETY MIDDLE STRUCTURE

THIEF - FREE SAFETY DROPPING INTO THE HOLE AREA

VISION ZONE - A STYLE OF ZONE COVERAGE WHERE DEFENDERS DROP 10 LANDMARKS WITH VISION TO THE QUARTERBACK

WILD - THE TERM WE USE TO DESCRIBE BASIC MATCH HALVES COVERAGE WITH A SQUAT CORNER & VERTICAL CURL APEX.

ZONE ALIGNMENT - PRE-SNAP ZONE PRESENTATION WITH DEFENDERS ALIGNING RELATIVE TO FORMATION INSTEAD OF MATCHUPS.

CORNERS ALIGN RELATIVE TO #1, APEXES ALIGN RELATIVE TO #2, AND MIDPOINT ALIGNS RELATIVE TO #3.

ZORRO - THE TERM WE USE TO DESCRIBE QUARTERS COVERAGE WITH TRUE ZONE PRINCIPLES. CORNER & SAFETY HANDLES VERTICALS.
