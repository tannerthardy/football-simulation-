THE ALCHEMY OF OFFENSIVE PLAY CALLING

When it comes to calling offensive plays, “great” is subjective. However, there are

characteristics that most successful NFL play callers share that can be studied even if they're

not always quantifiable in the box score.

MANUFACTURING EXPLOSIVE PLAYS — This is the thing that typically gets the

attention as the sexier side of play calling, but it’s still important to recognize as a

fundamental aspect of successful play calling. The ability to manufacture explosive

plays incorporates other important aspects of the job.

SIMPLICITY DISGUISED AS COMPLEXITY — When we evaluate offenses, the way they

look gets a lot of the attention. The reality is that the way things are presented are

done so to accomplish more simplistic goals (ex. creating an advantageous matchup

or leveraged, providing the QB with info that allows them to streamline their

post-snap process, etc.). The great play callers accomplish the most basic goals by

merely presenting themselves as complex.

LIKE GREAT FILMS, THEIR CALL CHARTS TELL A STORY — Think about great play

calling like a great movie. Plays are individual frames. Drives are scenes. Every single

frame matters within a scene. The way they’re sequenced can impact the overall

effect for better or worse. There are setups and payoffs. Chart the offense of a great

play caller and the plays will start to tell a story...probably a very good one.

MASTERING THE THREE STAGES — There’s the game planning, the calls as dictated by

the plan, and the adjustments that are typically necessary to stay ahead of the

defense. Being good in only one or two of these stages means the play caller is

banking on players to go above & beyond to make the offense work. You'll notice that

the premier play callers excel in all three.

TALENTS ARE WEAPONIZED AND FEATURED — Every offense has its philosophy and

priorities in how the staff wants them to play — “fast,” “aggressive,

ca

physical,”

“fundamentally sound,” are all buzzwords with coaches during their introductory

press conference. They’re all really neat and when the offense actually comes to

fruition with those descriptors still holding true, a round of applause is deserved. But

they all mean very little when the talents of the players aren’t given the chance to

shine by being featured schematically. Any idiot can call a play and hope their guy is

better on that snap. Great play callers are consistently putting their players’ talents in

position to produce.

SITUATIONAL MASTERY — Any good call is always relative to the situation in which

it’s called. Mastering situational offense allows us to be extremely specific in what we

call and we can play on our terms. We have a “Short” (1-2 yards to go), “Medium”

(3-6 yards to go), “Long” (7-10 yards to go), and “XL” (11+ yards to go) category

based on the distance within 1st, 2nd, 3rd & 4th downs. Each will have some core

beliefs that stay the same and some that will change based on the game plan.
