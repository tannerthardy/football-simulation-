MADDEN (ALL GO)

Deep Horizontal Stretch — Progression Read w/ 2-on-1 Reads — 5-Step Pocket Science

INTENT: We want to be running this against zone — specifically Single Safety (Cover 3/9) with

Vision & Break principles — but we will have answers vs. M/M and match principles. At its core,

our Raider (All Go) package is built on the Deep Horizontal Stretch read, so we work this off

basic 5-step Pocket Science (3-step from Gun/Pistol) with a hitch to be able to quickly read our

key defenders and hit the routes on their landmarks at 18-22 yards.

PROCESS: We'’re reading this inside-out, top-down based on our 2-on-1 stretch reads. We

throw the Seam (Bender) routes off of “5 + Hitch” footwork, then hitch to the outside Go

routes before resetting back down to the underneath outlet. The coverage must sufficiently

eliminate the Seams before we progress out to the Go in order to make the concept difficult to

cover when executed properly. The mindset of the route runners will be based on the coverage,

so we must be completely synchronized with them in how it's read and how we adjust

Vs. MFC, key the Post Safety and find the Seam away from the squeeze with a landmark

of 18-22 yards deep. Work inside-out from the Seam on the side away from rotation

(work weak vs. strong rotation, work field vs. boundary rotation, etc.). Use your body

language on your drop to control the Post Safety. If they carry the Seams, know that the

Go routes will be 1v1 with the CB locked on and we can not stretch him. If they don't

carry, know that we can also stretch the CB.

Vs. MFO, key the Apex defender to the side of the underneath outlet and read it from

Bender to Outlet. We may build in a flash fake from Gun to control the undercoverage

and maximize the 2nd layer depth. Possible hole shot vs. Cover 2. The ball should be out

quickly enough that the spacing of the Benders is not an issue.
