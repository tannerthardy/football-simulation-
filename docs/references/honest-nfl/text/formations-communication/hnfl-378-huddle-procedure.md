Forming and Breaking the Huddle:

1. Center always sets up huddle SIX yards from the ball. Set up quickly. (Speed break @ 3)

2. Huddle is an oval with the OL facing away from LOS. QB will enter from the side closest to our sideline.

3. Line up quickly. Be in a position to see, hear, and look at the QB as he calls the play.

4. QB has complete control of the huddle and once he starts talking everyone is quiet.

5. You will receive the following information in the huddle:

A. Alerts (Snap count, potential Kill, Screen)

B. Pre-Formation Movement — “Movement into final formation” (‘Y’ Reset, “Y’ Shift, “Y' Motion)

C. Formation (Green Rt, West Rt, Trips Lt)

D. Formation adjustment (Snug, Nasty, Off)

E. Motions — “Movement from initial formation” (‘F’ Rt, ‘Z’ Short, ‘Z’ Lt)

F. Protection (w/ Tag) or Run series. (82, 2 Jet Hit, 96, 34)

G. Play terms. (“Y’ Piano, Buccaneer, ‘W’)

H. Snap Count (On 1, On 3, 2™ Color)

I. “Ready Break!” - Everyone claps breaking the huddle and gets to L.O.S. with sense of urgency.

6. When you do not hear what is said, call “Check - Check”

7. “Ready-break” is the signal to leave the huddle - clap hands - lineman move to LOS quickly.

8. The huddle reflects the attitude of each man and the team - KEEP THE HUDDLE SHARP and EVERYONE

BREAKS THE HUDDLE!!

EXAMPLE: “HB SHACK to GUN TREY LT 83 BUCCANEER BREAD ON ONE, READ Y-BREAK”
