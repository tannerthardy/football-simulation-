```text
Our system is of “West Coast” roots with modern flourishes to streamline communication where needed. In order to properly

digest information and be our best in a game setting, disregard any preconceptions you may have before entering the meeting

room. Our calls are structured carefully to maximize the amount of information we can deliver in 25-second intervals. Calls are

constructed in small sections that serve each position differently. Master those individual parts and you will master the offense.

DROPBACK PASS CALL STRUCTURE

PERSONNEL — (NOT INCLUDED IN HUDDLE CALL)

SHIFT — Denotes pre-snap movement by two or more players. (ex. EXPLODE to Gun Empty Rt)

“INTO” MOTION — Denotes pre-snap movement by a tagged player that gets us into the called formation. (ex. ‘Z’ SHORT

to Gun Trips Rt Bunch denotes a short motion by the ‘Z’ that ends up in the called formation)

BACKFIELD ALIGNMENT — Denotes the alignment of the players in the backfield, namely the QB and whether they align

under Center, in Gun, or Pistol.

BASE FORMATION — Denotes the basic formation structure and alignments. (ex. Trips Lt)

FORMATION ADJUSTMENT — A tag that denotes an adjustment to the base formation and/or alignment. (ex. Trips Rt

BUNCH, Trips Rt NASTY, DbI Rt SNUG)

SET THE ‘Y’ SIDE — Denotes the direction of the ‘Y’ alignment, which the corresponding players at or on the LOS align

relative to. Any formations for which the ‘Y’ rule does not apply will be noted on install. (ex. “Trey RT” = ‘Y’ aligns isolated

closed right with 3 receivers opposite to the open side)

“OUT OF” MOTION — Denotes a motion in which the tagged player moves from the designated formation into a look

designed for an individual play or package. (ex. “Gun Trey Rt Flex Buster ‘X’ RT” = start in Trey Rt Flex Buster, then ‘X’

motions across right)

ROTECTION — Denotes the protection to execute. Series of protection (ex. JET, 20 series, 70 series, etc.) denotes who

is involved and the type. Call digit (0-1, 2-3, 4-5, 8-9) denotes mechanics of the protection. Any protection with three

digits denotes quick game protection. (ex. 2 JET Falcon, 200 JET Stick Spacing, 383 Arizona)

PROTECTION TAG — Represents an additional directive applied to the protection call required to execute the call. (ex. 2

Jet CHIP, 3 Jet SALSA)

CONCEPT — Denotes the base passing concept. If there are multiple concepts, the concept to the call side is first and

the concept away is second. We use position tags frequently in order to best communicate and apply nuance to the base

concept (ex. ‘Y’ MAESTRO denotes ‘Y’ as the depth-setter of the mesh concept)

ALERT — Denotes a potential adjustment to part(s) of the concept called to be aware of and ready to convert to. (ex. 2

Jet Vegas @DEACON)

KILL — Denotes a built-in change of play call to be ready for based on a specific look that we deem unfavorable to the

original call, but may be exploitable if we run the “Kill” play instead. (ex. Fox 2 ‘X’-‘Y’ Hook KILL RUN IT builds in a run

play if the box count is favorable)

CADENCE — Denotes the timing of the pre-snap operation used to promote synchronization among all 11 offensive

players on the field.
```
