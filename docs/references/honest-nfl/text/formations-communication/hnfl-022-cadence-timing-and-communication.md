```text
“CADENCE” = The audible cue we use as a mechanism to synchronize our timing

and communication on offense based on the called count in the huddle. There

are varying types of cadences that serve different purposes. We believe in

weaponizing the cadence to not only ensure our offense is totally synchronized,

but also for the ways we can use it to challenge defenses. We can disrupt the

get-offs of the pass rushers, uncover post-snap rotation intentions, and just

keep defenses on their heels to begin the play. Our primary cadence is what we

call a “Non-Rhythmic Color-Number” cadence, but it is just one of a handful of

types that we will use regularly.

“RHYTHMIC” (CADENCE) = An audible cue in which the QB uses Color-Number in

truly rhythmic fashion to snap the ball. Players outside of the core are using a

visual cue of the Center snapping and the near OT coming out of their stance.

“NON-RHYTHMIC” (CADENCE) = An audible cue in which the QB uses

Color-Number in a non-rhythmic fashion to snap the ball. Players outside of the

core are using a visual cue of the Center snapping and the near OT coming out of

their stance.

“SILENT COUNT” (CADENCE) = A visual or physical cue in which the QB uses a

“leg lift” or initiates a “Center tap” to kickstart the snap count. Players outside

of the core are using a true visual cue of the Center snapping and the near OT

coming out of their stance.

“YELLOW” (CADENCE) = Calls for a double-count dummy cadence before going

on one. We use our Yellow cadence as a tool to uncover context clues of the

defense’s pre-snap presentation and how they may be moving post-snap. We

utilize it most often with our Man/Zone Alignment Read concepts (ex. Tampa

Read, Stache Wrap, Buccaneer Mini Me, etc.). BOLD text denotes a hard count

and the underlined “HUT” denotes the true signal to snap.

“YELLOW FIFTY... YELLOW FIFTY...SET HUT! HUT-HUT-HUT...WHITE EIGHTY... WHITE EIGHTY...SET HUT!”
```
