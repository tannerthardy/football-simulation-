CADENCE -- KILL PROCEDURE

Kill Package:

This is an important part of our offense in which we can call 2 plays in the huddle and run the

better play based off the defensive look. We can go from Run to Run , Run to Pass, Pass to

Pass or Pass to Run.

It sounds like this: Gun Trips Rt 35 ‘W’ kill 82 Buccaneer Bread on Yellow ... Break!”

“Set-Hut” Blue 80 ...Blue 80 Set-Hut...Hut (QB - Mike Point) .... Blue 80 ...Blue 80 Set-Hut

“Set-Hut” Blue 80 ...Blue 80 Set-Hut...Hut... “Kill, kill... Kill, kill’... (QB - Mike Point)... Blue 80

...Blue 80 Set-Hut (The Play is 82 Buccaneer Bread)

Note: Our kill signal will be swiping hand across the chest. (Signal can change by game plan)

Dahmer Kill:

There will be times when we want to use “Dahmer” kill to protect the package by using a fake

call. The QB will come into the huddle and alert the huddle to “Dahmer.” This will always be

used with GVA plays. (Previously called “Reload” -- defenses understood it too easily)

Example: QB Alerts the huddle to Dahmer and calls Solo Rt P. 97 W ‘F’ Seattle

“Set-Hut” Blue 80... “Kill, Kill... Kill, kill” ... Blue 80 ... Blue 80 Set-Hut (Play is P. 97 W ‘F’

Seattle).

Dexter Kill:

When we utilize our kill package, defenses may make their own check in response. If they get

to a look that is no longer favorable to the kill play, we use a “Dexter” call to revert back to the

primary call. “Dexter, Dexter” kills the kill, just like Dexter kills killers.

We do not alert for this in the huddle as we do with “Kill” and “Dahmer.” It must be understood

by all of us that when we hear “Dexter,” the initial kill is off. We go on one after any Dexter.

Ex. “Gun Trips Rt Same 34 Bam kill Arizona”

“Set-Hut” Blue 80... “Kill, kill ... Kill, kill” ... Blue 80 ... (“defense checks*) Blue 80 ... “Dexter,

Dexter! ... Dexter, Dexter!”... White 80 ... White 80 ... Set-Hut (Play went from Same 34 Bam

to Arizona, then back to Same 34 Bam).

Note: QB put rubber gloves on your open,

ao

Ing hand. (Signal can change by game plan)
