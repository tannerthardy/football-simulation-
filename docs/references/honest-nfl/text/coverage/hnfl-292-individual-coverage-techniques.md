INDIVIDUAL COVERAGE TECHNIQUES

IN ORDER TO FULLY COMPREHEND THE COVERAGE MECHANICS PLAYED WITHIN THESE POPULAR MATCH & ZONE CONCEPTS,

WE MUST UNDERSTAND THE INDIVIDUAL ROLES WITHIN THEM. THESE ARE THE TECHNIQUES THAT WE CONSIDER PARAMOUNT

TO THAT UNDERSTANDING. VISUALS WILL FOLLOW ON THE SUBSEQUENT PAGES.

QUARTERS (CB) — COVER 4/6/8 — MAN #1 UNLESS “CHINA” OR “IN” CALL IS MADE, THEN ZONE THE 1/4.

QUARTER FLAT (APEX) — COVER 4/6/8 — KEY 2-1. MATCH 157 FLAT OR FINAL #2 UNDERNEATH. #3 AWAY = TAKE #2 BACK ACROSS.

QUARTER HOOK (MIDPOINT) — COVER 4/6 — KEY 3-2-1. RELATE TO THE FINAL #3 IN THE DISTRIBUTION.

NSIDE QUARTER (SAFETY) — COVER 4/6 — KEY 2-1. TOP-DOWN ON #2 DEEP OR SQUEEZE TO #1.

TIGHT QUARTERS (CB) — COVER 4/8 — MAN #1 WEAK UNLESS SHALLOW (“IN”). “IN” CALL = ZONE THE 1/4.

TRIX (SAFETY) — COVER 4/8 — KEY 3-X. MATCH #3 UP/OVER OR SQUEEZE BACK TO THE ‘X’ RECEIVER

TRIP (SAFETY) — COVER 4/8 — KEY 3-POST. MATCH #3 UP/OVER OR ZONE THE POST, VISION & SQUEEZE.

RUE 1/4 (CB) — “ZORRO” — KEY 2-1. RESPONSIBLE FOR VERTICALS OF #1 & #2. VISION & SQUEEZE TECHNIQUE.

TRUE 1/4 (SAFETY) — “ZORRO” — KEY 3-2. RESPONSIBLE FOR VERTICALS OF #2 & #3. VISION & SQUEEZE TECHNIQUE.

CLUE 1/3 (CB) — “TREY” — KEY 2-1. RESPONSIBLE FOR VERTICALS OF #1 & #2. VISION & SQUEEZE TECHNIQUE.

TIGHT 1/3 (CB) — “DANGER”/” FREEZE” — MAN #1 UNLESS SHALLOW (“IN” CALL), THEN ZONE THE 1/3.

CLOUD 1/3 (SAFETY) — “TREY” — KEY 1-2. RESPONSIBLE FOR VERTICALS OF #1 & #2. VISION & SQUEEZE TECHNIQUE.

SEAM/FLAT (APEX) — “DANGER” /” FREEZE” — KEY 2-1. “MATCH-CARRY-DELIVER” RULES ON #2. #3 AWAY = TAKE #2 BACK ACROSS.

#3 RECEIVER HOOK (MIDPOINT) — “DANGER” /” FREEZE” — KEY 3-2-1. MATCH THE FINAL #3 IN THE DISTRIBUTION. TAKE #3 UP/OVER.

MIDDLE 1/3 — GENERAL — KEY 3-2-1. ZONE THE POST WITH VISION & SQUEEZE TECHNIQUE. PLAY INSIDE-OUT.

BONUS HOOK (LB/EDGE/DT) - “DANGER”/” FREEZE” — OVERDROP #3, TAKE AWAY HOT WINDOW (EDGE/DT). MATCH #3 UNDERNEATH

(LB).

SQUAT (CB) — “WILD” — FUNNEL #1 INSIDE, SINK & SEE #2/#3 FOR FLAT THREAT. NO FLAT THREAT = MAN UNDER #1. BALL BRINGS

THEM FLAT.

SMASH (CB) — “TAMPA”/” KICK” — FUNNEL #1 WIDE WITH AGGRESSION, FLIP HIPS FOR VISION, SINK & SEE #2/#3 FOR FLAT THREAT. NO

FLAT THREAT = MAN UNDER #1. BALL BRINGS THEM FLAT.

VERTICAL CURL (APEX) — “WILD” — KEY 2-1. WALL TECHNIQUE ON FINAL #2. MATCH THE IN, CARRY THE SEAM, DELIVER THE FLAT.

VERTICAL HOOK (MIDPOINT) — “WILD” (TRIPS) — KEY 3-1. WALL FINAL #3. MATCH THE IN, CARRY UP/OVER, DELIVER THE FLAT.

MIDDLE HOOK (MIDPOINT) — “WILD” — KEY 3-1. WALL FINAL #3. MATCH THE IN, CARRY UP/OVER, DELIVER THE FLAT.

DEEP 1/2 — “WILD” /”CLOUD” — KEY 1-2-3. PLAY OVER THE TOP WHILE KEYING OUTSIDE-IN TO DETERMINE WIDTH NEEDED. TOP-DOWN

VERTICALS.

TAMPA 1/2 — “TAMPA” — KEY 1-2-3. SIMILAR TO “DEEP 1/2" WITH MORE WIDTH (POST RESPONSIBILITY IS ON THE MIDDLE RUNNER).

RE-ROUTE CURL (APEX) — “TAMPA” — KEY 2-1. RE-ROUTE #2, THEN DROP TO CLOSE OFF THE CURL WINDOW WHILE READING

PATTERNS.

MIDDLE RUN — “TAMPA” — KEY 3-2-1. OPEN TO SPEED AND KEY INSIDE-OUT FOR SEAM THREATS. VS. #2/3 SEAM, CLOSE THE POST.

SOFT SQUAT (CORNER) — “READ” — KEY 2-1. #2 DETERMINES RESPONSIBILITIES. PLAYS LIKE “QUADS” UNLESS #2 IS FLAT, THEN TRAP

THE FLAT.

SOFT SQUAT (APEX) - “BOOK”/”STUMP” — KEY 3-2. #3 DETERMINES RESPONSIBILITIES. PLAYS LIKE “QUADS” UNLESS #3 IS FLAT, THEN

TRAP THE FLAT. IF #2 IS FLAT, THEY’LL LATCH ON LIKE THEY WOULD ON A QUICK DROP BY THE QB.

READ #2 (SAFETY) — “READ” — KEY 2-1. #2 DETERMINES RESPONSIBILITIES. PLAYS LIKE “QUADS” UNLESS #2 IS FLAT, THEN PUSH TO #1.

READ #3 (SAFETY) — “BOOK” /”STUMP” — KEY 3-2. #3 DETERMINES RESPONSIBILITIES. PLAYS LIKE “QUADS” UNLESS #3 IS FLAT, THEN

PUSH TO #2. IF QUICK DROP BY QB, PLAY “TOPPER” TECHNIQUE AND DRIVE ON 2"° IN/OUT BREAK.

EAD CURL (APEX) — “READ” — KEY 2-1. WALL THE FINAL #2 WITHIN THE “READ” CONCEPT.

READ HOOK (MIDPOINT) - “BOOK”/”STUMP” — KEY 3-2. WALL THE FINAL #3 WITHIN THE CONCEPT.

TRAP FLAT (CORNER) — “TRAP” — KEY 2-1. MAN-MATCH TECHNIQUE. MAN #1 UNLESS #2 IS FLAT, THEN TRAP THE FLAT.

TRAP 1/2 (SAFETY) — “TRAP” — KEY 2-1. MAN-MATCH TECHNIQUE. TOP #2 UNLESS THEY GO FLAT, THEN PUSH WIDE TO #1,

#3/HOOK (MIDPOINT) — “DANGER” — KEY 3-2-1. “COMBO” ON #3. LB TO #3 MATCHES THE ROUTE & LB AWAY ZONES THE HOOK (LIKE

“LURK”).

RAIDER (APEX) — “RAIDER” — KEY 2-1. “LURK” MAN TECHNIQUE ON #2 UNLESS THEY GO SHALLOW, THEN ZONE OFF & PATTERN READ

FOR WORK.

SINK (CB) - “BUSTER” — KEY 2-1. MATCH #1 WITH AWARENESS OF #2. IF #2 IS FLAT, OVERPLAY #1. CARRY #1 UPFIELD, POSSIBLE LATE

STEAL #2.

BUSTER (APEX) — “BUSTER” — KEY 2-3. PLAY #2 & #3 IN & OUT WITH “MIDDLE READ” DEFENDER. MATCH 1°" FLAT OR FINAL #2

UNDERNEATH.

MIDDLE READ (MIDPOINT) — “BUSTER” — KEY 3-2. PLAY #3 & #2 IN & OUT WITH “BUSTER” DEFENDER. MATCH 157 IN OR FINAL #3

UNDERNEATH.

TIGHT 1/2 (SAFETY) — “BUSTER”/” CHOKE” — KEY 2-3. “DEEP 1/2" TECHNIQUE WITHOUT HAVING TO WORRY ABOUT #1 UPFIELD.

URL-FLAT (APEX) — “TREY” — KEY 2-1. TRUE “LANDMARK-VISION-BREAK” TECHNIQUE. CURL DROPPER WITH FLAT RESPONSIBILITY.

HOOK-CURL (MIDPOINT) —- “TREY” — KEY 3-2-1. TRUE “LANDMARK-VISION-BREAK” TECHNIQUE. HOOK DROPPER THAT PUSHES WIDER

WITH #3.

WALL (APEX/MIDPOINT) — GENERAL — A HARD INSIDE-LEVERAGED “DENY THE INSIDE” TECHNIQUE IN WHICH THE DEFENDER IS

RESPONSIBLE FOR ANYTHING INSIDE & UPFIELD BY THE FINAL #2/#3 IN THE COUNT. THEY WILL ZONE OFF AND LOOK FOR A NEW #2/#3

COMING INSIDE WHEN THEIR MAN GOES FLAT. USED IN VERTICAL CURL, VERTICAL HOOK, DEUCE HOOK, READ HOOK, READ CURL, ETC.
