DESCRIPTION

Cover 9 is a 3 deep, 4 under middle-closed zone coverage with rotation away from the Nickel/Sam. It is

most commonly played with “Key & Match” zone principles in order to stay vertically sound up the seams

while also emphasizing tight matches underneath. When played to any “x2” formation, it plays as “Nine.”

Against “x1” formations, it plays as “Strap” with the weakside LB looking up the final #3 up/over.
