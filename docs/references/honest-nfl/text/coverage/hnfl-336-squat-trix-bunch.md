Pass downs from Cover 8 vs. 3x1 Bunch with multiple deep threats.

WHY?

Bunches give “Key & Match” pickups trouble due to the threat of multiple immediate exchanges.

“Squat” plays match principles w/ true zone pickups. Mike doesn’t need to take #3 up & over.

HOW?

-- Allows us to play Halves using zone distribution to match routes by area of release. Corner is a

“Squat” defender responsible for the final #1 low. Nickel is a “Vertical Curl” defender responsible for

#2 up & in. Mike is a “Middle Read” defender responsible for the final #3 underneath. Safety is a

“Deep 1/2" vertical squeeze defender relative to the formation.

-- Free Safety is a “Trix” defender keying #3-to-X. They are responsible for anything up or over by the

final #3. The rest of the backside plays out like Fire Zone would for the Corner and Dime.
