# QB_HEAD: the passer's head turns at the shared rate (attempt 1, off)

Integration's go, 2026-11-16 (Defense's zone trace: qb.ts set `a.head` to his gaze point every tick, so his head
jumped between reads in a tick and zone defenders who lean on his eyes stepped with it, 30% of their reversals). The
shared head (src/sim/head.ts, HEAD, off) skipped the passer by design ("until the QB lane moves it to want").

## What it does

- His head turns toward the point he wants to look at by head.ts's one turn (`turnHead`, extracted from `stepHeads`,
  which now calls it: HEAD on is unchanged): 6 rad/s within the shared neck range off his body facing, over the
  shoulder for a target behind. The same function whether HEAD is on or off.
- His picture is taken along where his head points, at the gaze's range (`mind.observe`, `receiverLook`); his clear
  look (`inClearLook`) is about his head.
- Defenders read `a.head` as before; it now moves at the rate.
