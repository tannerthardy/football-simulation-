# The base coverage mix, and where the passing excess lives (2026-11-11)

Report only; no engine change. Rules + Coaching, Integration item (1) after `DB_SLOTS`.

**The question.** The card showed Cover 3 at 39–46% of dropbacks against the
NFL's 29–35%, and Cover 1 at 11–14% against 20–22%. Why does the defensive
caller land there? Would more man coverage help, or is our man technique why
the learner avoids it?

**Answer.**
- **Most of the gap was the coach sample.** Every diagnostic game uses two
  fixed rosters per seed, so the card's four defences had four coordinators:
  two single-high trees, one two-high and one Tampa.
- **Over league-sampled coordinators the caller gives Cover 1 20.2%,** in range.
- **The residual is Cover 3 on 2nd and long.**
- **Man technique is not the problem.** Man is our most NFL-like coverage. The
  passing excess lives after the catch against zone.

29.7%. `EARLY_DOWN` (Cover 3 ×1.12, Cover 1 ×0.82, Cover 0 ×0.75, Cover 2 man

offence off schedule is passing.

## 2. The learner and the man technique

**The learner doesn't avoid man.** The defensive multipliers at game end are
Cover 1 ×0.87 and Cover 3 ×0.85; every family is beaten about equally.

**Man vs zone** in 32 league-sampled games (run-ref-20, seeds 101–116 and
201–216, 2,939 dropbacks, collector rows), same situation and same target class:

| | man (C0, C1, C2 man) | zone (C2, Tampa, C3, C4, C6) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| yd/dropback, 1st & 10 | 7.31 ± 0.79 (n 260) | 9.64 ± 0.41 (n 1,045) |
| yd/dropback, 2nd/3rd & 7+ | 7.59 ± 0.77 (n 224) | 9.87 ± 0.47 (n 833) |

The NFL YAC here is charted `yardageGainedAfterTheCatch`.

**Reading.**
- Completion is the same in man and zone. The difference is after the catch.
- A man defender is in phase with his receiver at the catch; our man YAC is the NFL's.
- A zone defender has to break from his landmark and rally, and ours give up about 3 yd more after the catch on every class of throw.
- Calling more man would lower yards only by hiding that, so the mix should not be forced. The lever is zone defenders' break, rally and tackle after the catch (Coverage, Defense).

## What follows

- **A flagged early-down repair:** on 2nd and 7+, no early-down tilt. It is small, and a promotion candidate on the league-sampled card.
- **A tooling fix:** diagnostic games should sample coordinators as well as alignment habits. `withIdentity` redraws habits, not coaches.
- **The zone-vs-man YAC split:** sent to Coverage and Defense as the lead on the YAC excess.
