# PA_DROP: the play-action drop by alignment (2026-11-12)

**Question** (Integration, concept queue: play action before the boot). Play action is 25.4% of NFL drop-backs and
4.0% of ours. Before raising the call rate: does our play action earn what the NFL's does, and if not, why?

## Measured (same operators both sides)

**Call rate** (`scripts/diag/concepts/pa-effect.ts`; BDB 2025 `plays.csv`):

| | Ours (develop, 32 league games) | NFL drop-backs |
|---|---|---|
| All | 4.0% | 25.4% |
| 1st down | 5.5% | 40% |
| 2nd & 1–3 / 4–6 / 7+ | 1.4 / 1.0 / 5.0% | 36 / 34 / 23% |
| 3rd & 1–3 / 4–6 / 7+ | 1.5 / 2.4 / 2.1% | 19 / 3 / 2% |
| Under center / shotgun | 4.6 / 3.7% | 83 / 15% |

Three PA concepts out of about 35 pass concepts. The caller weights PA ×2.2 under center (×1.1 gun) on downs 1–2 with
7+ to go, ×0.15 otherwise.

**Effect and the second level's response** (`pa-effect.ts` / `pa_effect_nfl.py`, traditional drops, weeks 1–3):
- **Who counts:** off-ball box defenders, 3–8 yd off the line and within 6 yd of the ball at the snap.
- **What it measures:** their depth change toward the line, and their peak downhill speed, in the first 1.0 s.

| | NFL no PA | NFL PA | Ours no PA (engine-48) | Ours PA (engine-48) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Yards a play | 5.90 | 8.26 | 8.29 | 6.72 |
| LBs downhill at 1.0 s, p50 | −0.05 yd | +0.42 yd | −0.34 yd | +0.59 yd |
| Peak downhill speed, p50 | 0.2 yd/s | 0.8 yd/s | 0.3 yd/s | 1.5 yd/s |
| Sacks | | | 3.7% | 13.4% |

Our linebackers already bite, a little more than the NFL's. Play action lost because of the drop.

**The drop** (`nfl_pa_drop.py` / `pa-drop.ts`): depth behind the snap spot, the deepest before the pass and when,
and the time to the pass.

| | NFL | Ours, off | Ours, PA_DROP on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gun PA: deepest / pass | 6.0 yd at 2.1 s / 2.3 s | 8.2 yd at 2.6 s / 3.65 s | 5.9 yd at 1.85 s / 3.3 s |
| Under PA: depth at 0.5/1.0/1.5/2.0 s | 1.9 / 3.3 / 5.3 / 7.1 | 2.0 / 2.9 / 4.2 / 6.0 | 2.0 / 3.4 / 5.3 / 7.2 |
| Under PA: deepest / pass | 8.1 yd at 2.6 s / 3.2 s | 8.2 yd at 3.2 s / 3.75 s | 8.1 yd at 3.1 s / 3.55 s |

- **Gun:** NFL gun play action is a short mesh and a shallow set, out faster than a gun drop-back (2.5 s). Ours gave
  every PA the same 0.7 s fake and 8.3 yd drop.
- **Under center:** in Singleback the back's fake point sat on the QB's drop line (0.4 yd off it). The QB dropped
  into him, slowed to about 1.2 yd/s at 0.9 s and re-started.

## Change (`PA_DROP`, off)

- **Gun play action:** a mesh, then a set at 6.0 yd (`dropSpec 'pa'`).
- **Under center:** the drop to 8.1 yd. The fake is carried on the drop at full speed, on a line 0.6 yd off the back's
  track.
- **The back's fake track:** a back aligned behind the QB fakes on a track 1.5 yd to the strong side (a zone track
  to the B gap).
- **Unchanged:** the release comes from his reads as before, so the time to the pass is a result, not an input. The
  linebackers' read is unchanged.

- **The samples are small** (about 120 PA plays per arm), and the engine-49 off arm already does better than the
  engine-48 one (9.6 against 6.7 yd a play). The forced panel agrees in direction: gun PA sacks 9 → 0 and yards a

- **The bite holds with the shorter gun mesh,** in the NFL's direction and a little strong.
- **Time to the pass is still late:** gun PA 3.3 s against the NFL's 2.3 s, with the set NFL-like. Our three PA
  concepts are post, dig and go routes, while NFL gun play action includes quick concepts. That is the concept and
  caller work, next.

## Noted, not changed

**Our non-PA drops are late by this operator.**
- Gun: deepest 7.9 yd at 2.3 s, pass at 2.95 s; NFL 7.6 yd at 2.2 s, pass at 2.5 s.
- Under center: 7.9 yd at 2.55 s, pass at 2.95 s; NFL 7.1 yd at 2.1 s, pass at 2.3 s.

Integration is recording this separately. The scorecard's time-to-throw band calls 2.75 s fine, so the two definitions
need reconciling first.

**Defense's trace of the play-action fake:** the line's feet pass-set from the first step under its low stance, so
backside ends read pass early. That is the line's technique during the look (Flex's `pa-look`); this change is the
QB's and the back's.
