## Correction and information

Use the existing cut/man technique after a confirmed `under` declaration,
observed lateral motion greater than upfield motion, and movement into the
installed low-hole area. Existing nine-yard acquisition and seven/eight-yard
depth limits remain. For the rat, the eight-yard lateral carry boundary stays
relative to the installed landmark, rather than following the defender toward
a sideline. Observed upfield declaration or departure releases the cut. No timer.

The entry cushion uses the acquired receiver position. Optional
`ManOptions.initialPosition` initializes cushion only; it is neither a new live
target nor a reason to restart the technique every tick. Existing producers
keep their previous initialization. Hidden changes cannot change the rat's
request/cushion before corresponding evidence arrives. This does **not** repair
other man constructors' live initialization, the global carrier shortcut or
the engine's wider attention/occlusion model. "Unavailable" in the focused
control means no acquired motion declaration, not a new absent-image API.

The original man does not automatically exchange or abandon his receiver.
The rat adds a called cut, not an unmodeled communication event. Post safety,
vertical responsibilities, spacing, physics, capabilities, braking, hands,
grips and release laws are unchanged. Accepted acquired-back support and throw
recognition still operate through the coverage wrapper.
