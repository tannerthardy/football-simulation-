## Small correction and why it is sound

After the existing scope/tube checks, cheap rejection requires the requested
velocity to **exactly equal the body's achieved velocity**, zero feet lock,
no push/squeeze, and settled own support: no capture deviation, lean, load,
felt force, foot velocity, fall direction or acceleration/moment filter state.
Existing eligibility already excludes active foot/strike and constrained work.
No small-angle threshold is restored.

For this unloaded state, free movement preserves the current travel line.
Facing/effort/fatigue caps can change speed along it; there is no transverse
motor or recovery demand. Project each acquired near peer onto the normal of
that line at0 and0.6s using its existing observed velocity. Only when both
endpoints have the same sign and the minimum transverse distance remains
strictly outside the ordinary physical touch radius (plus numerical1e-9) can
that peer be rejected. Linearity makes those endpoints bound its entire sweep.
The original acceleration tube already rejects other peers conservatively.

This proves a local clearance bound independent of longitudinal speed; it does
not assume the body immediately adopts a requested direction. Any mismatched
request, unsettled support, crossing/touching/unknown picture or failed proof
continues to the same World preview. It changes no body, memory, random stream,
peer acquisition or full/half/gather selection. The proof is tied to the current
free-movement law, not future unreviewed locomotion/physics experiments.
