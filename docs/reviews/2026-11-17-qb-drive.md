Correction to that measurement: our flight was first measured to the moment the ball stopped being 'air', which

## A. QB_DRIVE

Off: naturalFlightFor = 0.38 + 0.022 d + 0.00065 d^2 (d release to catch, Oct-08 BDB calibration; slow short because
d is 14-16 yd on a short throw), a 1.05x floor on his arm's minimum flight, and a 1.2x touch option over 15 yd.
NFL (BDB 2025 weeks 1-3, 2776 traditional dropbacks), flight p25/50/75 and release speed p50: 0-5 air 0.6/0.7/0.9 s,
20.7 yd/s; 5-10 0.6/0.8/1.0, 22.4; 10-15 0.8/1.0/1.3, 22.7; 15-20 1.1/1.3/1.5, 23.2; 20-30 1.4/1.8/2.1, 21.8; 30+
2.3/2.5/2.7, 21.0. Release speed is constant; depth is bought with loft (implied launch 11 to 35 deg).
On: the natural flight is the fit to those medians, T = 0.0731 + 0.03945 d + 0.00022 d^2; arm scaling as before; touch
stays his priced choice; the receivers' expectation reads the same function.
Games (8, seed 11), off / on: flight 0.85 / 0.76 s (0-5), 0.91 / 0.77 (5-10), 1.22 / 1.04 (10-15); speed 19.2 / 22.1,
19.2 / 22.2, 20.1 / 22.1; catches 439 of 649 / 463 of 678; breakups 79 / 62; ints 11 / 8; drops 29 / 39.
Staged 1536: sacks 3.5 / 3.4%, completions 944 / 946, ints 14 / 10, yd 6.80 / 7.52, time to throw 2.95 / 3.00 s.
The catch model's only speed term is +0.08 difficulty above 24 yd/s (catching.ts): **unsourced**; untouched. It is
active: 26.7% of throws under QB_DRIVE have a mean flight speed over 24 yd/s (18.8% off).
Drops 4.5 -> 5.8% (29 -> 39 of 649 / 678 throws): the catch difficulty is the ball's placement at the catch (stretch,
high, low), the 24 yd/s term, a tracking penalty only on flights over 1.4 s, and the shoulder-tracking term, which
falls with a shorter flight. So the faster ball raises it through two terms: the 24 yd/s term on more throws, and the
placement at the catch (the receiver has less flight time to close an off-target ball before it reaches him). The
closing-speed term does not act on drops. NFL check: none in this data (BDB 2025 has no drop flag; player_play has
wasTargettedReceiver and no drop); a drop rate by ball speed needs charting data.

## B. QB_RHYTHM_THROW

What forces the stop: every windup commanded a stride ramping up from zero (his drop braked out at the motion's start),
the drop itself decelerates onto the set point (his speed as the drop phase ends p50 1.44 yd/s), and the existing
rhythm start (a throw started in the drop's last step) fires only when read 1 is ready now: 8 of 99 throws started in
the drop on quick and short concepts. Built: a throw started in the drop carries the drop's velocity into the plant,
decaying to the stride by the delivery, charged by the platform terms. Effect: none measurable (no-set throws 4% -> 3%,
staged identical), because the start it acts on is rare. What would move it, not built (a second mechanism, for a
ruling): the rhythm start gated on read 1 being ready by the release (t + windup >= ready, the anticipation the throw
already prices), and the drop's last step not braked to the set point on a rhythm concept.
