## Reuse, limitations and decision

Original/intermediate requests are explicitly null. Final intents cannot reconstruct discarded requests; Flex's separately pinned stage capture is not retroactively attached to this observer. No new observer or intervention was added. Position samples are rounded.1yd, velocity.01yd/s; **no rounded-position differentiation** is used. Native event/frame times are.05s bins; contact events and sampled impulses are not exact first-contact ground truth. Acquired positions may already be projected, so cue age is not added again. Facing is not gaze; proximity is not contact. Neither these observations nor NFL tracking can identify grip force from a no-contact play. Fixed seeds do not keep draw assignments fixed once behavior diverges; only final RNG states exist here.
