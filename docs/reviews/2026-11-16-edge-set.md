# EDGE_SET: the edge widens with the reach, no faster (2026-11-16)

Defense lane, Integration item 1. This is built on END_KEY's branch (#815), with PA_HINGE underneath.
- Flag: `EDGE_SET` (on/off, default off).
- Code: `src/sim/behaviors/defense.ts`, `PassRusher.decide`, in the reach answer (`reachOut` with `EDGE_REACH`).

## What was wrong

Measured on real outside zone toward the end, with the same operator on both sides. NFL: `nfl_pa_backside.py
NFL_POP=oz`. Sim: Flex's `pa-backside-trace.mts PAB_CONCEPTS=oz_weak`.

| | widening at 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 s | within 1.5 yd of an OL |
|---|---|---|
| NFL, play-side end | 0.4–0.6 / 1.3–1.6 / 2.3–2.8 yd | 64 / 79 / 76% (gun) |
| ours | 2.7 / 3.4 / 4.6 yd | 13 / 63 / 99% |

Why ours over-widened:
- With KEY_PACE, the reach answer stepped him at the blocker's lateral speed plus 0.5 yd/s, from his own spot.
- An edge starts about 1.2 yd outside his key, so he stayed ahead of the blocker and kept gaining.
- On play action the same answer left the fake-side end 3 yd from his tackle. He was unblocked on 105 of 576 plays.

## The change

As taught: hands on the reach blocker's outside half, square; widen with him and no faster; squeeze the lane he is
trying to open.
- His lateral target is the blocker's outside shoulder in his own picture, one beat ahead.
- That target is fed forward at the blocker's lateral speed, with no lead of his own.
- When he is wider than the shoulder, he works back to it.
- When he is caught inside it (the reach gained the edge), he fights to the same shoulder instead of giving up the
  outside.
- His depth is unchanged (EDGE_UPFIELD).
- Commit rule: the target is one continuous function of the blocker in his picture, on either side of the shoulder.
  There is no side switch, so no margin is needed.
