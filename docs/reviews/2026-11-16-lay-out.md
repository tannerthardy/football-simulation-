# LAY_OUT: the moment the carrier goes by (attempt 1, a miss) (2026-11-16)

Defense lane, zone YAC (Integration priority #1). Base: engine-73 candidate e3ba53f7. Flag `LAY_OUT`, off.
Code: `src/sim/tackle.ts` (`goingBy`, `tryTackles`).

## Why
The zone YAC excess is the 20+ tail. In the trace (`scripts/diag/yac/finish-trace.mts`, develop e9023c6f), the
closest defender comes level with the carrier at 1.66 yd (p50): outside arms (1.51), inside lunge range (1.96) and
dive range (2.26). There the lunge waits for a last-reach forecast (94% of ticks), and the dive needs the carrier
separating > 1.6 yd/s with the defender behind him (met on 6%). The window lasts 0.17 s (p50).

## The change
On his own picture of the carrier's motion relative to him: if the closest point of the relative path is outside his
arms, or already behind him, he reaches now inside lunge range or lays out inside dive range. This replaces the
last-reach wait and the separating-and-behind proxy for that case. The outcome is the existing reach and dive path.

## What it points to
`scripts/diag/yac/pursuer-speed.py`, one operator, NFL vs engine-73: pursuers 2.5-6 yd from a carrier moving
> 6 yd/s, from the side or behind. Ours arrive faster (p50 6.9 vs 6.1 yd/s; closing +3.0 vs +2.1). But over the
next 0.5 s ours lose 1.1 yd/s, while the NFL's gain 0.1. With the gather withheld, the loss is −0.7. Something in the
last 2.5-6 yd takes his speed off; the gather is part of it. That is the next diagnosis, before attempt 2.
