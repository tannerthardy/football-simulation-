How a look resolves

From when his clear cone holds the man, his picture of the separation sharpens as 1 − e^(−t/τ). τ is his processing as perception, slower under pressure and when the coverage disagrees with what he expected before the snap. At a read's step he moves on only once the look clears how sure he needs to be.

What rating changes

Only τ, how sure he needs to be, and how pressure moves both. No completion, sack or interception is set by a rating; they follow from where his eyes were and what his picture said.

What to watch

Whether the three passers' eyes leave the same read at the same moment, how full the read ring is when they leave, and whether the throw comes earlier for the better reader.
