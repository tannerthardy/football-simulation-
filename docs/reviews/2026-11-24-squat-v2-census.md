## Reading

The revised sink does what it was asked to: Cover 2 and Cover 6 stop over-closing (.243 and .114 against the NFL's .177 and .096), the corner's split at #1's break and his depth at 3.0 against a vertical #1 land on the NFL's, and the 10-15 yd window is where the NFL's is. What it does not do: the corner is still 1.3-2 yd nearer to #1 at the break than the NFL's (4.2 in Cover 2, 5.3 in Cover 6), the flat goes from 2.04 to 2.88 open per game against the NFL's 1.50 (open-flat YAC 40.6 against 17.4 per game), and the throws it opens up go to the receiver who beats the hole: breakups fall and completion rises in the same direction as before. Cover 3 and Quarters at 10-15 yd are over-closed in both arms (.03 and .02-.04 against .10-.11): a default-arm fact, not the sink's.

## Caveats

- Each arm is one 24-game draw: cell differences of about 0.04 in a wide-open share, and about 1.5 points in a per-throw rate, are inside the noise.
- #1 / #2 identities and the break operator are the earlier ones (`quarters_break.py`); the corner-depth operator is new and its n is 24-204 per cell.
- Default here is engine-94 with FLIGHT_BREAK and HEAD_FOLLOW on, so the default arm's 10-15 yd cells differ from the earlier develop 0226f0d3 default (Cover 2 .433 against .494, Cover 6 .280 against .255).
