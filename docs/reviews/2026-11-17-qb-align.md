## The model

A throw is powered from the ground up. The turn into it is a throwing turn (stride, hips, shoulders) at SHOULDERS_SET's
rate. On his believed time (his pressure clock beyond the turn plus 0.25 s) he either resets (the release waits for the
turn; full throw; the shoulders and stride turning are what defenders read) or arms it (the release on the windup,
turned as far as a throwing turn gets in it). The angle still unturned at the release is thrown with less of his body:
top ball speed x (1 - 0.25 s), miss x (1 + 1.0 s), s a smoothstep of the unturned angle to 90 deg, 1.5x across the body
(a right-handed passer is assumed: a target to his left), his throw-on-the-move keeping half. Priced in his window as he
would throw it. The look-off moves his eyes only; his body stays set to the real read.

**Sources: none checked. The three numbers (25% top ball speed at 90 deg unturned, 2x miss there, 1.5x across the
body) are unsourced choices**, set by the reasoning below and checked for order only; the kinetic-chain statement
("lower body and trunk give roughly half of a throw's speed") is general biomechanics knowledge, not a verified citation.
They are to be replaced by measured or cited values when a source is found. Handedness: the player model has no
handedness field at all (every passer is treated as right-handed); left-handers are not represented, not ignored by a
rule.

Coefficients are chosen, checked for order only (set and aligned < stepping open < arm throw off-platform < across the
body): the kinetic chain's lower body and trunk give roughly half of a throw's speed, an arm throw keeps the shoulder and
arm, so a quarter of the top speed is lost at 90 deg unturned; the miss doubles there; across the body is worse than the
open side.

One platform model: THROW_PLATFORM prices the release's translation (his velocity along and across the target line);
QB_ALIGN prices the trunk's rotation; both act on the same release (its miss and its arm speed). The gather (QB_GATHER_THROW)
and QB_FOOTWORK's steps and shoulders-on-a-step set where his trunk faces before the decision; QB_ALIGN starts from
that facing.
