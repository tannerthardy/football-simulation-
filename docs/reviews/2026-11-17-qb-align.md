# QB_ALIGN: the throw's body alignment (attempt 1, off)

The user via Integration, 2026-11-17: zone defenders now read the quarterback before the throw (eyes, then shoulders
and front foot). If his eyes and body point one way and he throws the other, that should cost him power and accuracy.

## Diagnosis (develop 2f5315d9, engine-74; scripts/diag/eyes/release-align.ts, 307 staged throws)

- His trunk off the target line at the decision: p50 29 deg, p90 79; over 45 deg 42%, over 90 deg 4%.
- At the release: p50 1 deg, p90 9; never over 45. The turn was the body's free yaw inside the 0.4 s windup.
- The windup did not lengthen with the turn (0.53 s at 0-15 deg, 0.43 s at 45-90), and the release error (1.04 / 0.94 /
  1.05 / 0.71 yd by bin) and the ball's pace (0.050-0.055 s a yard) did not change: no time, speed or miss charged.
- No foot exists in the production body; his motion at the release stands for the stride (off the line p90 9 deg).
- SHOULDERS_SET (off) has a throwing turn fitted to the NFL turn into the throw (TURN_BASE + A / TURN_RATE, qb-release.py:
  15-45 deg 0.3 s, 45-90 0.5 s, 90-180 0.7 s), but no choice to throw without it and no cost for an unturned angle.

## The model

A throw is powered from the ground up. The turn into it is a throwing turn (stride, hips, shoulders) at SHOULDERS_SET's
rate. On his believed time (his pressure clock beyond the turn plus 0.25 s) he either resets (the release waits for the
turn; full throw; the shoulders and stride turning are what defenders read) or arms it (the release on the windup,
turned as far as a throwing turn gets in it). The angle still unturned at the release is thrown with less of his body:
top ball speed x (1 - 0.25 s), miss x (1 + 1.0 s), s a smoothstep of the unturned angle to 90 deg, 1.5x across the body
(a right-handed passer is assumed: a target to his left), his throw-on-the-move keeping half. Priced in his window as he
would throw it. The look-off moves his eyes only; his body stays set to the real read.

**Sources: none checked. The three numbers (25% top ball speed at 90 deg unturned, 2x miss there, 1.5x across the
body) are unsourced choices**, set by the reasoning below and checked for order only; the kinetic-chain statement
("lower body and trunk give roughly half of a throw's speed") is general biomechanics knowledge, not a verified citation.
They are to be replaced by measured or cited values when a source is found. Handedness: the player model has no
handedness field at all (every passer is treated as right-handed); left-handers are not represented, not ignored by a
rule.

Coefficients are chosen, checked for order only (set and aligned < stepping open < arm throw off-platform < across the
body): the kinetic chain's lower body and trunk give roughly half of a throw's speed, an arm throw keeps the shoulder and
arm, so a quarter of the top speed is lost at 90 deg unturned; the miss doubles there; across the body is worse than the
open side.

One platform model: THROW_PLATFORM prices the release's translation (his velocity along and across the target line);
QB_ALIGN prices the trunk's rotation; both act on the same release (its miss and its arm speed). The gather (QB_GATHER_THROW)
and QB_FOOTWORK's steps and shoulders-on-a-step set where his trunk faces before the decision; QB_ALIGN starts from
that facing.

## QB_BODY_FOLLOW (attempt 1, off; Integration with Defense's shoulder read, 2026-11-17)

Measured first (scripts/diag/eyes/body-follow.ts, develop 69277a00, 10 concepts x 4 coverages x 2 rosters x 2 seeds):
on the default engine his body already follows his read. QB_FOOTWORK defaults to 3 since engine-73, and bit 2 sets his
shoulders to the read when a footwork step lands, instantly (free yaw): trunk off a new read at the change p50 21 deg,
0.2 s after 15; within 15 deg p50 0.00 s, p90 0.35; 90 of 386 eye changes moved on before the body arrived. Trunk off
the eventual target 0.5 s before the release p50 36 deg, 0.25 s before 7 (the windup's free turn).
Built: the reset to a new read (bit 2's shoulder read, or his eyes without bit 2) starts TURN_BASE after it and turns at
the throwing-turn rate; a look-off keeps the body on the real read. With QB_ALIGN, without / with QB_BODY_FOLLOW: trunk
off the new read 0.2 s after p50 14 / 22 deg; eye changes moved on before the body arrived 95 / 138; at the decision
p50 27 / 31 deg, over 45 deg 38 / 40%; at the release p50 7 / 10; staged matched 598 plays sacks 5.0 / 4.7%,
completions 368 / 366, ints 3 / 2. So the body following the read is not what was missing: it follows on each step
already, and making the reset take its real time makes the shoulders lag the reads more. Defense's shoulders on the
eventual catcher (14-25%) reflect reads moving faster than steps (the throw goes to a read the body has not reached),
which QB_ALIGN prices as a reset or an arm throw. Patch:
docs/reviews/patches/engine71-footwork/qb-align-body-follow-after-rhythm-on-69277a00.patch (QB_ALIGN and QB_BODY_FOLLOW,
after the QB_DRIVE and rhythm patches on develop 69277a00).
