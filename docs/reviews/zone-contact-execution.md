## The demonstrated mechanism

The existing zone reader chooses **reach**, **runoff** or **seal** from the
blocker's acquired hip picture and estimated movement capability. Runoff means
the outside shoulder is not presently attainable; its selected drive includes
carrying the defender laterally. That interpretation is held on the existing
decision cadence. It is not permission to declare a successful block.

At loaded contact, `RUN_CONTACT_COMPONENTS` correctly removes the drive's
tangential component to avoid counting it twice. But the remaining tangent
request only follows the defender and corrects the seal offset. It never
realizes the selected runoff drive. In the saved strong RG/DT_S encounter,
the read still selects drive `(0.673,−0.740)` while the final sideways request
falls near 0.5–0.9 yd/s at 1.20–1.30 seconds. Some further loss is teammate
spacing; actual body acceleration is a third, separate stage.

The correction projects the **same held runoff drive** into the unit contact
tangent and realizes it at the existing 2.4 yd/s rep tempo. This is a bounded
realization of a selected **direction**, not restoration of a previously stored
speed. The added contribution is capped by the existing mirror bound and
combined with co-flow by `max`, not addition. Existing co-flow may itself
exceed that bound, as before. Normal/anchor commands are not rewritten.
Additional lateral work still spends the same traction/support budget;
unchanged rules do not guarantee unchanged normal protection.

The contribution requires component mode, a current solo reach responsibility,
the same acquired defender, ownership of the real contact, no helpers and no
unreleased combination. Reach and seal add no runoff work; a normal technique
read ends it. Legacy component-off composition already contains the drive
tangent and stays unchanged. The parked no-argument reach-track hook also
retains its prior behavior. No new timer, information, speed capability,
strength, grip, release gate, assignment or contact law is supplied.
