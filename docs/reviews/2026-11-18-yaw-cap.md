# YAW_CAP: the hips' yaw rate as a measured capacity (stage b of the facing design, flag off)

Branch `tools/yaw-cap` off develop 7a157055 (engine-91). Design: `2026-11-18-facing-as-state.md`. One parameter, `YAW_CAP_PCT` (default 55): the profile's `turnRate` (already derived from the body's cone/shuttle score) is scaled by it. `hipYawRate(profile)` is read by the body's yaw motor (`turnRateOf`), the coverage flip-time estimates (`defense.ts`, `techniques.ts`) and the engaged hips' yaw bound (`engagement.ts`), so a controller's idea of his own flip time matches his body. A body capacity from direct measurement, a stated and labeled choice; no outcome is fitted. Off is byte-identical to develop (seed 101, one game, all 22 players' tracks, `cmp` on the export).

## Why 55: the measured rate

Orientation yaw rate (|o[k+1] - o[k]| / 0.1 s), open movement (no one within 1.5 yd), one operator for NFL (BDB 2025 weeks 1-9 dropbacks) and the sim (`scripts/diag/body/yaw_rate.py`; 8 games seed 101 each arm). deg/s, p90 / p99:

| speed bin | NFL receivers | sim hips off | sim hips 55 | NFL defenders | sim hips off | sim hips 55 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.5-3 | 94 / 245 | 127 / 477 | 142 / 307 | 82 / 200 | 119 / 279 | 120 / 218 |
| 3-5 | 131 / 304 | 128 / 379 | 137 / 289 | 89 / 210 | 191 / 287 | 139 / 232 |
| 5-7 | 116 / 248 | 89 / 266 | 90 / 242 | 88 / 216 | 220 / 281 | 140 / 165 |
| 7+ | 97 / 199 | 86 / 240 | 92 / 211 | 69 / 147 | 186 / 225 | 105 / 205 |

The cap does what it should where the sim was off: defenders' p90 at 3-7 yd/s drops from 191-220 to 139-140 (NFL 88-89) and receivers' slow-speed tail from 477 to 307. The shoulders (`o` in the sim) still show receivers' p99 near 560-590 deg/s against NFL 199-304: that is the trunk (TRUNK_RATE 360 deg/s riding on the hips), not the hips, and is untouched here (stage c / the facing-intent stage).

## Controls (before any staged outcomes)

1. **Flip bench** (lone CB, 12 profiles, medians; backpedal 3 yd/s for 1.0 s, hips turn into a run on the same line; speed at 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 / 1.0 s): off 3.77 / 4.83 / 5.73 / 6.47; PCT 75 3.73 / 4.73 / 5.65 / 6.40; **PCT 55 3.67 / 4.54 / 5.49 / 6.27**; PCT 45 3.65 / 4.42 / 5.33 / 6.14. 45 deg off the line: PCT 55 3.73 / 4.72 / 5.61 / 6.36 (off 3.79 / 4.77 / 5.65 / 6.39); 90 deg off: within 0.03 (PCT 55 2.88 / 3.58 / 4.52 / 5.38; off 2.91 / 3.60 / 4.54 / 5.39). A planted 180 deg turn takes 0.45 s off and 0.65 s at 55. The cost of the cap is 0.1-0.3 yd/s for under a second in a full reversal and nothing in the 45-90 deg cases.
2. **Turn-cost table** (speed change over 0.5 s by entry speed x heading rate, open field, `turn_cost.py`, 8 games each arm): the cap moves no cell beyond noise. 60-100 deg/s: 4-5 yd/s -0.28 (off) vs -0.33 (on) against NFL -0.27; 5-6 -0.67 / -0.67 (NFL -0.52); 6-7 -0.86 / -0.91 (NFL -0.63). 100+ deg/s at 4-5: -1.40 / -1.31 (NFL -1.18). The sim's pre-existing gap to the NFL (a positive speed gain in slow turns, a deeper loss in the 6-8 yd/s hard turns) is the same with the cap on or off: it is the movement law's, not the hips'.
3. **Route-break speed** (`route_break.py`, the NFL's operator, 80 reps x dig/short_in/cross/go vs cover 3, ball held): dig min/in 0.77 and 0.77, v_out 5.4 and 5.4; short_in 0.83 / 0.84 and 5.0 / 5.2; cross 1.06 / 1.06 (NFL IN 0.80, v_in 5.6, v_min 4.5). No route-break row changes: a route runner leads his break (ROUTE_FILLET, travel +-60 deg `FACING_LEAD`), so the cap rarely binds him.
4. **Planted turns** (defenders whose travel turns 90-130 / 110-156 deg off their facing from a 2-4 yd/s start, `facing_speed.py` part 2; n 400 / 283 at 55): speed at +0.8 s 3.80 / 3.70 (NFL 3.79 / 3.69), and the dip is the same as off (2.52 / 2.38 at 55 vs 2.55 / 2.38 off; NFL 2.73 / 2.79). The cap does not lose speed the NFL does not show. The facing turned at +0.4 s drops from 83 / 103 deg off to 66 / 82 with the cap (NFL 35 / 41): still about twice the NFL's, and the ~0.3 yd/s deeper dip against the NFL at +0.2-0.4 s is not the hips'; both are open and belong to the facing-intent stage, not to a lower cap. I did not relax the planted case: nothing in it needed relaxing.

## Staged outcomes (paired, YAW_CAP off -> 1, PCT 55; `start-staged.ts` + `start-staged-report.py`)

| set | plays | complete | sacked | pressured | PBU | INT | yards/play (paired se) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| zone: 12 concepts x 5 coverages x 2 formations x 3 rosters x 2 seeds | 720 | .646 -> .649 | .026 -> .017 (se .005) | .163 -> .165 | .087 -> .100 | .014 -> .010 | 8.68 -> 9.33 (.36) |
| man: 10 concepts x 5 man coverages x 2 formations x 2 rosters x 2 seeds | 400 | .505 -> .492 | .035 -> .072 (se .014) | .198 -> .200 | .165 -> .165 | .010 -> .010 | 5.46 -> 4.93 (.49) |
| rush: 6 concepts x cover3/1/0 x 2 formations x 6 rosters x 3 seeds | 648 | .449 -> .400 (se .016) | .057 -> .057 | .284 -> .299 | .162 -> .191 | 0 -> .017 | 5.84 -> 5.46 (.34) |

Man-to-target separation at 1.0 / 2.0 s moves by under 0.05 yd in all three sets (rush 3.044 -> 3.028 at 2.0 s). Dither (`test/dither-budget.test.ts`, YAW_CAP=1): passes, as with the flag off.

**Man sacks (.035 -> .072) are not a coverage effect.** Of the 31 discordant plays, 23 sack only with the cap on. Sixteen of the twenty sackers I traced are the same DE_S, and the plays repeat across the five coverages because the four-man rush does not depend on coverage. Counted as independent (formation, roster, seed) cells, it is 5 cells that sack only on and 6 that sack only off. The rush set, where the same rushers recur, is balanced (15 plays each way; 7 vs 10 cells).

**Rush completion (.449 -> .400) is real and is the defense's hips.** Capping only the offense's hips leaves completion at .448 (-.002); capping only the defense's gives .386 (-.063, PBU +.035, INT +.015). Leaving the defenders' own flip-time estimates at the old rate (the controller unaware of the cap) gives the same -.051, so it is the body's capacity and not the controller's estimate. The first body to diverge between arms is a CB, LB, FS or the matched X, at 1.0-1.6 s, not the QB, line or blockers (125 outcome-changed plays). Defenders are not closer or farther on average (separation unchanged), but at the throw more of them are facing the passer: DB and LB hips within 60 degrees of the bearing to the QB, 0.433 -> 0.477 in the rush set (216 plays). In the NFL-operator view (shoulders at the throw frame, DB/LB/S, all pass plays) that share is NFL 0.684, sim off 0.724 and 0.764 with the cap, so the sim's defenders already face the passer more than the NFL's do (median angle NFL 40 degrees, sim 21, capped 18) and the cap moves that further from the NFL. QB reads treat a defender facing him as one who sees the throw (qb.ts, THROW_REACT / `sawThrow`); the earlier break would explain the PBU and INT rise. That link is consistent with the numbers but I did not isolate it directly.

So the cap does not create the excess; it lowers the rate at which a defender's hips can leave the QB, and the sim's defenders did not leave him enough to begin with. A lower hip rate for defenders cannot be tuned away. What the NFL defender does while turning belongs to the facing-intent stage (a) and Coverage's MAN_TURN, not to the cap's value.

## What this does not claim

PCT 55 is chosen from the table above (the sim's p99 lands inside the NFL's range for receivers and defenders in all four bins); 45 and 75 are the sensitivity handles. The NFL `o` is a 10 Hz sensor estimate, so its tail is a floor on hip peaks; a body that really turns faster for a tenth of a second is under-read there and the 55 may be a little low.
