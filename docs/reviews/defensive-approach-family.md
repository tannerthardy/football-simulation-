## Forecast versus achieved approach

Same-state forecasts were promising: A020 at 4.05 s improved minimum predicted
separation **1.585→1.324 yd**, and A042 at 2.65 s **1.701→1.442 yd**. Both forecasts
remain upright. A020's earlier first intervention at 3.75 s improves a larger
miss **3.296→3.037 yd**; neither option predicts contact within that horizon.
The selector can therefore reward a shorter miss without establishing a useful
entry opportunity.

At each native first intervention, the incoming defender body and consumed cues
are exactly equal across arms. The alternative's first **50 ms position and
velocity exactly match** World execution. Subsequent requests are replanned:

- A020's gather weight changes **1→0.465→0→0→0.064** over 3.75–3.95 s. The
  controller blends into another approach objective. At the original forecast
  boundary, actual defender position differs from the held-command prediction by
  0.617 yd and velocity by 2.990 yd/s.
- A042's gather stays 1 over 2.65–2.85 s. Updated pictures and repeated scoring
  select ordinary gather again after the initial alternatives. Its boundary
  differences are 0.584 yd and 2.245 yd/s. This is not A020's mode transition.

These are limitations of scoring one held request as a proxy for a sequence of
decisions. First-step execution provides no numerical discrepancy to repair.
The runner's recorded state remains unchanged until A020 4.60 s / A042 3.35 s,
at or after first grasp; a new offensive decision before contact does not explain
the trial's entry result. Later contact/execution differences are coupled effects.

## Decision and smallest next experiment

Do not promote this trial. It supplies neither useful achieved entry improvement
on the witnesses nor a transferred approach benefit. A042's favorable support
and runner fall remain visible; they do not rescue that narrower mechanism claim.
No additional passive force or execution recording would change this decision.

The next discriminating experiment is a constant-motion acquired-picture encounter
that evaluates the **sequence of replanned requests**, using one coherent approach
objective across gather and interception. First prove achieved position, velocity
and leverage, then reuse these native witnesses. It must retain attainable ball
contests, useful close wraps, force leverage, stale-picture uncertainty and valid
last reaches. A persistence timer or stronger grip would not address the demonstrated
forecast limitation. This is a recommended next experiment, not another behavior
change started here. Shared spacing and physical execution remain with their owners.
