# OPEN_TOP: the deep defender's open-field approach (attempt 1, a miss) (2026-11-17)

Defense lane, zone YAC tail. Base: develop 6f1136ae. Flag `OPEN_TOP`, off.
Code: `src/sim/open-field.ts` (`openTopVelocity`), `src/sim/behaviors/common.ts` (`chase`).

## Diagnosis it builds on
67 never-reached zone completions (`scripts/diag/yac/tail-film.mts`, `tail-classify.py`).
- The nearest deep defender (NB/SS/FS, ~9 yd off, ~5.5 yd deeper) charges the catch at once.
- He goes into the close gather, and the runner passes him 1.5-2.5 yd to his side: 61% of the tail yards.
- On NFL 20+ zone plays a deeper defender gives ground in front for > 1 s on 50% of plays; ours on 6% (`mirror.py`).

Why OPEN_FIELD's on-top did not do it (tail-film mode tags):
- It runs on 56-61% of chase ticks at 5-12 yd, but its point is at most 3 yd ahead of the runner, so he keeps coming downhill.
- The gather takes 20% of ticks at 2.5-5 yd and all of them inside 2.5 yd.

## The change
As taught (handbook defense-and-perception §10; WR_DB_Mechanics_Textbook §16). Phases:
- close: at full speed to the on-top point;
- balance / give: from his braking distance at the closing speed he sees (plus one reaction time and contact), give
  ground at the runner's downfield pace while squeezing the lateral gap at 2.0/s and the depth gap at 1.0/s;
- strike: through the near hip inside lunge range, or once the runner commits past his leverage.
Margins: the give phase is left only 1 yd beyond its entry; the strike holds. A first build let "past his leverage"
fire before he had taken any leverage and sent him straight to the strike; fixed (only from the give phase) before
measuring.

## Why it missed
- He enters "give" only about 3 yd from the runner (p50) and stays 0.3 s.
- In it his downfield speed is −0.18 yd/s against the runner's 2.69: he never actually gives ground.
- The balance distance from the profile's braking figure is far shorter than the distance in which his body can stop a
  full-speed charge and turn to run with the runner. He arrives still charging, and the strike fires at 1.8 yd.
- Next (attempt 2, if taken): set the balance distance from the deceleration and turn he can actually achieve (the same
  physics the gather uses, followAccel), so the coming-to-balance starts where the NFL defender's does (about 9 yd).
