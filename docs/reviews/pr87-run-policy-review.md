## Policy decision

The demonstrated football defect is a missing feasible combined response to
crossing teammate traffic. A newly requested direction does not erase incoming
momentum. Full, half and gather can all intersect the same teammate; adding a
transverse correction without preserving useful braking can make that worse.

The submitted policy retains the native spacing/gather choice, then considers
one acquired-peer transverse adjustment and, if necessary during overlap, its
existing half-speed composition. It is limited to man/zone controllers with
an explicit own objective and known acquired peer pictures. It changes no
assignment, receiver responsibility or information source.

I checked selection against the actual achievable World forecast, rather than
comparing an entering velocity to an unexecuted new command:

- Own support, momentum, facing, posture, effort and other action fields remain.
  The new request is capped at original requested speed and uses ordinary field
  containment. Down, free, dive/lunge, active squeeze/foot/strike, engagement,
  loaded support and tactile constraints decline the free alternative.
- Missing/nonfinite own motion or peer position/velocity/age declines the new
  option; negative ages also decline. No live peer pose/velocity or behavior
  query is substituted. Finite delayed pictures remain conditional estimates;
  their ages are neither refreshed nor projected twice.
- Each evaluated path remains upright, makes positive progress in the own
  maneuver frame and creates no newly predicted overlap with a previously clear
  peer. If native movement overlaps, require material clearance relief and
  progress toward the original goal relative to standing. If native movement
  clears, require a clear path and better goal progress than that native choice.
  Free previews remain conditional on acquired teammate motion; they do not
  predict future opponent impacts or other players' next decisions.
- Clearance and task progress are separate. A better local request can still
  collide on a genuinely blocked route. The rule does not award clearance,
  perfect coverage, equal lateral movement or a required football ending.
- Retained passing state is an own maneuver frame, not remembered peer motion.
  Each evaluation consumes the current acquired picture and current command's
  goal. Passing behind or turning away ends that frame. The score is local,
  not a globally optimal coverage planner.
