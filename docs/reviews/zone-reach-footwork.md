# Zone reach footwork: mechanism supported, promotion HOLD

The acquired runoff sometimes loses its own playside racing intention at
contact: feet merely match the defender even while the installed job calls for
working him wide. A small feet-only correction produces actual lateral progress
under loaded hands. It does **not** establish a complete wide-zone improvement:
the strong LG gives up depth, and the wider weak-side LT creates runner traffic.
Keep those adverse outcomes, review this boundary, and hold adoption.

## Decision and physical result

RunBlocker publishes an optional **own acquired footstep goal**, only for its
existing eligible solo acquired runoff against the same target. The existing
phase reader, observation cadence and shoulder decision are unchanged. Reach,
seal, live combinations, helpers and other occupied joints keep the old policy.
There is no new perception read, private opponent instruction or future result.

The held goal uses the existing free-track tempo `.45 * own vmax`, bounded by
the existing contact mirror allowance. After the ordinary contact calculation,
it supplies a world-playside Y request while retaining that calculation's X
request. This intentionally can change a diagonal frame's normal component;
it is not a claim to preserve every contact-frame component. The unchanged
lost-depth anchor may override it. World movement, balance, contact forces and
one shared traction budget determine what is achieved. No hand setting or
defender request is changed by this hook.

The first native command difference is at pre-step0.70s/post-step0.75s, with all
22 pre-step bodies identical. LG asks X .362767 in both arms; Y changes from
-.706609 to -2.618230. The matched mirrored unit controls also compare the
same complete initial state and real hand settings, then step the real World:
the new request produces greater lateral speed with unchanged hand capacities,
no direct body edits, bounded arm impulses and negligible work residual.
Hidden defender velocity/down changes do not change the cached footstep goal
or produce extra see calls. This protects the **new hook**; it does not certify
all historical information use in the contact controller.

## Adverse results and the first remaining causes

LG already requests substantial playside speed on much of the accepted tape.
More requested width cannot by itself repair a loaded body's limited movement.
OZ LG loses .388yd more depth at2s and .563yd at3s, for only .037/.077yd more
width. Strong WZ LG loses .382/.541yd depth and actually has .047/.193yd less
width. The new floor changes hand/contact response before the unchanged anchor
engages; preserving requested X is not preserving achieved X. This is a material
cost, not a proof that the World needs stronger legs or weaker defenders.

One short matched diagnostic settles the weak-WZ slowdown's producer. It wraps
existing behavior returns/ordinary see calls for observation only, with zero
extra reads, draws or commands. All53 ticks exactly reproduce the saved commands
and all22 bodies in each arm. At1.90s RB's own position, velocity and football
request are identical: request(4.681847,3.884583). Accepted spacing leaves it;
candidate spacing halves it, then requests zero at2.40-2.60s. The acquired LT
picture is legitimate and .05s old: (39.943,31.691), velocity(.214,2.158), versus
accepted (40.075,31.222), velocity(.501,1.396). No announced called-motion
substitute is applied to that peer. The blocker really changed the approach
geometry; missing perception is not demonstrated here. At3s RB X41.998 ->
39.742, a2.256yd delay. The diagnostic identifies a restrictive spacing response,
but has **not** established that it is unnecessary or shown an achievable better
RB option. Neither collision deletion nor moving the LT out of the way is valid.

Next bounded decision: at the first loaded LG runoff transition, compare the
same physical state's playside step with its existing brace/depth response;
determine what useful width is achievable without the demonstrated depth loss.
Keep the weak LT/RB encounter as the traffic control and separately assess any
runner adjustment against its real acquired picture/momentum. Do not tune this
floor, force all seven blockers flat, or begin a broad runner-navigation project.
