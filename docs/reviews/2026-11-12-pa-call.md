# PA_CALL: play action as a tag on the call (2026-11-12)

**Question** (Integration, after PA_DROP). Play action is 25.4% of NFL drop-backs and 4% of ours. With the drop fixed,
our three play-action concepts earned 12 yd a play against 7.3 without, about twice the NFL's premium. Why, and how
should play action be called?

## What NFL play action throws (BDB 2025 plays.csv, traditional drops)

| | Air yards p50 | Under 5 / 5–15 / 15+ | Time to throw | Yd/play |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gun PA (807) | 4 | 53 / 28 / 19% | 2.3 s | 7.74 |
| Gun no PA (4,948) | 5 | 45 / 38 / 17% | 2.5 s | 5.85 |
| Under PA (913) | 9 | 42 / 25 / 32% | 3.1 s | 8.41 |
| Under no PA (1,481) | 5 | 49 / 35 / 16% | 2.3 s | 5.93 |

- **Gun play action is mostly the quick game with a mesh in front.**
- **Play action is an action put on a concept, not a family of its own.** Ours had three deep PA-only concepts.

**The NFL's PA rate on drop-backs, by situation and alignment** (`PA_TAG_RATE`):

| | 1st | 2nd short / medium / long | 3rd short / medium / long | 4th |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gun | 24% | 23 / 21 / 16% | 11 / 3 / 2% | 6% |
| Under center or pistol | 81% | 75 / 79 / 77% | 79 / 86 (n 7) / 47% (n 17) | 73% |

The two small under-center cells are shrunk to 50% and 47%.

## Change (`PA_CALL`, off; use with PA_DROP)

`PlayCaller.playActionTag`: after the concept is chosen, the caller puts the run action on it at the NFL's rate for the
down and distance and the QB's alignment.
- **Eligible:** quick and drop-back concepts, from either alignment. Screens and empty sets are never tagged, and the
  three PA concepts already carry it.
- **The tagged call** is the same concept with the fake (the back's track, the line's look, PA_DROP's drop by
  alignment).
- **Drawn on its own stream,** so the rest of the call is unchanged.
- **Situation and alignment only;** team tendency comes later.

## Read: league games, engine-49, seeds 101/201/301/401, 8 games each, PA_DROP on

| | Tag off | Tag on | NFL |
|---|---|---|---|
| PA share of drop-backs: all / gun / under | 4.4 / 3.7 / 6.3% | 28.4 / 15.1 / 63.7% | 25.4 / 15 / ~80% |
| Gun: PA / no PA yd/play | 11.59 / 7.63 | 9.78 / 7.80 | 7.37 / 5.83 |
| Under: PA / no PA yd/play | 12.66 / 6.49 | 9.08 / 6.65 | 8.90 / 6.65 |
| Premium: gun / under | +4.0 / +6.2 | +2.0 / +2.4 | +1.5 / +2.25 |
| All drop-backs: yd/play / sacks | 7.53 / 4.5% | 8.12 / 4.6% | |

- **The premium is in the NFL's range.** The earlier over-worth was the deep-only PA menu; tagged play action is
  mostly ordinary concepts behind a fake.
- **The cost, plainly:** calling play action a quarter of the time adds yards to a passing game that is already high.
  - On these unpaired league games, all drop-backs go from 7.53 to 8.12 yd a play (+0.6).
  - An earlier run of the same flag, before quick concepts were eligible under center, gave +0.32.
  - The scoreboard will move with it. The base drop-back yardage gap (our gun non-PA 7.8 against the NFL's 5.8) is the
    larger problem and is outside play action.

## The second level and the safeties

`pa-levels.ts` / `pa_levels_nfl.py`, same operator:
- **LBs:** off-ball box defenders, 3–8 yd off the ball and within 6 yd of it at the snap.
- **Safeties:** 9+ yd deep at the snap.
- **Rushers excluded:** anyone within 1 yd of the line before the throw.

| | LBs downhill 0.3+ | Bite p50 | Turned back p50 | Depth change at 2.0 s / at the throw |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NFL gun PA | 66% | 1.24 yd | 1.9 s | −0.50 / −0.08 yd |
| Ours gun PA | 69% | 1.43 yd | 1.7 s | −0.50 / +0.51 yd |
| NFL under PA | 68% | 1.21 yd | 1.9 s | −0.15 / +2.29 yd (throw at 3.2 s) |
| Ours under PA | 69% | 1.60 yd | 1.85 s | −0.68 / +0.90 yd (throw at 2.9 s) |

| Safeties | Downhill 0.3+, no PA / PA | Depth change at the throw, no PA / PA |
|---|---|---|
| NFL gun | 17 / 20% | +4.05 / +3.09 yd |
| Ours gun | 32 / 30% | +2.45 / +2.58 yd |
| NFL under | 13 / 12% | +3.29 / +7.58 yd |
| Ours under | 41 / 51% | +1.93 / +1.54 yd |

**Reading:**
- **Our linebackers bite as often as the NFL's,** a little farther (1.4–1.6 yd against 1.2), and turn back on time.
- **Under center they are still 0.68 yd downhill at 2.0 s,** against the NFL's 0.15. That slower recovery from a
  bigger bite is the under-center part of the premium.
- **Our safeties come downhill far more than the NFL's on every play** (30–51% against 12–20%), and play action barely
  changes it. On under-center play action the NFL's safeties bail (+7.6 yd at the throw, against deep shots: 32% of
  throws 15+); ours don't (+1.5).
- **Both safety gaps are coverage behaviour across the board,** not play action's. They are for Coverage.
- **Air yards:** our menu is intermediate-heavy (51–56% of throws at 5–15 yd against the NFL's 24–39%), with fewer
  deep shots under center (15% against 32%), and YAC is high everywhere (gun no PA 6.5 against 4.8). Known gaps.

## Seam left for later (not built)

The owner wants to stress-test analytics claims once the sim is trustworthy: play action without establishing the
run, never running on 2nd and long. Those need the defense and its caller to update on this opponent's tendencies
across plays. Today a defender's run/pass prior is league-wide by situation, and the bite depends only on the look in
front of him.

`playActionTag` takes the situation and alignment only. A team-tendency term (its run rate, how recently it ran) is
where the offense's side of that would plug in. The defense's side is the recognition prior. Neither is built.

## For Defense

On under-center play action our linebackers are still 0.68 yd downhill at 2.0 s against the NFL's 0.15. They recover
+0.9 yd by the throw against the NFL's +2.3. That slower recovery from a slightly bigger bite (1.6 against 1.2 yd) is
the part of the under-center PA premium that sits above the NFL's. Not a blocker for PA_CALL.
