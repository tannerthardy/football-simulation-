# The passer's picture of perimeter defenders: age and occlusion in games (Tools/Physics, 2026-11-24)

QB lane's finding (QB_V2 harness, gun_trips_11, seed 19): the passer's head is within 3 degrees of the flat defender he keys for 0.75 s, yet `sim.see(qb, CB_S)` returns a picture 1.05-1.45 s old, refreshed once. Integration's ask: stage it, per tick the picture age and which bodies occlude; is a passer's view of a perimeter defender blocked through the line?
Measured on the **default engine** (develop `c7d4f2bf6`+, no QB_V2), `scripts/diag/perception/qb-sight.mts` (a read-only tap on `PlaySim.tick`: it asks `sim.see(qb, d)` for every CB / NB / S / LB each tick from 0.3 s to the release and tests `lineOfSight`; it advances only the per-tick visibility memo), 8 games (4 seeds x 2), 698 non-screen pass plays, 253,557 defender-ticks (`data/reviews/perception/qb-sight-default-engine.md`).

## What the model does

`sighted()` (SIGHT_CARRIER, SIGHT_PURE) gives the passer **the newest moment, at least his delay back, at which he had the man in sight**; `lineOfSight` is one ray from his eye (0.936 of his stature, lowered by his posture) to the target's **eye point** (one point: 0.936 of his stature), blocked by any other body whose torso cylinder (the body's radius, up to his shoulder height 0.818) or head cylinder (`HEAD_RADIUS` 0.125 yd = 9 in, up to his stature) crosses it.

## Results (perimeter defenders: CB and nickel at least 6 yd wide of the passer and 0-7 yd deep, 0.8-2.4 s)

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| picture age, all CB (1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 s) p25 / p50 / p75 | 0.20 / 0.25 / 0.30; 0.20 / 0.25 / 0.40; 0.20 / 0.25 / 0.75 |
| picture age, perimeter, line clear now: p50 / p75 / p90 | 0.25 / 0.30 / 0.60 s |
| picture age, perimeter, line blocked now: p50 / p75 / p90 | 0.25 / 0.40 / 0.70 s |
| share of perimeter ticks with the ray blocked | **0.18** (all defenders 0.15) |
| length of a blocked run (consecutive ticks): p50 / p90 / max | 0.10 / 0.30 / **1.10 s** |
| play-defenders with a blocked run of 0.5 s or more / 0.95 s or more | 69 / 6 of 1,446 perimeter play-defenders (4.8% / 0.4%); 1,039 were ever blocked |

**Who blocks: only heads, never torsos.** Counting blocked ticks by the blocking body: RT head 1,292, LT head 1,239, DE_S head 683, DE_W head 565, Y head 487, RB head 454, H head 443, X 177, Z 165, RG 111, LG 100 (the long runs of 0.5 s or more: RT 22, LT 21, DE_S 9). A passer's eyes (1.95 yd for a 6'3" man)
are above every lineman's shoulder in his set (1.4-1.7 yd), so no torso blocks; a **9-inch helmet cylinder on the line to a wide corner** does. The QB lane's guess that "the trips receivers and the nickel are between them, it may be the line's heights" is half right: it is the tackles' and the ends' helmets, and the backs'.

**The test is stricter than a body is.** One ray from one eye point to the target's one head point. A target has a width and a passer's eye moves: testing the same occlusion to the target's shoulder height or to his head shifted a torso half-width (0.2 yd) either side, the share fully blocked is **0.114 of perimeter ticks (from 0.177) and 0.074 of all defender-ticks (from 0.149)**: a third to a half of the blocked ticks are a single-ray artefact. (A passer's real eye also moves: he steps and shifts in the pocket, 0.3-0.5 yd, and sees past a helmet.)

## Answers to the QB lane's two questions

1. **Is this occlusion right?** Partly. The geometry (who is where, how tall they are, the sunk postures) is plausible; the blocker is a helmet; but a point-to-point ray makes a tackle's 9-inch head block a 12-yd-wide corner for as long as the three bodies line up. A real passer sees the corner partly (his shoulder, his body) or moves his head.
2. **Does he get a glimpse through the gaps as the bodies move?** Yes in the default engine: blocked runs are short (p50 0.1 s, p90 0.3 s), picture age p50 0.25 s even when blocked, so a **1.0-1.45 s blackout is a tail event** (0.4% of perimeter play-defenders have a run of 0.95 s or more, 4.8% of 0.5 s or more), the case QB_V2's staged trips stick hits (the right tackle's head or the nickel's on the line to the flat). It is not typical of the old controller's games, but it is a real fault for a read that stares at one man: the blackout is 4-7x the median and falls exactly on the man he keys.

## Proposal (not built; needs the go): `SIGHT_BODY` (off)

The occlusion test against the target as a body: clear if the ray to **any of** (his head point, his shoulder height, his head point shifted 0.2 yd either side across the line) is clear; the passer's eye as a point as now. Consequences to measure on a card: the passer's picture age of perimeter defenders (p90 0.7 s expected to fall), `sighted()` consumers for all observers under SIGHT_CARRIER / SIGHT_COVER / SIGHT_RECOG (every observer's occlusion halves: defenders' pictures of receivers behind bodies too), and the pass-rush and coverage reads that depend on being unseen.
Alternative or addition (QB lane's choice): the passer's eye sampled across his shoulder width (0.15 yd either side) as a head movement. Neither changes the age rule.
