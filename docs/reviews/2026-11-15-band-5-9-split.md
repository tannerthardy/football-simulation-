# The 5-9 yd completion gap, taken apart (2026-11-15)

Question (Integration): the scorecard's 5-9 yd band completes 59-63% (seeds 23 / 31, intended air yards) against the NFL's 77.8% (passLength 5-10). Same cut first; if it stands, split the incompletions.
Operator: `scripts/diag/route-speed/arrival_split.py`, one function over the common play format (`nfl_bdb2025.py` + `nfl_bdb2025_ball.py` for the NFL, `sim-export.ts` with its ball track for the sim). Population: BDB 2025 weeks 1-9 dropbacks with a named receiver, not batted at the line, an arrival event and a ball track (NFL 6,749 passes);
sim 16 games, seed 101, equal-strength teams, develop 0c8c38b4 (engine-66) (`sim`: 1,355 passes) and the route-card branch `claude/laughing-cray-4vqegz` f63193cd with ROUTE_FILLET, ROUTE_EFFORT, ROUTE_END and SCREEN_DECLARE on (`card`: 1,302). The band is the ball's x at the arrival frame (its actual travel past the line, which is what passLength is), 5 <= air < 10.

On the same games, the sim's completion by INTENDED air yards (what the scorecard bands on) is 73.1% for the 5-9 band (n 655), by the ball's arrival depth 81.0%: of the 655 passes aimed at 5-9, 206 arrive at 0-4 yd (and complete there) and only 400 arrive in 5-9. So the cut moves the sim's band number by about 8 points. Overall completion on attempts (native, equal-strength teams): engine-66 71.0%, card arm 66.4%; the scorecard's league sample (seeds 23 / 31) reads 63-67%, below an equal-strength sample; that is the other part of the scorecard's 59-63%.
Stopping condition met: on the identical cut the 5-10 yd band is not 15 points under the NFL; it is 3.6 points over (engine-66) and 0.8 over (card arm), n about 400 each (standard error about 2 points). What the scorecard shows as a gap in this band is the aim-point cut plus the sample, not a lower catch rate at those depths. What is still under the NFL on the arrival cut is behind the line (62% against 92%): screens and flat throws behind the line complete far less often in the sim.

Re-weighting the NFL's completion rates by the sim's separation mix gives about +2.5 points of completion, which is most of the remaining 3.6.

## Checks and limits

- The nearest-defender distances are positional (no touch inference); the NFL arrival stamp precedes the catch (see miss), which also moves sepT slightly.
- sim n is 427 / 400 per arm in this band (one 16-game set per arm); NFL 1,194. A 3-point difference is inside two standard errors for each sim cell; the cut result (8 points between aim and arrival cuts on the same games) and the separation mix are the findings.
- The sim sample is equal-strength teams, not the scorecard's league sample; I did not have the arrival depth in the scorecard records, so the league-sample number on the arrival cut is not measured here.
