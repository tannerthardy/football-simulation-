## What is wrong

1. **Who the key man is.** `keyMan` names a flat / curl-flat key as "the second-widest defender within 8 yd of the
   line on the key's side", from the true alignment. From doubles the second-widest man there is the strong-side
   defensive end, a pass rusher, on 42 of 48 plays: the read is of a man rushing the passer, and it comes out "high"
   in nearly every coverage. From trips it names the nickel, who is the real flat player about half the time (in cover
   2 the flat belongs to the squatting corner). The corner key (smash, corner_flat) names the corner and reads by the
   shell as taught (the corner squats in cover 2: the corner route; he bails in cover 3: the hitch).
2. **A man who does not move.** `readKey` reads movement along the stretch (|v| >= 1.0 yd/s at the read), else where
   the man stands against the stretch's midpoint (s >= 0.5: low). A man standing still past the midpoint reads low
   whatever his job; Rules' cover 4 nickel (3.5 -> 4.1 yd deep by 1.2 s) is read low on where he stands.

## Proposal (not built)

As taught, from his picture, never the true alignment or the defense's call:

- **The key man, identified pre-snap by the coverage shell as he reads it.** Candidates are coverage players in his
  picture at the snap: off the line (2 yd deep or more) or a detached man outside the tackle box; never a man on the
  line in a stance (a rusher). For a flat / curl-flat key: against two-high (his belief's single-high mass < 0.5) with a
  hard corner (6 yd deep or less, outside leverage on #1), the corner is the flat player; otherwise the overhang: the
  first coverage player inside the corner on the key's side, over or outside #2 ("count the flat defender"). Hook, corner
  and deep keys keep their current identification (the corner key reads correctly) with the same candidate rule.
- **The read is his movement after the snap**, in his picture: his displacement along the stretch since the snap, with a
  commit margin (enter 1.0 yd, exit 0.5 yd; chosen), not his position against a midpoint. A man who has not committed by
  the cue is read by the concept's coached default for a man who sits: for a flat / curl-flat key the flat (stick,
  curl-flat: "if he doesn't widen, throw the flat"); for the corner key the corner route (he has not squatted on the
  hitch... to be sourced per key with Rules). A wrong pre-snap shell read names the wrong man, as a real QB is fooled by
  a disguise: that is the error the model should keep.
- **Witness:** the low share by coverage for each keyed concept (it must follow the coverage, as the corner key does),
  "named = real flat player" by coverage, and the outcome cells Rules gated (flood and stick from trips).

## Attempt 1, built (QB_KEY_READ, off) and gated

As ruled, with Rules' `key.sits` (#813, rules/key-sits ad225b1a) for the sitter default. One correction inside the
attempt: a man who has not moved but stands on one of the two routes in his picture (within 0.25 of the stretch's
ends, chosen) has taken it, and the other route is the throw (Rules' own rule: "throw the route he is not standing on";
smash's squatting corner stands on the hitch, and the first build's sitter default sent the ball to that hitch in every
coverage). Only a man between the routes gets the concept's sitter default.

Gate (key-read-panel.ts on rules/key-sits with the repair, 1007 keyed plays):
- named = the real flat player, flat / curl-flat keys: off 157 / 418, on 388 / 418; a down lineman named: off 265, on 0.
- the read follows the coverage (low share by coverage, per concept, in the panel's table): hitch_out from doubles off
  0/6 in seven coverages, on low against cover 1 / 2-man / 3 / 4 / 6 and high against cover 2 / tampa 2; smash on high
  against man, low against cover 3 / 4 / 6.
- outcomes, off / on at the commit margin 0.5, 1.0, 1.5 yd: completions 687 / 707, 709, 719; interceptions 23 / 19,
  19, 19; sacks 17 / 16, 16, 15; yd a play 8.11 / 7.87, 7.74, 8.07. The margin moves which reads are movement and which
  are defaults (move 839 / 640 / 461), not the outcomes. Yards dip where off threw the longer route in every coverage on
  a pass rusher's key (double_outs from doubles 12.7 -> 6.7, corner_flat from trips 13.4 -> 8.6); completions and
  interceptions improve.
- the read is committed: it changed once on 31-45 plays (a perceived move past the margin the other way).
- PA_TABLES on changed nothing on these concepts.
