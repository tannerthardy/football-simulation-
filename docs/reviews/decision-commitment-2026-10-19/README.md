## Diagnosis

**There is no single lack-of-commitment knob.** In these saved ordinary runs,
77 defender histories across seven inputs commit to run and never reverse that
belief during the observed intervals. Play-action controls preserve genuine
run→pass rereads. Recognition already has hysteresis; carrier choices, blocker
plans and installed match branches also have persistence. Lower recognition
usually lengthens decisions rather than causing more frequent fresh decisions.
A universal delay would sometimes make the players slower to a justified reread.

Low speed alone cannot identify the bad decision. Counter Mike successfully

The useful question is whether each action makes achievable progress toward
its installed responsibility using available evidence.

## The working player model

Keep four concepts distinct within existing owners:

1. **Responsibility:** the installed football job, leverage, help and required
   handoff/completion. It persists while valid; it is not a frozen coordinate.
2. **Evidence and interpretation:** personally acquired motion/contact, aging
   memory and installed expectations. Ratings affect delay, quality, useful
   interpretation and stable errors. Low-IQ players can hesitate or commit
   quickly to the wrong answer; do not manufacture per-tick indecision.
3. **Chosen action:** target, fixed/moving goal, movement mode and the cue that
   authorized it belong to one decision. Continue it while updating own-body
   feedback; transfer on meaningful contrary evidence, completion or a legitimate
   handoff. Reuse owner-local held-read structures rather than a global phase manager.
4. **Execution and safety:** traffic/approach/contact must preserve the action's
   purpose when an attainable alternative exists. They may brake or invalidate
   it for a real obstacle, unavailable support or physical constraint. They
   must not silently substitute “stand here” simply because the goal was lost.

Useful urgency comes from how soon the threat reaches the duty relative to
what the player's body can achieve. Full speed everywhere is not that model.
Urgency, commitment and rereading should cooperate through explicit action
contracts, without an unbreakable latch or a mandatory extra read.
