# DIR_CURVE and TURN_CARRY (flags off), 2026-11-17

Base develop 2f5315d9, branch `tools/turn-arc`. Both flags default off; flag-off behaviour is unchanged.

**A. `DIR_CURVE=1` (needs DIR_CAP).** The direction cap reads the measured NFL curve in place of DIR_CAP's line to the backpedal share: p90 share of forward speed by the angle of orientation off travel, 1.00 / .97 / .88 / .75 / .67 / .66 at 15 / 45 / 75 / 105 / 135 / 165 degrees (2022 BDB weeks 1-9, 8,634 dropbacks, `scripts/diag/yac/facing_speed.py`), linearly interpolated, flat past 165. Caveat: the curve pools offense and defense and all open movement, and `o` is the shoulders where the cap reads the hips. Individual scaling: a man's fall below 1.0 is the table's fall times (1 - his backpedal) / (1 - the median man's backpedal), so the median man reads the table exactly. The median is the generated league's profile.backpedal (0.53, 66 players, all positions; DB/LB 0.50; `DIR_CURVE_MEDIAN_PCT`), a sim attribute, not an NFL fit.

**B. `TURN_CARRY=1`.** In `locomotionAccel`, when the request is more than 90 degrees off his velocity, the acceleration is blended between today's law and a turn on the lateral limit that holds speed (tangential demand = hold, or brake to a lower wanted speed; lateral = the full envelope on the ellipse boundary). Weight |sin 2phi|: 0 at 90, 1 at 135, 0 at 180, so it is continuous with today's law at 90 and a true stop or reversal at 180. Both accelerations are inside the friction ellipse, so their blend is. No constants fitted.
