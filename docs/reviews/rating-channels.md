## Independently traced channels

Both traits are generated by `players/generator.ts`, with position tiers,
archetype/experience adjustments and final integer clamping to 1–99. Execution
normalization is **(rating−1)/98**, clamped: 1/50/99 becomes 0/.5/1. Suitability
uses **raw rating points**, not those normalized values.

**leadBlock:** FB core and TE secondary generation → `roles.ts` FB_LEAD weight
3/8 and TE_H weight2/6 → roleSuitability/overallAt → `team.ts` pickOffense.
FB selection includes familiarity/availability; H selection uses max(TE_H,TE_F)
after the Y slot is assigned. A changed H-back role score need not change the
winning score or lineup. The diagnostic runs both actual selectors separately.

The execution trace also follows `derivePhysical`, fresh `SnapMind`,
`assignBehaviors`/`planRunBlocking` and `RunBlocker`, plus the cached common/run
trench traits in `engagement.ts`. These consume specific capacity, mind,
runBlock/handTechnique and other ratings; there is no leadBlock input in those
derived values, generic rating-key branches or cached technique values on this
source. The native F really has a **lead** job; this is not a parked blocker.
The measured fixed-lineup 1/50/99 arms have identical requests, complete bounded
result/body/mind/perception/intent states and random consumption. This supports
**selection-only on the traced current implementation**. One unchanged fixture
alone would not establish absence of a consumer; the source/cached-value trace
is part of that conclusion.

**playRecognition:** LB core generation → LB_MIKE weight2.5/9.5 and other role
weights → actual pickDefense, separately from the frozen execution lineup.
Before PlaySim construction, changing the rating rebuilds:

- `derivePhysical.reactionTime`, a **mental perception latency** stored in the
  profile (all force/movement/contact capacity fields remain invariant);
- `SnapMind`'s cached reaction/decision interval and `Agent.snapReadyAt`;
- `Recognition`'s gameSense-scaled prior, gain, integration tau and coherent
  per-play bias; `assignBehaviors` also rebuilds the legacy fallback read time;
- delayed `sim.see` pictures and recognition read/hysteresis used by the
  installed zone behavior and its cached aim timing.

Other current consumers include QB reads, run-read margins, man/coverage
judgments, ball contests and cached defensive engagement reads. They are not
all exposed by this LB case. In this fixture the two direct Agent.r reads occur
at construction; **zero later direct reads does not mean no execution effect**.
JSON records actual cached values and actual consumed picture calls, without
adding sensory queries. The fallback Agent.readAt is distinct from the live
Recognition.readAt; do not label the former an observed commitment.

## Player-type claims

Selection-only means leadBlock can change **who plays**, and thus aggregate
results, without making a fixed player a better lead technician. Implemented
execution means playRecognition has a real, composite decision/perception
channel here; it does not establish human calibration or universal superiority.
Inactive exposure and unavailable input/provenance are different from an
implemented channel producing an unchanged response. Missing inputs fail rather
than silently count as zero effect. Full intermediate traces remain in JSON.

A separate implementation task could study the lead blocker's **pre-contact
near-shoulder fit and entry-angle placement from the acquired defender path**,
distinct from sustaining a run block after contact. That needs an actual
observed-information/footwork mechanism and opposing controls first. This is a
proposal only: do not duplicate runBlock, add a success multiplier, change
strength or award a block win to manufacture a leadBlock effect.
