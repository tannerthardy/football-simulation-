# The plant: braking, the low-speed redirect and re-acceleration, ours against the NFL (measurement; no build)

Tools/Physics, 2026-11-25, for Integration, Run Game and Defense (their question after #952: is brake-plant-reaccelerate honestly slower than arcing in our World for a body asked to reach a near point 90-120 degrees off its travel, when NFL defenders choose the plant). No engine change, no controller change. Develop `de9692248` (engine-102). Operator `scripts/diag/pursuit/plant_legs.py` on the same census-format tracks as #952 (NFL: 5,266 completions and 5,684 runs through the end of the play; sim: the 24 games of #952, 592 pursuer turns; bench: `scripts/diag/pursuit/plant_bench.mts`, 1,296 composite cases on four pursuit bodies, both turn directions). Tables: `data/reviews/pursuit/plant-report.md`, `plant-bench-composite.md`.

## In short

1. **No leg is short, and the composite is not short.** The bench's best strategy reaches a point 6, 9 and 12 yd away at 90 and 120 degrees in the NFL defenders' fastest tenth of times or faster in every cell with data (0.0-0.8 s faster; table below), on a path as short as the NFL's p25. The NFL times are what defenders did on the way through such a point, not an optimised intercept, so the bench being faster is expected; the bench being slower would have been the shortfall.
2. **Braking (leg 1): the body brakes harder than any NFL defender does except in the extreme tail.** Peak deceleration into a redirect of 60 degrees or more, NFL p50 / p90 / p99 (contact-free): 3.4 / 4.9 / 7.0 yd/s2 from 5.5 yd/s (n 121), 4.0 / 5.4 / 6.6 from 7 (59), 4.2 / 6.0 / 6.9 from 8.5 (9). The bench body asked to stop brakes at 6.9-7.0 (its `brake`, 6.3-7.0 by body) from 5.5, 7 and 8.5 yd/s. The NFL's mean deceleration into a redirect is 2.2-2.7 yd/s2 over 1.5-2.2 s and 5.8-12.4 yd, a long gentle brake.
3. **Low-speed redirect (leg 2): ours turns tighter than the NFL at the median and the 90th percentile, and a friction circle describes both.** At 2-3 yd/s the lateral acceleration (p50 / p90 / p99) is 3.2 / 5.3 / 6.9 yd/s2 in the NFL, 4.7 / 6.0 / 6.4 in the sim in game, 5.8 / 6.3 / 6.4 on the bench; heading rate 1.2 / 2.1 / 2.9 rad/s in the NFL, 1.8 / 2.5 / 3.1 in game. The combined |a| at the 99th percentile is flat across the share that is braking on both sides (NFL 7.3 / 7.3 / 7.5 yd/s2 for braking under a third / a third to two thirds / over two thirds; sim 6.6 / 6.6 / 6.7; bench 6.4 / 6.4 / 6.9): a circle of radius about 7.3-7.7 (NFL) and 6.5-6.9 (ours). The 8-12% gap is only in the tail, where the NFL's 10 Hz positions inflate a two-step acceleration (a 0.05 yd position error is about 1.2 yd/s2 on a 0.4 s centred difference); ours are exact, so the tail is not comparable and nothing below the 90th percentile shows a gap.
4. **Re-acceleration (leg 3): ours is at or ahead of the NFL.** After the lowest speed of a plant (under 3.5 yd/s), 2 yd of path takes 0.77 s (NFL, n 992), 0.73 s (sim, 70), 0.78 s (bench plant) at 3.8 / 4.1 / 4.5 yd/s, and 4 yd takes 1.28 / 1.18 / 1.17 s at 4.7 / 5.2 / 5.7 yd/s; the NFL's quickest tenth does 4 yd in 1.00 s (the sim's 1.00 s, the bench's 1.10 s from a lower start: its lowest speed is 1.6 against the NFL's 2.1).
5. **Arc against plant in our World: the plant wins by 1.2-2.1 s at 90 degrees, and it is a tie at 120.** At 90 degrees a point 6-12 yd away lies inside the turning circle (radius v2 / a_n: about 5 yd at 5.5 yd/s, 8 at 7, 12 at 8.5, on `lateral x (1 - 0.2 v / vmax)`), so a continuous full request orbits it (3.9-4.8 s and 16-22 yd of path where it gets there at all; at 7 yd/s from 12 yd and at 8.5 yd/s from 9 and 12 yd it does not reach it in 5 s) and a plant or a slow request reaches it in 2.1-3.3 s. At 120 degrees the arc and the best plant agree to within 0.06 s in 9 of 9 cells (the plant is 0.05 s faster at 6 yd, a tie or the arc 0.01 s ahead at 9 and 12 yd). This is Integration's tie. It is the geometry: at 120 degrees the point is behind the turn's arc but the body is already heading away, so the plant's cost (the speed it sheds, which it must regain) is what it saves in path.
6. **Which term would flip the 120 degree choice**, one body term scaled on the bench (counterfactual, not a proposal; mean of best plant minus arc over the nine cells, negative = the plant wins):

| bench body | best plant minus arc at 120 degrees (s) | cells where the plant is ahead / tie (within 0.03 s) / the arc is ahead |
|---|---|---|
| as it is | -0.015 | 3 / 6 / 0 |
| lateral x 1.1 | +0.021 | 0 / 5 / 4 |
| lateral x 1.2 | +0.056 | 0 / 2 / 7 |
| brake x 1.2 (8.4 yd/s2) | -0.083 | 8 / 1 / 0 |
| re-acceleration time constant x 0.9 | -0.028 | 4 / 5 / 0 |

More lateral capacity favours the arc (it turns without braking); more braking favours the plant; faster re-acceleration favours it a little. The measured legs say braking is not short (the NFL's p99 deceleration is the bench's 7.0), so a braking increase to 8.4 is not supported by tracking, and lateral capacity is not short at the median or 90th percentile. So nothing in the body would, on this evidence, pick the plant at 120 degrees where the NFL would; at 90 degrees the plant is already picked by the geometry.

## Limits

- The NFL composite is a sample of what defenders happened to do, with their own targets unknown; a point they passed through was not necessarily their goal. The comparison it supports is one-sided: the bench best at or below the NFL's fastest tenth rules out a shortfall; it does not say the NFL arcs or plants.
- Contact-free filters (no body within 1 yd) cut the NFL composite to 8-19 events per cell, so the main composite table is the unfiltered one; the contact-free one gives the same times within 0.2 s where it has the events (5.5 yd/s).
- Tails of acceleration (p99 and the circle's radius) are not comparable between 10 Hz tracking with the provider's smoothing and exact bench positions; the median and 90th percentile are.
- The bench bodies are four average pursuit bodies; the NFL is a mixed population. Entry speeds 7.75 yd/s and over have few NFL events (9 braking, none in the composite).
- The sensitivity runs scale one term of the bench body only; they say which term moves the choice, not that any term should move.
- The sim in-game samples are small (8 events per braking cell, 70 re-accelerations); the leg tables show them where they have 8 or more.
