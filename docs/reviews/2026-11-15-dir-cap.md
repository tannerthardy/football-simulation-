# DIR_CAP: a monotone directional speed cap and the trunk priced once (Tools, 2026-11-15)

Flag `DIR_CAP` (off; `=1` turns on). Code: `src/physics/kinematics.ts` (`directionalSpeedFactor`, `legDriveFactor`, `trunkPrice`, `effectiveVmax`, the start cap),
`src/physics/world.ts` (the push and leg-drive laws, unchanged except for the trunk term). Test: `test/dir-cap.test.ts`. Diagnostics:
`scripts/diag/route-speed/{nfl_dir_capacity.py,sim_dir_capacity.ts,sim_flight_speed.ts}`, `scripts/diag/dir-cap-lone.mts`.

## What the NFL curve is

BDB 2025 weeks 1 to 9, 322 zone-coverage DBs (CB, FS, SS), ball held, 0.5 s after the snap to the throw, speed at least 1 yd/s, 772k frames. The angle is the compass
shoulders `o` against the provider's direction of travel, folded to 0 to 180, 15 degree bins. Speed is the provider `s` over that man's own p98 over all his
frames. The capacity is the top decile per bin (the median is choice).

| bin (deg) | top decile | share of forward | legacy cap (shuffle .60, backpedal .68) | DIR_CAP (backpedal .65) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-15 | 1.04 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| 30-45 | 1.00 | 0.96 | 0.93 | 0.92 |
| 45-60 | 0.95 | 0.91 | 0.87 | 0.88 |
| 75-90 | 0.84 | 0.81 | 0.66 | 0.76 |
| 90-105 | 0.78 | 0.75 | 0.61 | 0.70 |
| 120-135 | 0.68 | 0.65 | 0.63 | 0.65 |
| 135-180 | 0.65 | 0.625 | 0.64-0.67 | 0.65 |

The NFL curve is monotone, with the backpedal the slowest point. The legacy curve has side-on (90 degrees) at 0.60, under the square backpedal at 0.68, which is backwards
from the sport (Defense's PEDAL_CAP oddity), and from 60 to 120 degrees it is 0.10 to 0.15 under the NFL.

## The change

* **Hips.** `directionalSpeedFactor` under the flag is full speed within 20 degrees of the line of travel, a straight fall to the backpedal's share at 130 degrees, and flat
  beyond. `shuffle` is no longer read by the cap (controllers that read `profile.shuffle` directly for a square lateral shuffle are untouched).
* **Trunk.** `trunkPrice`: `1 - 0.0018 x |trunk|` per degree, linear, no line or wobble term (the twist-price cut found none). The number is a STATED CHOICE (Coverage's:
  10 percent at the 55 degree limit) checked against an observational curve: NFL twist price, ball held, matched on route and time, -0.26 / -0.54 / -1.11 yd/s at 15-30 /
  30-60 / 60+ degrees against a 6.5 yd/s cruise (4 / 8 / 17 percent), and the shoulder curve above up to 55 degrees (0.96 / 0.92 / 0.90 predicted against 1.00 / 0.93 / 0.91
  observed). The NFL data carries the confound that a receiver looking back may throttle by choice, so it is an upper bound on the capacity cost. I did not verify a gait
  source that puts a number on the cost; the likely mechanism is the arm drive (counter-rotation of the shoulders against the hips), so the number is not derived from one.
* **No double charge.** Hips are charged by the curve and the trunk by its own term. `o` is the shoulders, so the NFL curve cannot split them: the pair is checked only where
  the trunk is the whole of the angle (hips on the path, below), and in the sim's own shoulder bins.
* **The leg-drive force law is NOT moved.** `world.ts`'s push and leg-drive laws read `legDriveFactor`, the legacy curve. When the monotone curve was applied there too the
  pass rush's engagement dither went from 0.0364 to 0.0440 /s (104 to 123 events, budget 0.039); a top-speed capacity is not a statement about the force of a hand fight, and
  the same curve in both is a choice I should not make from this data. With the trunk term alone the rush row is 0.0366 (104).
* `directionalSpeedFactor` is also what controllers read to plan (a defender's `need <= cap` test), so their plans follow the new cap.

## Lane reads

* **Zone DBs (the same operator as the NFL, 128 zone passes).** Capacity by hips angle off, on against off: 0.97 / 0.96 at 0-15, 0.61 / 0.61 at 75-90, 0.59 / 0.56 at 90-105,
  0.53 / 0.54 at 120-135. The sim's drops are controller and acceleration limited, not cap limited, so the cap change does not move them. `ZONE_EYES` is not on develop; its
  deep and underneath speeds before and after need its branch.
* **Receivers.** Route speed with the ball held is bit for bit the same (go 9.29, dig 6.07 at 3.5 s; hips on the path, no twist). Through the flight of a deep ball (four_verts
  X, staged throw at 2.2 s, 48 throws): 8.19 / 8.20 / 8.24 / 8.23 / 8.33 / 8.17 / 8.14 off, 8.20 / 8.20 / 8.23 / 8.21 / 8.32 / 8.17 / 8.17 on. The sim's receiver does not
  twist until about 1.5 s after the release (18 degrees), so the price is not charged there yet; where it will bite is a controller that looks early, which the NFL's median
  62 degree twist in flight says they do.
* **Run set (1,321 plays, paired).** Mean yards capped at 20: 3.68 to 3.58 (-0.09, SE 0.08). Stuffs 11.2 to 11.5 percent, 10+ yd 7.5 to 6.8, 20+ yd 2.2 to 2.0, missed + broken
  open 0.248 to 0.263 a run. Small, and in the direction a slower cap in the open gives.

## What it leaves open

* The NFL backpedal (0.625 of forward) is above our bodies' (0.54 to 0.55); `profile.backpedal` is a physical-profile question, not changed here.
* A square lateral shuffle (hips 90 degrees to travel) is 0.76 under this curve, from the NFL shoulder-angle bin where defenders run with open hips and square shoulders; if a pure
  shuffle should stay slower than an open-hip crossover run, that needs hip-angle data the tracking does not have.
