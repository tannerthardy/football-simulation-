The new correction is at the **contact owner**, after the defender has already
acquired and declared a runner-support objective. A receiver's body can be
behind that approach while the contact controller keeps requesting a sideways
move in its old surface frame. In WZ7 this eventually moves away from the
acquired runner. A defender can request a better exit without being physically
entitled to defeat the hands or reach the runner.
