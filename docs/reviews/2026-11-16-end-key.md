# END_KEY bit 2: the edge keys through the triangle (2026-11-16)

Defense lane. This is the end's half of the play-action backside end; Integration ruled it attempt 1. It is built on
the card head (1cda30345) plus Flex's PA_HINGE patch (`docs/reviews/patches/pa-hinge-1cda3034.patch`).

Flag: `END_KEY`, numeric, default 0 (off). Code: `src/sim/behaviors/defense.ts`, `PassRusher.decide`,
`END_KEY_THROUGH`.

## Population (settled first)

- In gun_doubles_11 the back is offset to the weak side, and his fake goes to his own side. PA_DROP_FAKE moves it to
  the strong side only when the back is directly behind the QB (`assign.ts`).
- On the staged PA grid, the fake goes toward DE_W on 36 of 36 plays.
  - `out/diag/pa/one.mts`, one play: ball y 23.58; back 25.88 → 25.25 at 0.8 s; DE_W 29.24; DE_S 17.59;
    `paFakeSide` +1.
- So DE_W is the fake-side end and DE_S is the end away from the fake. Earlier tables headed "backside end" were about
  DE_W.
- Flex split the NFL rows to match (`nfl_pa_backside.py`): the fake side is the side of the back's first lateral move.

## What was wrong for the away-side end (DE_S)

`scripts/diag/defense/end-trace.mts` (36 staged PA plays, PA_HINGE=1) and a temporary hook on the key read.

- His key (`manInFront`: nearest surface player, unlimited reach on the edge) is **Y**, not the tackle.
- Y has no blocking stance and releases upfield (0.25 → 1.61 yd/s at 0.4–0.7 s).
- So `runStep` is false on every tick. The hinging tackle, more than 3 yd inside him, is never his key.
- He goes upfield at about 0.8 s, 0.58 / 1.85 / 2.80 yd past the line at 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 s, still 2.8 yd outside his
  tackle at 1.5 s.

**The coached read.** Gridiron Strategies, "Defensive End Reads in a Forty Front" (Rules, page checked):
- The TE's release is not a pass read: "Once you read release, look for the next part of triangle".
- The tackle's step tells him the rest:
  - zone away: flatten and pursue, check boot;
  - pass set: "keying the tackle to set the pass rush".

## The change (bit 2)

- When the surface man he keyed has no blocking stance in his picture, the edge keys on through to the nearest
  offensive lineman (his tackle).
- The existing key reads then play that man's step:
  - a hinge or zone step toward the ball: key-squeeze, or END_CHASE when the whole line steps away with no puller;
  - a pass set: the rush.
- It adds no new threshold.

## Bit 1: tried and removed (a miss)

Bit 1 had an unengaged edge read his key's stance as a run/pass cue: low −1.2, high +1.2, weight 1.5, the cue an
engaged lineman takes from his block. On PA it moved the fake-side end the wrong way:
- During the run look, every DE_W committed run at 0.9 s (pPass 0.13) and fit inside.
- His depth fell to −0.21 / 0.34 / 1.19, further from the NFL fake-side end, who holds his width and gets upfield.
- It did release faster once the tackle set, but the net path was worse.

What tells the NFL end that a play is a fake is a separate cue study (Integration item 2).
