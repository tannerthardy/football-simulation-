# Why the flat is rarely thrown (2026-11-11)

Report only; no engine change. Rules + Coaching for Coverage, who own the QB's
choice at the throw. Integration item (a) after `CALL_MIX` (#531).

**Background.**
- NFL flats are 12.7% of targets and complete 83%.
- Ours are 4–7% in the concepts that run them (`docs/reviews/2026-11-11-call-mix.md`).
- With the flat read first (`FLAT_FIRST`, branch `rules/flat-first`), flat targets rose only from 1 to 6 of 87 attempts, and the extra look cost 0.3 s.

**Answer.** The flat is open: as open as the NFL's flats, or more. The QB's
own throw estimate (`QBPass.evaluate` / `window` in
`src/sim/behaviors/qb.ts`) judges it closed. With the nearest defender
3–4.5 yd away, he rates the flat:
- **P 0.36, INT risk 0.13.**

NFL flats thrown with the nearest defender at that distance:
- **complete 87%, 0 interceptions** (n 78).

His INT bar is about 0.03–0.11, so the INT-risk estimate alone blocks most flat
throws. The NFL targets 19% of the flat routes it runs; the sim targets 4%.

## Population

**Sim**
- Coverage's matched panel: seeds 7 and 11 × 4 dice shifts, 788 dropbacks.
- Captured on develop 8519cc5a + #527 and replayed on 01b9037e (run-ref-17).
- Flat runners: receivers on a `flat` or `arrow` route. That gives 176 runners on 152 plays.
- `scripts/diag/call-mix/flat-open.ts` wraps `QBPass.evaluate` passively. All 152 probed plays match an unprobed replay to the yard.

**NFL**
- BDB 2025 weeks 1–3, dropbacks, routeRan FLAT (`scripts/diag/call-mix/nfl_flat_open.py`).
- 821 WR/TE and 1,377 RB flat runners; 424 targeted.

**Operator.** Both sides use the same one:
- depth past the ball's snap spot and width from it;
- speed;
- the nearest defender's distance and closing speed (positive = closing).

## 2. The QB's estimate against NFL outcomes, by defender distance

| nearest defender | sim evaluations | sim P p50 | sim looks clearing the bar | sim INT risk p50 | NFL flat throws (share) | NFL completion | NFL INT |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| under 1.5 yd | 192 | 0.11 | 0% | 0.43 | 19 (4%) | 37% | 0% |
| 1.5–3 | 117 | 0.27 | 22% | 0.25 | 42 (10%) | 71% | 2.4% |
| 3–4.5 | 63 | 0.36 | 33% | 0.13 | 78 (18%) | **87%** | **0%** |
| 4.5–6 | 3 | 0.64 | 100% | 0.00 | 96 (23%) | 74% | 0% |
| 6+ | 14 | 0.52 | 71% | 0.09 | 189 (45%) | 81% | 0.5% |

**Sim** side:
- Every `evaluate` call on a flat runner, from both arms of the panel: installed read order and `FLAT_FIRST`. 389 evaluations.
- "Clearing the bar" means P ≥ need, where need = 0.6 − 0.12 · aggression − 0.32 · urgency. It is about 0.56 on a first look.
- Throwing also needs INT risk ≤ about 0.03–0.11.

**NFL** side: all targeted flats (WR/TE and RB) with a result, n 424, by the nearest defender at `pass_forward`.

**Two effects compound:**
- **Few looks.** With the installed order the flat is the current read on 19 of 152 plays, first at about 1.8 s. At 2.0 s the QB has evaluated only 8 of 157 flats.
- **Pessimistic looks.** When he does look, a flat with a defender 3–4.5 yd away rates P 0.36 / INT 0.13. The NFL completes 87% of those with no interceptions.
  - Under 3 yd, the INT-risk term (0.25–0.43) is several times the NFL's interception rate (0–2.4%).
  - The likely contributors are in `window()`:
    - the "first" (sitting-on-the-route) pick term, `first * (0.15 + 0.35 · ballSkills)`;
    - the undercut sampling along a flat, low trajectory;
    - a defender's time to the catch point measured to a point led downfield of a receiver running away from him.
  - I did not isolate which term dominates. That is Coverage's next measurement if they take it.

## What follows

- **Read order isn't the lever.** `FLAT_FIRST` shows it: the flat is seen sooner and still rejected.
- **The flat routes themselves aren't the problem:** they are as open as the NFL's.
- **The QB's estimate of short, wide throws is what to calibrate.** A defender's ability to reach a lead point on a receiver running toward the sideline, and to undercut a flat ball, should be checked against the NFL table above, which gives completion and INT by defender distance at the throw. This is Coverage's lane (the choice at the throw), report only from here.
- The sim's flats also run 1–2 yd/s faster and 2 yd wider than the NFL's by 2.0 s. If anything, that makes them more open than the NFL's.
