# CURL_SINK: the curl-flat defender sinks to his curl window as he widens (task 126)

Defense lane. Experimental flag `CURL_SINK` (off). A defect in the promoted `CURL_FLAT_BACK` branch, measured against
its own stated design; the switch exists so a card can attribute it.

## The defect

`CURL_FLAT_BACK` (engine-55) says: with No. 2 not carried vertically, the curl-flat defender who sees the back or No. 3
showing to his flat "sinks to his curl window and widens under it toward the flat with his eyes on the QB ... and breaks
on the throw". Its target depth was `Math.max(los + 2, Math.min(lm.x, a.pos.x))`: the shallower of the window (8.5 yd)
and where he stands. A back who shows to the flat before the defender has dropped therefore holds him at his alignment
depth for the rest of the play.

Staged flood (engine-69 defaults, `out/diag/cd/dig.mts`, 111 dig throws across eight calls): the Cover 3 doubles
curl-flat (NB) held 4.4 yd from 1.0 s to the throw at 3.4 s; across dig throws the curl-flat on the dig's side was at
4.0 / 4.8 / 9.2 yd (p25/p50/p75) at the throw, under 6 yd 59% of the time. The weak #1's dig broke in behind him at
13 yd.

## What is taught

The Cover 3 curl-flat player opens to his curl window (10-12 yd, under No. 1) and widens with what shows to the flat,
eyes on the quarterback; he plays the flat from high to low and breaks on the throw. Holding at alignment depth gives up
the curl and the dig behind him without gaining anything on the flat, which he plays from his break anyway.

## Change (flag on)

The branch's target depth is the window's landmark depth (`lm.x`); its width, the cheat toward the flat by what the QB
shows, the pace (`zs`) and the break on the throw are unchanged. ZONE_SETTLE's freeze at the QB's set still applies
(`settle()`); a defender pulled shallow is part (b), the underneath layer's hand-offs, with its own design note.

Stated cost: he is deeper when the flat is thrown, so a ball to the back in the flat is caught further from him.

## Measured (staged, `scripts/diag/defense/curl-sink.mts 3`: every pass concept but screens against cover3, cover3_match, cover3_seam, cover4, cover6; gun_doubles_11, ace_11; seeds 1-3, both flips; 1920 plays; off → on)

- The branch fires for 634 curl-flat defenders, at a median 2.4 s, with him at 5.3 yd.
- His depth while the ball is held (p50): 1.0 s 4.7 → 4.7, 1.5 s 4.8 → 5.0, 2.0 s 5.5 → 5.7, 2.5 s 6.2 → 6.8,
  3.0 s 6.9 → 7.4, 3.5 s 7.2 → 7.4.
- Dig with him on the dig's side: under 6 yd 26 → 24 throws (completion 0.88 → 0.79), 6 yd or deeper 99 → 102 (0.64 →
  0.65; 14.9 → 15.0 yd per throw).
- The stated cost, throws to the back on his side with the branch fired (100 → 105): 5.6 → 5.6 yd per throw; his distance
  at the catch 3.4 → 4.1 yd; to within 1 yd of the catcher 0.5 s after the catch either way (never 0.61 either way).
- All plays: completion 0.630 → 0.631, 8.47 → 8.48 yd per play.

The defect is real and the fix does what its design says, but it moves little: in these match calls the curl-flat
defender spends his first 1.5-2 s in the match carry's pre-release man pedal (`man-pedal` 76% at 0.5 s, 73% at 1.0 s,
54% at 1.5 s, depth 4.7-5.4 yd), then carries (`open-carry`, `open-carry-seam`), and reaches this branch late. The
underneath layer's depth is mostly decided there: part (b), with the hook pass-off and ZONE_SETTLE's freeze.
