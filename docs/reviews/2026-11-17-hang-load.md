# HANG_LOAD: the hanging weight is carried once (Tools, 2026-11-17, develop d7d6e3a9, flag off)

Rule (Integration, from the off-feet holder diagnosis): a man hanging on a runner is one weight. Today `World.feet` gives a down or falling holder friction on his whole weight (`fr * G * m`) while `heldSwing` also counts `HANG_SHARE` of it on his hold, and the carrier's feet and balance see none of it (an upper-body hold, `legs` 0, is skipped by `heldSwing` altogether).

Change (`HANG_LOAD=1`, off by default, `src/physics/world.ts`, `hangLoad` and `feet`):

* The share a holder's hold carries, `hangShare(state)` (0 up, 0.5 falling or down; `HANG_AIRBORNE` unchanged), comes off his turf: his friction capacity is `fr * G * m * (1 - share)`.
* It goes onto the carrier for every unbroken grasp, once per holder and carrier, upper-body holds included: the weight `W = share * m_holder * G` is subtracted from his lift (`liftImp`), so his traction grows with it, and, hung at the torso's edge on the holder's side (lever = his profile radius, an assumption: the grasp point sits at his centre in this model), its moment is an equivalent horizontal acceleration at his centre of mass, `W r / (m H)`, added to the tip term that moves his capture point. His balance model decides what follows; nothing was added to decide a fall.
* His own acceleration law does not take the holder's mass a second time: the holder's horizontal inertia is already in the joint's two-body solve; the carrier's `locomotionAccel` acts on his own mass.
* `FALL_TIP` (3 yd/s^2 of drive along `sup.fall`, "a free body's forward tip") is not applied to a falling man who holds a runner: he is dragged, and the tip drive cancelled his friction (`FALL_FRICTION` 0.25 x m g is about 255, `FALL_TIP` x m about 285). `FALL_FRICTION`, `DOWN_FRICTION` and `FALL_TIP` are untouched and still unsourced; a later check should take a sliding-on-turf friction source (kinetic friction of clothed or padded bodies on natural grass; the football-turf and artificial-turf traction literature, e.g. studies of shoe-surface and body-surface friction) and decide the three together.

Grasp joints are made only in `tackle.ts` (`grasp`, `joint` at line 517); blocks and piles use `hands` joints, which this does not touch. A non-up holder of a grasp exists only in a tackle.

Results (staged hold trace, 3 seeds, 206 / 205 bonds on a carrier above 6 yd/s; lone-pair bench `scripts/diag/yac/hang-bench.mts`):

* Holder turf force along the run, median: falling +28 -> -116, down -697 -> -348 (the carried half comes off). Carrier acceleration with a falling holder -1.07 -> -1.21 yd/s^2, with a down holder -2.48 -> -1.61, with all holders up -3.01 -> -3.00.
* Bonds alive at 1 s 49% -> 48%; carrier speed at 0.5 s 5.18 -> 5.17, at 1.0 s 5.23 -> 5.23; a tackle or down event within 1 s of the bond 38% -> 37% (cover 2 27 / 27, cover 3 49 / 48, cover 4 38 / 37, cover 6 37 / 35; our event, not the NFL's 66-76% down-within-1-s, which is measured differently); broken tackles per bond 0.19 -> 0.20.
* Lone pair, carrier at 6 yd/s asked for 9, holder falling or down, upper hold, carrier speed at 1.0 s off -> on: 75 kg carrier with a 110 kg holder 4.25 / 2.67 -> 5.25 / 4.74; 95 with 95: 4.85 / 3.60 -> 5.68 / 5.28; 110 with 75: 5.43 / 4.47 -> 6.10 / 5.79. A heavier holder slows him more and a heavier carrier keeps more speed, with the flag as without; no carrier left his feet in a second in either case. The moment is small: `W r / (m g)` is 0.07-0.19 yd against a base radius of about 0.17 yd, and he recovers while he runs.
* Staged zone and man completions (`zone-man-after.mts`, 2 seeds), YAC mean off -> on: cover 1 3.25 -> 3.46, cover 2 8.39 -> 8.66, cover 3 6.55 -> 6.61, cover 4 5.13 -> 5.27, cover 6 5.36 -> 5.61.

So the conservation is right and the carrier now bears the load, but a down holder drags half as hard as before (his friction was counted on his whole weight while half of it was also on the hold), and the carrier's balance gains little. The flag lowers the drag on the carrier and raises zone YAC; it does not by itself make hanging on a runner slow him more.
