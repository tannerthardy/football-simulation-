# PA_BOOT: naked play action, v1 (parked) (2026-11-12)

**Status:** parked on branch `rules/pa-boot`, flag off. It cannot be judged until the boot-side end responds to run
action.
- **What it needs:** Defense's question of where a lineman's run/pass belief comes from on a PA snap, and the
  line's run steps during the look (Flex's `pa-look`, as Integration noted).
- **Integration's order since:** the ordinary play-action drop, then the caller, then this.

## What is built

- **Call structure** (`BOOT_CONCEPTS`): `naked`, off outside-zone action. The QB boots to the strong side:
  - #2 strong runs the sail (`deep_out`), and #1 clears;
  - the backside #1 runs the deep crosser (`cross`), and #2 a post;
  - the back fakes away and swings to the boot side.

  Read order: sail, crosser, flat (Evely 2023). Sources are in
  `docs/football-handbook/play-action-protections.md`.
- **Protection** (`ProtectionCoordinator.leaveFree`): the boot-side end, the widest defender on the line within
  7 yd of the ball on the boot side, is left to the QB; no protector is assigned to him.
- **The QB's path** (`dropSpec 'boot'`, `bootMove`): 8.6 yd deep, running at the boot side, half turned, to 9 yd
  wide; once set he attacks the line by 1 yd.
- **His read of the end** (`bootLook`), once, and committed:
  - out of the fake his eyes go to the end (`gazePoint`);
  - he decides on a fresh picture (≤ 0.15 s old), or 0.6 s after the fake;
  - **crash** (down the line toward the fake at least as fast as he comes upfield, or inside his path): flat first;
  - **at him** (closing faster than 1.5 yd/s from within 9 yd): the quickest throw;
  - **contain:** he pulls up where he is and throws back: crosser, sail, flat.

## Forced read (`scripts/diag/concepts/boot-probe.ts`)

48 plays: gun doubles and trips, Cover 1–4, seeds 1–3, both flips.

| | Value |
|---|---|
| Launch, p50 | 7.5 yd deep, 6.9 yd wide, released at 3.1 s |
| His read of the end | at him 36, crash 8, contain 4 |
| Attempts / completions / sacks / scrambles | 27 / 6 / 13 / 5 |
| Yards a play | −1.0 |
| On crash | 2 of 8 complete, 6.8 yd a play, no sacks |
| On contain | 1 of 4 complete, 4.5 yd a play, no sacks |
| On at him | 3 complete, 13 sacks, −3.3 yd a play |

- **The read is right; the end isn't fooled.** On play action the backside end is a pass rusher from the snap, so the
  QB correctly reads "at him" on three plays in four and the naked fails, as it does in football against an end who
  stays home.
- **Defense's trace:** the fake fails on the offense's side. The line shows a low stance for 0.35 s while its feet
  pass-set from the first step: 52% of backside ends read pass by 0.3 s (0% on a real zone run), and the 43% fooled
  stay fooled through 1.0 s.
- **The backside end on zone flow away** (`scripts/diag/concepts/backside-end.ts`, 32 league games):
  - on zone runs he chases 1.6 yd toward the play by 1.5 s (51% at least 1.5 yd);
  - on play action he rushes upfield (2.75 yd deep by 1.5 s; 32% chase).

## Fixes made during the first build

- **A pressed corner was taken as the end.** Fixed: the box only.
- **His picture of the end was 0.7 s old.** His gaze was on the middle of the field, so the picture showed the end
  widening when he was coming. Fixed: the eyes go to the end out of the fake.
- **The crash/at-him order:** a crashing end is also "closing" while the QB is still near the middle, so his
  direction decides.
- **The back's flat route cut upfield through the line.** Fixed: a swing behind it.
- **The QB decelerated into a 5-yd point.** Fixed: the drop aims at the full width.
