# HOT_LOOK: the hot throw costs the look (2026-11-12)

**Question.** Flex measured the QB with a free rusher at 2 yd, using the same operator on both sides:

| | Throws | Sacked | Scrambles | Throws away | Release after the 2-yd mark, p50 / p75 | Completion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NFL | 57% | 25% | 17% | 15% | 0.30 / 1.10 s | 48% |
| Ours | 77% | 17–19% | 4–6% | 1% | 0.25 / 0.4–0.5 s | 55–59% |

Integration's hypothesis: the hot path (`hotThrow`) takes the instant best over every receiver on the alarm tick,
wherever his eyes are. Is that the cheat, and what does pricing the look change?

HOT_PLAN (the plan's answer first under pressure) is retired. As built it never fired:

- the plan-answer source was taken 0 times on engine-38;
- with FREE_BOUND off, HOT_PLAN off and on were byte-identical over 1,536 panel plays.

## Diagnosis on engine-38: the hot throw, split by target

Instrument: the forced-concept panel (`scripts/diag/read-plan/panel.ts`, 1,536 plays per arm), with diagnostic fields
recorded on QBPass. Play is bit-identical with and without the recording.

| FREE_BOUND on, elsewhere | 55 | 0.00 s | 0.30 / 0.40 / 0.40 | 22 / 41° | 31 / 56° | 0.51 | 4 |

- **The decision is made on the tick of the first alarm, every time.** Release follows only after the windup, with no
  tail. The NFL's p75 of 1.10 s is the quarterback who has to find someone; ours never has to.
- **The cheat is mostly time, not throws behind his back.** The median hot target is about 30° from his gaze.
  About one in five is more than 60° away.
- **His picture of each receiver is young at the alarm.** League games: every read is p25 / p50 / p75 / p90 0.20 / 0.25 / 0.35 / 0.55 s
  old. His wide peripheral sector (`qbmind.periphHalf`) keeps re-acquiring receivers at 1.7× the sensing delay.
  So the choice of where to look is made on fairly fresh, but not current, information.
- **A defect in the plan's gaze.** "On the read" was always the back, with his gaze 136° off him:
  - QB_RHYTHM's pressure jump moved `readIdx` to the back but did not close a vertical peek or triangle;
  - so `gazePoint` kept his eyes on the verticals' middle;
  - an unread key held his eyes the same way.

## Change (`HOT_LOOK`, off)

`src/sim/behaviors/qb.ts`:

- **`hotLook`.** The hot target is chosen as before, from his pictures (`bestOption` at urgency 1).
  - A target inside his clear look (`qbmind.clearHalf`, the sector that refreshes a picture at the plain sensing
    delay) is thrown after the windup, as before.
  - Anyone else first needs his eyes there: the head turn at `HEAD_RATE`, an assumed 400°/s, then a glance of
    0.15 + 0.2·(1 − decision-making) + 0.1·(1 − awareness) s.
  - Meanwhile he evades. His gaze stays where it was until the head arrives, so the picture refreshes only then.
  - He is then judged again on the fresh picture. If it has gone, he chooses again, or has no throw in time and
    falls to the existing scramble / throw-away / evade (Flex's hook).
  - Nothing is barred.
- **No blind throws during a look.** A read his eyes have left is not thrown (`startIfFinish`).
- **The plan's gaze follows the pressure jump.** The peek or triangle is closed, and an unread key is no longer
  watched once he has moved off his first read.

Body: release still follows `startThrow`. SHOULDERS_SET, which adds the body turn (`turnTime`), is off in the reference
stack, so after a look the shoulders snap as before. Read with SHOULDERS_SET if the body turn is wanted too.

## Reading it

- **The look does what it should on its population.** Alarm → release grows the tail the NFL has: p75 0.40 → 0.85 s
  and p90 0.65 → 1.35 s with FREE_BOUND on (NFL p75 1.10 s). Hot throws fall by about two thirds, and looks precede
  the rest.
- **The gate holds partly.** With FREE_BOUND on, sacks no longer fall to almost nothing: 0.4% → 1.5%, against 2.6%
  with it off. Engine-37's cards had 4.2% → 0.9%.
- **The free-rusher gap is mostly outside the hot path.** About two thirds of the free-rusher-at-2-yd plays are thrown
  with the alarm never firing: an ordinary read-logic throw, its bar lowered by urgency (`thr − 0.32u`), started
  while the rusher was still farther than the alarm's 0.45 s. Those throws go to the read his eyes are on, so they
  are not the "elsewhere" cheat.
  - The remaining gap to the NFL (throws 81% vs 57%, sacked 5% vs 25%, release p75 0.20 vs 1.10 s) lives there:
    when the bar drops, and what the throw costs then.
  - That is the next diagnosis. It overlaps Flex's extend / throw away / take-the-sack choices.
- **INT went up in the games** (29 → 36, 25 → 34), but this is game divergence, not the flag. The split, gaze
  fix alone vs look alone, was run two ways:
  - League games, FREE_BOUND off, 16 games each: off 29, gaze fix alone 29, look alone 31, both 36.
  - The paired forced-concept panel (engine-39, the same 1,536 plays in every arm), which settles it:

    | Arm | INT | Plays changed vs off | New INT | INT lost |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | Off | 35 | | | |
    | Gaze fix alone | 34 | 6 | 0 | 1 |
    | Look alone | 35 | 17 | 0 | 0 |
    | Both | 34 | 23 | 0 | 1 |

  - No play gains an interception from either part.
