## What the latest build still gets wrong

The current-source Z14 Y still collides with LT's DE for31 World steps (1127.98 impulse in the recorded report) and never loads his assigned Will before Will reaches the back. Z13 LG and Z19 RG still stall in departure and never load Mike. These are approach/access failures, not proof that a secured block is weak. #150 addresses a separate acquired-traffic question and remains held for Run's installed climb versus useful first-level help disposition.

A repeated first-level error is different: after losing hands, Y/H or a tackle keeps chasing the defender or recovering to the installation's original drive normal even though an acquired carrying back establishes another useful side. The original normal is appropriate for the initial strike; it is not a universal grade of a later block. A defender can be behind in that old frame while still screened from the current runner. Globally widening the inline fit gate or rotating every loaded rep therefore gives the wrong correction.

## Small retained correction

Only `RunBlocker.attack` and a private `runnerSideFit` helper change. They reuse #147's observer-owned, normally refreshed work picture and existing bounded `chooseRunBlockFit` movement preview:

1. Admit only free OL or installed inline blockers doing a first-level drive/reach/down/cutoff. Unreleased doubles and pull/climb/lead/stalk jobs retain their rules.
2. Require an acquired positive carrying picture of the installed RB. Installed expectations, unavailable/negative possession and the snap-carrying QB do not activate this recovery. New observations enter only on the existing work-picture beat; repeated decisions hold that picture.
3. Recover to the assigned defender's observed surface facing that back when the existing1.1 lost-leverage boundary is crossed. Preserve a useful runner-facing screen rather than recovering across it to the old normal. Use the same placement error, body clearance, attainable preview, speeds and braking.
4. Do not start this recovery after the acquired runner has passed the observed defender by the existing2.5-yard run-contact continuation boundary. This limits the new objective; it does not release, reassign or renew a job.

The installation still owns target, drive and contact frame. Actual reach, strike acquisition, hands, loaded-contact motor, collection, keeper/release policy, defensive work-off, physics and capacities remain accepted. This is not the loaded-contact/API trial in the archives. A blocker is not guaranteed to regain leverage or hold a defender.
