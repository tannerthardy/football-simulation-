# Play art in the replay viewer (2026-11-17)

A pre-snap view of the call for the person watching, built into `viewer/replay.template.html`. The exporters (`npm run replay`, `npm run film-pack`) now attach `art` to every filmed scrimmage play.

**Source.** `scripts/play-art.ts` is a passive read-only tap on `PlaySim.prototype.tick` (first tick, after `assignBehaviors`). It copies the call (concept, formation, coverage, front, pressure), the alignment's roles (`defAlign.spots[slot].role`, man targets, rush gaps, run fits), the installed receiver routes (`ownRoutes`) and each agent's assigned behavior into plain data. It draws no random numbers and nothing in the simulation reads it: `scripts/play-art-check.ts` shows the same game's log, score and next draw identical with and without the tap (seed 21), and every one of the 120 filmed scrimmage plays matches a sheet.

**View.** Offense: called routes with the break and the QB's read order, the back's job (route, check release, fake, run aim), protection, run blocks (target, track or pull), QB drop. Defense: each zone as its nominal area with the owner tagged, pattern-match seats marked with the rule in words, man lines, rush and blitz paths, run fits on a designed run. A legend and a per-player list of assignments in football words sit under the field; clicking a row isolates that player. `A` or the Play art button toggles it; "Keep play art during the play" leaves it on. Loading a play paused shows it; playing from a clip start holds the pre-snap picture 3.5 s.

**Shown as a rule, not a line (decided after the snap).** Pass-protection pick-ups, a back's check release, pattern-match zone jobs, screen and downfield blocking targets, a run block with no named target.

**Not shown yet.** Pre-snap motion paths (the art is the alignment before motion; the motion is named), a defense's disguise (the coverage drawn is the one played, not the one shown), blitz pick-up responsibilities, the key defender as a marked player (the concept's key is quoted in words), zones as the engine's real shapes (it plays a landmark; the boxes are the textbook area), and plays recorded before this change.

`scripts/play-art-shots.mts FILE.html OUTDIR` screenshots a spread of plays across cover 1/2/3/4/6, play-action, screens and runs for a check.
