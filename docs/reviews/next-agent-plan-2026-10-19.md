## Decision

Continue parallel work, but finish useful corrections already in flight before
adding more experimental systems. The immediate bottleneck is independent
football-contract decisions and delivery of a proven catch correction, not a
shortage of tests or a requirement for a new architecture.

Three team objectives:

1. Turn reviewed blocking-access corrections into useful accepted football.
2. Finish catch-opportunity correctness and adjudicate the existing deep-coverage candidate.
3. Extract useful learning from run-v3 through local causal repairs, without promoting its package.

The target is believable decisions and feasible execution on varied individual
snaps, including legitimate offensive and defensive wins. More rushing yards,
fewer completions or a better pooled NFL distance is not an acceptance rule.
