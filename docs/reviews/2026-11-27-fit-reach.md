# FIT_REACH: fits by where a man stands and who can reach a gap (2026-11-27)

Rules + Coaching, for Integration. Base: rules/lead-back cb07aba93 (merged as #995). The flag is `FIT_REACH` (default
on; `=0` restores FIT_RULES's output exactly). It applies to label-free fits only, in `src/playbook/ir/fits.ts`.
Faithful mode and its equality proof are untouched: `test/ir-fits.test.ts` passes both ways.

## The rule (Integration's ruling)
1. **A front player fits the gap he stands in**, from his position against the blockers at the snap (the five linemen
   and attached ends).
   - A gap is the space between two blockers, counted out from the ball. On a side with no attached end, everything
     outside the tackle is C.
   - **Data wins over position:** an explicit front `fits` entry (control, lag, a front's designed gap) and a called
     blitz gap.
   - **Head-up on a blocker** (within 0.3 yd) he stands in no gap. He keeps the spot's gap when it is one of the two
     beside that blocker: a 6 on the end keeps C. Otherwise he takes the gap beside that blocker toward the spot's gap:
     a backer walked up over the guard, installed at a far gap, plays the near shoulder.
2. **Interior pool:** the fold's own test decides it. A man on or outside the nearest detached receiver on his side,
   zone or man, is force or alley, never an interior fitter.
3. **Which gap:**
   - the nearest leftover gap on his own side;
   - the A gap across the ball only when, in the whole assignment, nothing on his side is left and no other fitter
     stands nearer to it;
   - no two fits cross;
   - a gap no eligible fitter can take stays unowned.

   A defect found while building this: the gap search required every fitter to have a gap whenever any gap was left,
   even one he may not take. Every assignment then failed and the fitters kept stale spot gaps. It now counts only the
   gaps each man may take.

## Addendum (Integration, from an outside review of develop f9e262b24)
1. **`noFold` applied only to forces.** In the build, the fold's relational test (`outside`) gates every candidate for
   an interior gap, force or not:
   - the insert pool (`backers`): a non-force insert outside his side's nearest detached receiver becomes alley;
   - the low-safety pass;
   - the force's fold, as before.

   An outside corner is never in the interior pool. Example: the quarters corner at 6.3 yd, re-film above.
2. **The linemen label in `noFold`.** Under FIT_REACH a detached receiver is a non-back who is not on the line inside
   the core (by fact), so a tackle over or an unbalanced line is not read as one. FIT_REACH=0 keeps the labels.
   **The catalog does not move:** 0 of 4,321 captured snaps change jobs (census).
3. **check.ts: the passer's eligibility.**
   - A quarterback taking the snap within 2 yd of the ball (under center) is ineligible; in the gun or pistol he is a
     back like any other.
   - The snap description carries his depth, not his alignment name, so the test reads the depth.
   - This is checker only. The IR suites pass.
4. **Recorded for next:** a non-carrying back needs an explicit job in the description: lead, fake (path and how long
   he holds it) or check, instead of falling into the stalkers. One field would serve the fullback dive, split-back
   counter and play-action fakes.

**Still riding in `rest` and opaque refs** (the list asked for):
- `PassDescription.rest` (PassConcept less id, name, family, drop, strong, weak and back) holds:
  - `teBlock` and `progression` (by receiver number);
  - `beats` and `minReceivers`;
  - `fake` (a fake id);
  - `screen` (who catches, who releases, tempo, count, receiver release and sell, kill, line set, blocks);
  - `read` (prose) and `needs`;
  - the back-as-#3 fields.
- `RunDescription.rest` (RunConcept less id, name and scheme) holds `direction`, `aim`, `meshTime`, `pullers` and
  `beats`.
- Opaque refs:
  - `Ref<'formation'>` (the offense preset);
  - `Ref<'concept'>` (pass and run presets);
  - `Ref<'route'>` (every route job and the back's route);
  - `Ref<'pass-family'>`, `Ref<'drop'>` and `Ref<'run-scheme'>`;
  - `Ref<'coverage'>` and `Ref<'coverage-family'>`;
  - `Ref<'pressure'>` and `Ref<'front'>`.
- Zone ids (`ZoneId` strings in coverage roles: deep_third_s, curl_flat_w and so on) and the gap letters are vocabulary
  rather than refs. The sim reads zones by id: `isDeepZone` and the flat or curl-flat side tests in fits.ts.
