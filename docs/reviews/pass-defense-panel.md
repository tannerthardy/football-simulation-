## The football diagnosis

The new 28-snap panel has21 catches, one with a later fumble excluded from final
YAC scoring. Six ordinary completed catches gain20+ YAC. Three of those six
have no first physical contact until2.25–4.25 seconds after the catch. Three
others acquire a hold and break it. These are distinct failure families; the
first three cannot establish a weak-grip explanation.

One repeated first decision is repairable before the throw: **blocking evidence
closes a receiver's match key permanently.** In11/28 snaps a rule enters a terminal
`one`/`zoned` branch on an acquired blocking picture, then receives a confirmed
vertical release from that same player without restoring the key. Counting
requires a vertical beyond three yards before a genuine out/under/stop exchange;
brief ambiguous movements are not counted as the defect.

This affects Quarters safety, seam/curl-flat and wall rules. It is particularly
visible on an inline TE: low pads/contact are legitimate blocking evidence,
but do not establish that he will block for the rest of the play. A chip, jam,
delayed release or play action can change what the defender should do next.
The fault is in the permanence of the response, not proof that every early
blocking read was incorrect.
