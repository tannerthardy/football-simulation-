# Paired snap replay, engine-111's run fits (2026-11-27)

Rules + Coaching, for Integration. Tool: `scripts/diag/paired/replay.ts`, on develop 6d1a1dde8. No engine change.

**Method.**
- 16 games: seeds 1-16, the sim-game teams at strength 0.55.
- Captured on two paths: games played with FIT_RULES, FOLD_HOLD and GUARD_KEY all off, and games played on the
  defaults (all on).
- Every scrimmage snap was replayed with A (all three off) and B (all on, or one flag alone). The runs are called runs
  with a rusher.
- Means are A → B. "±" is the paired standard error of B − A. Rates are in percent.
- The cached rows predate the scramble filter, so a passer's scramble on a pass call can be in the run counts (2 plays
  on the on path).

Commands:
```sh
OFF="FIT_RULES=0,FOLD_HOLD=0,GUARD_KEY=0"
npx tsx scripts/diag/paired/replay.ts --games 16 --kind run --out out/paired-e111 --path $OFF --path "" \
  --a $OFF --b "" --key personnel        # then --key scheme; then --b with one flag on: "FOLD_HOLD=0,GUARD_KEY=0" etc.
```
