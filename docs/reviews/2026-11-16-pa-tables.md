# PA_TABLES: a play-action call installed from its concept's tables (2026-11-16)

Rules + Coaching. This is a repair, under an off flag. Integration ruled that it lands on unless the card shows a fault,
and that it goes ahead of the QB lane's bit 16 (PA reads decided on the plant out of the fake). Bit 16 needs the
concept's real key and order to exist first.

## The fault
PA_CALL tags a dropback concept as play action by building a new call: `{...c, id: c.id + '+pa', family: 'playaction',
drop: 'pa', fake}`. The read compiler looks up its install tables by `concept.id`:
- KEYS, ROLE_KEYS, SECOND_KEYS / THIRD_KEYS and DANGER;
- INSTALL_ORDER / ROLE_ORDER and BACK_AFTER_KEY;
- ONE_LOOK and CLEAR_OUTS.

So `smash+pa`, `quick_outs+pa` and the rest missed their concept's coached key and order, and compiled from geometry
alone.

**Measured** (`installedPlan`, 29 dropback concepts × gun doubles, gun trips and ace 12, develop defaults):
- **Off:** 57 of 87 PA-tagged plans differ from the concept's own plan.
  - Quick outs and slant-flat lose their keys.
  - Drive's key falls to S2/S1 from W2/S2.
  - Stick, levels and hitches lose their orders.
- **On:** 10 differ. Those are drop timing and backside order (W1/W2) only.

## The change
`tableId(concept)` (compile.ts) strips `+pa` when the flag is on. The compiler and the runtime look up every table by it.
The cache key carries the flag. Untagged calls are unchanged.
