## What was measured before anything was built

**The "moves off a come-off" gap was mostly not a reaction.** The earlier row said an NFL quarterback moves 0.73-0.99 yd
away in the 0.5 s after a rusher comes off his blocker, ours 0.06-0.18.

- In the NFL 63% of come-offs find him already running (3+ yd/s) and 29% moving at 1-3 yd/s; 7% find him still. In 7 of
  12 traced plays the rusher leaves his blocker because the quarterback has already left.
- A still NFL quarterback moves 0.5+ yd away 12% of the time (25% when the rusher stays off and closes). Ours 0-10%.
- On our side 11-16% of flagged come-offs have the rusher more than 2 yd from every lineman 0.4 s later (NFL 65%);
  the rest sit at arm's length. 30-40% of the 0.5 s after-windows are cut by the throw on both sides.

**What picks his direction is the coached rule.** First DL/LB within 3 yd after the drop, ball held 0.5 s more, he moves
1+ yd (NFL n 854):

| Body | Up | Lateral | Back |
|---|---|---|---|
| At the edge (wide of him by 1.5 yd, no more than 1 yd in front of his depth) | 59-67% | 29-36% | 4-5% |
| In front or inside | 23% | 56-58% | 19-21% |

- It is not "away from the body" (within 45 degrees of straight away: 20-22%) and not "the most open grass" (the most
  open of eight directions: 11-15%, chance 12%).
- A lateral slide goes to the side he later throws to 96% of the time, but that is measured after the fact. The only
  pre-decision proxy, the side his chest faced over the prior 0.5 s, predicts the slide 66% of the time.
- He responds to a body behind his facing at about three quarters of the frontal rate (48 against 58% at 2 yd).

**The ordinary "not still" is the hitch.** On throws from the pocket, between his deepest point and the motion, the NFL
quarterback moves at a median 1.0 yd/s (under 0.5 yd/s 25% of the time), about 0.5 yd net and mostly up. Ours stood
(under 0.5 yd/s 61-78%). QB_GATHER reproduces the speed distribution. Its path is wrong: see "Open" below.

## Three builds

1. **A reset decision on a time cause** (`docs/reviews/patches/2026-11-13-qb-pocket-attempt1.patch`). No change in his
   movement. A reset was decided on 28% of dropbacks, but two thirds fired on "he cannot finish his read and a throw in
   time", which is a quarterback about to throw: the windup began 0.05-0.35 s later.
2. **A side hitch** (`...attempt2.patch`). No change. The hitch is 0.5-1.0 yd at a slow pace, too small to be a slide.
3. **One decision on the nearest body he feels** (the flag as it stands). The re-diagnosis after two misses found what
   neither had touched: `pocketMove` climbs whenever any felt defender is wide of him within 4-5.5 yd, which a
   defensive end satisfies on most snaps, so every move of a yard was that climb whatever was in front of him.

## Two things a reader of the card must not misread

**He decides to slide more often than the NFL moves, and is seen to move less.**

- A slide is decided on 38 / 33% of dropbacks (4 games a seed, after the trigger was tightened to "free, or in a
  blocker's hands and closing"; 40 / 35% before, so the tightening barely bites: a blocked rusher inside 3 yd is nearly
  always still being walked back at him).
- 58-64% of slides are still under way when the ball leaves, and completed ones cover 0.4-0.85 yd.
- Counted as an observer would (the operator's 1+ yd in 0.5 s), he moves on 18 / 9% of events, about 4-8% of dropbacks,
  against the NFL's 41% of events, about 14% of dropbacks. The move starts about 0.6 s before the throw and is small.

**A slide is a free action for the throw.** Throws released during or within 0.3 s of a slide complete 68-78% against
68-69% for the rest. The slide rarely reaches the 2.2 yd/s where `missSigma` charges a moving throw. That is not evidence
the slide is right; the price belongs to the on-the-move throw mechanics in the out-of-pocket work.

**Seed 201's "back" share (45-60% of 10-18 events) is `evade()`**, not a walk back to his set point: ticks moving back
at 1+ yd/s while set and not sliding were 589 / 555 with a read against 61 / 33 out of reads. Under the alarm with no
throw he steps straight away from a rusher in front of him. The NFL also goes back on 19-21% of these.

## QB_HONEST_FEEL

Four reads of a rusher's true position in the quarterback's pressure code now use his picture: the behind-his-shoulder
test in `felt()` and FREE_BOUND's lag (his plain picture of the man), the distance `pressure()` reports (the felt
position), and where `evade()` steps from (the felt position). Per the later sweep the distance only fed a statistic and
FREE_BOUND's lag is dormant by default; the shoulder test and `evade()` are live.

Off to on, seeds 101 / 201 x 8 games, default engine-57: sack or loss 2.5 to 1.9% and 3.5 to 3.9%; time to throw p50
2.50 to 2.50 and 2.60 to 2.60 s; completion 74.1 to 75.0 and 69.5 to 67.7%; alarmed dropbacks 4% both.

On the throws that beat contact (4 games, 13-19 contacts, so thin): contact then threw 74% / sacked 21% off, 69% / 31%
on; a windup already under way at contact 86% and 89%. Those throws are ordinary reads on a bar lowered by felt
pressure (urgency p50 0.60; half would not clear his normal bar; 7% came through the alarm), started with the nearest
defender truly 1.1-2.0 yd away and a felt time to contact of 0.37-0.49 s against a 0.35 s windup. His picture runs
about 0.25 s late; nothing true-state is in that path. The motion's immunity to a rusher arriving is Flex's physics.
