# TURN_SPLIT: a request that turns him does not ask him to slow down (Tools, 2026-11-16, engine-73 candidate 13b18010c, flag off)

Defect, from the open-field turn-cost table (`scripts/diag/route-speed/turn_cost.py`): a request is a target velocity, and `locomotionAccel` reads it as a vector difference, so a request phi off his heading asks for a tangential change of |req| cos(phi) - v, which brakes him by v (1 - cos(phi)) / dt before the friction ellipse scales both components. The request's mean angle off his velocity is 6 / 18 / 29 / 48 degrees at 0-20 / 20-40 / 40-60 / 60-100 deg/s.

Change (`TURN_SPLIT=1`, off by default, `src/physics/kinematics.ts`): the tangential demand is blended, by cos(phi) clamped to 0..1, between the demand toward the requested SPEED, (|req| - v)/dt, and today's projection. The normal demand, the drive and brake limits, the lateral ellipse and the support limits are untouched; at phi of 90 degrees and beyond it is today's demand, and the blend is continuous in phi. No constant was fitted.
