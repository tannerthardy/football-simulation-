Sections C completion, D2 and12 say “independently selected” and “predeclared variation” but do not explicitly bind the ordinary transfer constructor selection to a rule before candidate outcomes.

Add one compact requirement:
“Before tuning, name the development witness, exposure-based transfer rule and adverse/discipline control. Keep inactive/rejected selections visible; do not replace a failed transfer with a favorable ending. Advance an inactive selection only under the same declared activation rule.”

This can remain a PR paragraph, not a new preregistration or ledger. It makes native-transfer verification capable of rejecting overfit claims. A second cherry-picked play is otherwise too easily labelled independent.

### 2. High/medium: make the coverage production milestone explicitly conditional on a free implementation slot

The WIP/calendar model is careful, but the difficulty table says “working bounded pilot this week” while the early three slots may remain occupied by INFO/contact/Run for3–5days. COV-6 attribution1–2days plus a COV-7 prototype2–3days may not fit after that.

Do not create an extra policy lane to force the schedule. The end-of-week table already allows pending/controlled/parked results; align the visible milestone with that contingency.
