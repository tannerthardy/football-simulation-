## Correction 1: clarify what “candidate-active opposing case” requires

Section8's sentence is directionally correct:
“The candidate's own active opposing case matters; activation under the old permissive diagnostic is not automatically activation under the new policy.”

It should explicitly avoid requiring the candidate to abandon man on the particular published PA fixture. That pair has equal own bodies and Z pictures at initial admission, but QB/back motion and confidence differ. A candidate may legitimately retain man because of those available inputs. Requiring it to be fooled on that specific play would encourage artificial policy weakening.

Suggested addition:

> Here active means that the candidate actually consumes the disputed cues and evaluates the relevant role option. A correct choice to retain man is an active discipline result. If claiming the candidate's own abandonment/recovery behavior is safe, also supply a challenge where it really takes support before a later threatening release; do not substitute an inactive ordinary pass. The permissive diagnostic remains the mechanism's reference cost, not a requirement that every candidate repeat its mistake.

The already written information-boundary warning is correct and must remain. An acquired-RB-only candidate makes this PA pass arm inactive for support; that is not active evidence about HB-pass safety.

## Correction 3: bound Run's ordinary transfer requirement

Section7's “useful declared-native example and independently selected ordinary constructor transfer” is a good evidence goal. It needs a stopping interpretation because the existing ordinary prefix found no qualified useful departure under a narrower screen, and the latest native WZ example still lacks timely useful backer access.

Do not require a favorable ordinary sample to exist and then keep searching until it appears. A supported family with normal live controllers can be a meaningful bounded capability even when current ordinary call selection rarely exposes it.

Suggested addition:

> Predeclare a small exposure-based ordinary transfer sample and retain absence of an eligible opportunity, inactivity and adverse results. A useful normal-controller declared-family result supports that capability only. Claiming improvement in ordinary-game football additionally requires active ordinary transfer. If the fixed sample lacks it, narrow the claim and preserve the hold rather than expanding seeds or forcing a successful ending.

If the intent is to require ordinary active benefit before adopting the entire experimental Run policy, that is reasonable for this prototype, but distinguish that production policy gate from declaring every component correction invalid. A correct preview-status/interface repair can be verified independently without claiming the whole combination capability is ready.
