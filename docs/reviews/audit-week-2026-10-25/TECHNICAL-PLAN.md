## 1. Recommendation and intended result

Use the week to finish a small set of football capabilities end to end. Preserve role-specific football logic. The next investment is coherent acquired evidence, responsibility transitions, achievable movement and contact—not a universal decision brain.

The original audit's useful sequence remains intact: evidence and input identity first; demonstrated information/action/contact seams next; then adaptation with an active opposing cost. Phase-one tools are delivered. The project should now use them to ship or decisively bound football behavior.

Three results matter most:

1. Defenders distinguish a real QB-running threat from an ordinary pocket adjustment, and a tackle launch uses the player's information while contact admission uses physical truth.
2. One supported zone-combination family produces useful first-level protection and timely second-level contribution through actual preparation, traffic and movement.
3. Coverage makes a coherent technique transition and tests a local support option against a real deception/recovery cost.

These are mechanism and supported-capability goals. They do not promise NFL-average closure, a fixed number of merged PRs, or that every nominated play improves. At least one active native opportunity and its adverse/discipline case must support a claimed football improvement. A correct local repair can move final yards in either direction.

The inexpensive supporting deliveries are independent verification, truthful trait channels, and a narrowly scoped QB self-arrival correction when a football slot opens. A small native push/preparation proof belongs in the week if it shares the current contact work; a broad shove-policy program does not.

The common decision hierarchy is conceptual and testable: installed responsibility → currently credible acquired evidence → permitted contribution and its cost → finite committed action → feedback/recovery. Each role owns its football choices. Keep the original valid action available, retire obsolete action state coherently, and allow credible deception or imperfect execution to produce mistakes. Existing trait-dependent timing/control should carry individual variation; do not add a separate success roll to every coaching point or require every player to make the same perfect read.

## 3. Important corrections to the plan's premises

### 3.1 Descriptions and reviews are claims to check

A PR can be mostly sound and contain a wrong explanatory sentence, weak negative control or incomplete model. Source and deciding experiments outrank titles, confidence labels and test counts.

Four independent planning reviewers inspected actual current/pending source, patches and discussions. Their notes are contact.md, run-coverage.md, info-strategy.md and validation.md under inputs/. Run's final review packet was decoded and its archive/JSON/patch hashes verified. That verifies the inspected source delivery; it does not independently reproduce its reported native outcomes. This planning pass did not run another broad simulation campaign.

### 3.2 The “held pursuit budget” is not currently a demonstrated cache bug

At accepted9ace, chase computes distance/effort and calls pursuitPoint every executed update. pursuitPoint recomputes interceptPoint; its persistent angleBias is not a cached interception/request.

The possible discrepancy is that an estimate assumes today's reduced effort throughout its future horizon, while the actual controller later raises effort inside 12 yd. That is a future request-policy assumption, not automatically stale stored state. First identify the exact consumed mismatch; do not implement cache invalidation for a cache that has not been found.

Likewise, current docs' suggestion that distance pacing explains the entire adverse P002 touchdown remains a hypothesis. Earlier passive execution records showed the first-whiff difference depended on different consumed draws, not the small probability change alone. Neither pacing nor randomness has been isolated as the sole cause of the entire ending.

### 3.3 Tackle policy and physical truth now meet in the same file

#273 adds a pre-contact lunge/dive choice inside tryTackles, still rooted in live sim.carrier. Obtaining a delayed picture after choosing the hidden current holder is not necessarily an acquired target-selection boundary.

Its lunge forecast consumes a Perceived picture, while beginLunge calls strike, which aims from live target position/velocity. The planned movement and emitted movement can therefore use different information.

These are precise source risks to verify with hidden-state invariance tests. Actual World contact and possession resolution must still use truth. The remedy is a small actor-owned launch/admission contract, not making physics operate on imagined bodies.

### 3.4 Geometry names do not establish attachment or grip semantics

#273's layout and trailing-foot proxies use .72×height and up to .25×height. Those are reduced-model assumptions, not measured NFL landmarks. Current World can retain a noncenter foot/reference attachment, including in legacy support. A comment describing the grasp simply as center-to-center is incomplete.

The candidate gripQuality normalizes differently from the physical admission envelope. That may represent grip versus wrap control deliberately; it cannot be accepted or rejected just by its name. Do not restore an old quality number to obtain preferred yardage. The earlier P013 quality/span rollback hypothesis is closed.

### 3.5 Current Run machinery already exists

The final local Run prototype connects departure to plannedClimb; adds direct/detour/unvalidated access, feedback previews, retained escape side, loaded tangent preparation and scoped state retirement. Rebuilding those components would repeat completed work.

Its latest corrected film shows actual helper load, adverse release momentum, and later teammate-spacing intervention. Those are the next factual boundaries. A full future path has not been validated merely because an unconstrained meeting-point calculation says attainable.

## 12. Verification that can reject a confident wrong result

Each material package has three different responsibilities:
- implementer supplies the mechanism and exact reproducible handoff;
- an independent verifier reproduces a decisive positive and strongest opposing case;
- the owning football/Physics lane checks responsibility, cue legality and physical meanings.

Tanner is used for unresolved football judgment, not as the first actor-identity or command-stage debugger. Routine details do not trigger an approval ceremony.

### Required handoff

Use the existing PR and tools. No new workboard or tracked receipt format is needed. Include:
1. actual accepted base, candidate and composed dependencies;
2. Node 22.23.3, lockfile/resolved settings, intended defaults;
3. exact constructor/hash and stable playerId/team/slot/index;
4. controller-start, event and physical-endpoint clocks with units;
5. claim, active opportunity, strongest opposing result and remaining uncertainty;
6. first consumed cue/choice/request and actual downstream/physical consequence;
7. fetchable small artifact/code and reproduction command.

A private out path or commit SHA without accessible inputs is not a completed transfer. Use an existing approved artifact/evidence channel. Avoid enormous multipart base64 threads; #255's merged timeline can fail transport despite the data being available through small-page reads.

### Tests that matter

- Hidden-state invariance: fix actually consumed evidence/own state and alter an unobserved fact. Only migrated decisions are certified by that test; actual physics/rules may change.
- Active exposure: show the candidate reaches the relevant choice and changes a consumed command when claiming benefit/cost. An inactive pass is an unchanged control, not deception safety.
- Observer neutrality: compare recorder off/on within one arm. Differences between baseline and treatment are expected, not restoration failures.
- Native transfer: use an independently selected constructor. Development cases are no longer blind holdouts. Scripted scenario capability is labelled honestly.
- Physical coherence: delivered contact/budget/point/work and supported motion, with a relevant miss/slip/recovery control.
- Trait truthfulness: identify the changed intermediate channel; higher rating need not win every play.

A lexical information guard is useful lint. It does not certify aliases, all ball-phase fields, whole allowed offense/physics files or a newly introduced policy inside tackle.ts. Shrink actual debt with semantic checks; do not expand a guard into a new global analyzer this week.

### Test tiers and randomness

Use focused checks while iterating. Once a production mechanism is stable, run PLAN's applicable quick six-game comparison and let CI supply the12-game PR tier. Reuse compatible finished cards. Run a second independent seed when making a general whole-game effect claim. A full48-game tier is for a large fix or a named unresolved composition/population question, not an automatic reward for finishing a diagnostic.

Do not rerun thousands of matched plays after every cue edit. Preserve the existing matched dataset and rerun the few deciding cases first.

Same seed does not preserve draw assignment after play divergence. Copy actual consumed probability/draw records where needed; do not reroll to get paired-looking endings. Explain the earliest mechanism before discussing final points/YPC/YAC.

### Stop or narrow

After two rejected mechanism attempts, change the hypothesis or stop the narrow treatment. Do not expand the seed search until it wins. A negative finding closes a question; it does not earn another week on the same corridor.

Accept a coherent explicitly reduced model with meaningful opposing controls when external detail is unavailable. Do not demand perfect anatomy, every hidden-state migration, or a population NFL match before any bounded football capability can progress.
