## What explains the wobble?

Several different failures can look like a player changing his mind. Treating
them as fresh random noise, or filtering all turns, would hide real assignments,
reactions and cuts. Saved native requests, selected aims, consumed pictures and
achieved bodies distinguish the following:

| Mechanism | Native/mechanical evidence | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body-turn motor rings after a steady request | A fixed90° command overshoots22.5° and reverses without contact or RNG. | Existing draft#162, isolated Physics review. |
| Gait uses a pose the body has not attained | Requesting yaw temporarily rotates the acceleration frame before finite yaw executes. | Independent attained-pose correction for Physics review. |
| Loaded lateral controller renews a stiff deadline | Same held shoulder/flow produces repeated large opposing requests. | Corrected ordinary run-rep arrival, independently reviewable. |
| Motion prediction follows a frozen point | Matching acquired flow can still chase the point's old side. | Caught in review; final contact candidate holds point, flow and adoption time together. |
| Free run-fit planner changes before its destination | IZ/Counter activate a new mode against the preceding `SnapMind.aim`, then reverse when the new point arrives. | Small atomic-adoption candidate retained; promotion held for arrival/football review. |
| Tracking switches steering formula at four yards | Same picture at4±1e−8yd gives a2.107yd/s,19.89° jump. | Two proposed repairs held after opposing controls. |
| Run-flow selection has a hard threshold | OZ24 observed lateral speed crosses1.5yd/s; desired fit jumps23.267→27.988yd and later back. | Separate target-selection defect, not repaired by mode coupling. |

These witnesses explain repeated **request** defects, not the whole claimed
1–1.5 reversals/second population rate. Physical bodies damp many commands.
A braking request opposing current motion, a real cut or a newly acquired threat
can be legitimate. NFL tracking supplies position/heading evidence, not requests
or gaze. No global smoothing filter, lower braking, faster players, altered
hands/grips, hidden route/possession access or forced tackle endings were added.
