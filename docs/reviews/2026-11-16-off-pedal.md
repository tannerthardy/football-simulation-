Defense lane, QCORNER (quarters MOD corner). Integration ruled that the pedal fix is shared across every off-aligned
man technique, and that its exit and QCORNER bit 4 are one piece.

Two flags, both numeric and default 0 (off):

| Flag | Bit | Code |
|---|---|---|
| `OFF_PEDAL` | 1 | `src/sim/behaviors/defense.ts`, `ManCoverage` |
| `QCORNER` | 4 | `src/sim/coverage/rules.ts` `qCorner`; `src/sim/coverage/techniques.ts` `QuarterShort` |

Shared code: `src/sim/coverage-leverage.ts` (`StemAcceleration`, `projectedStem`, `stemStopped`).

**Integration's ruling, 2026-11-16:**

  combined arm.
- QCORNER bit 4 stays in the code behind its bit and is not carded: a rule that fires on n 2 can't be read.
- The trade is about 0.5 yd of cushion at the throw on the quick game.
- Two follow-ups before bit 4 can come back:

## What was wrong

`out/diag/cd/pedal-trace.mts`, engine-71, the existing pedal. Quarters corners n 507, off-man n 251. Medians, upfield
yd/s:

| t | #1 true | seen (delayed) | request | achieved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 1.25 | 0.00 | −0.32 | −0.32 |
| 0.7 | 2.12 | 0.79 | 0.47 | 0.45 |
| 0.9 | 3.09 | 1.71 | −3.87 (off-man) | 0.54 |
| 1.0 | 3.53 | 2.14 | −3.02 (off-man) | −0.15 |

- **The sight lag is honest.** It is his perception delay, about 0.3 s. The route-observation filter adds none.
- **His request ran 0.32 below the seen stem, and forward before it.** The pre-release floor `a.pos.x − 0.2` pulled him
  upfield.
- **On his release read, CUSHION_NEED's close became a forward lunge at a climbing receiver.** This is a step function
  at a gate, and it is why he stood and then stalled.

## The change

**OFF_PEDAL bit 1: a repair of the existing pedal.**
- His pre-release floor is his own spot.
- Until the shared stem-stop read says the stem has stopped, his target is no nearer the line than he is. He spends
  excess cushion by pedalling slower than the stem, never by coming forward.
- With it, his request equals the seen stem tick for tick. He no longer stands, creeps or lunges.
- He has no pace of his own. The read step on the snap (moving before the stem reaches his picture) is left open.

**The stem-stop read** (one stated form; Integration ruled there was no shared break read before):
- It is CARRY_READ's projection of the receiver's upfield speed 0.5 s ahead, from the acceleration in his own delayed
  samples.
- He reads "stopped" below 1 yd/s, and "climbing" again only above 3 (CARRY_READ's endpoints).
- `StemAcceleration` was moved out of DeepCoverage unchanged. Flag-off output is byte-identical (2-game export md5
  e0ab70c5…, before and after).
- Later consolidations, each a separate flagged change: CUSHION_BREAK's re-square, and CARRY_READ itself.

**QCORNER bit 4.** A quarters MOD corner who has zoned off a short #1 (`zoneOffOne`) plays #1's path from his own
picture, instead of the deep quarter's drop:
- #1 stays on his side: he settles at his depth, outside-in, eyes on the QB.
- #1 crosses (2 yd or more inside his alignment in his picture; final once declared): he sinks and works inside to the
  window 5 yd over the crosser. The 5 yd is a stated choice.
- His break on the throw is the base technique's (`playsThrow` → `playBall`).

## Not shown, and limits

- **The "#1 short" sink does not measure bit 4.**
  - Our hitch stops at about 2.0–2.2 s, later than the NFL's, so "sink from 1.0 to 2.0 s" is mostly the pedal before
    the stop.
  - `QuarterShort` rarely fires: on hitches the corner is still in man at the throw (man-pedal 78%). Under the existing
    MOD rule, a short stop with no cross stays his man (`zoneOffOne`), so the zone-off seldom triggers.
  - Bit 4's settle/sink is therefore barely exercised, and the crosses class has n 2.
- **Double-move cost is unmeasured.** The pool has no double move (sluggo, hitch-and-go, out-and-up), which is what a
  stop read is most exposed to. Smash and the vertical set stand in. Integration is asking Rules whether a sourced
  double-move concept belongs in the pool. Nothing is added speculatively; a DB's protection against it is coached.
- **The read step on the snap** (the NFL corner's 0.5 yd/s at 0.5 s, before he can see the stem) is not modelled.
