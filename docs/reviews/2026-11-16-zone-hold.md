# ZONE_HOLD: commit margins on three zone-coverage choices (C14, 2026-11-16)

Defense lane. Flag `ZONE_HOLD`: a numeric bitmask, default 0 (off). The code is in
`src/sim/behaviors/defense.ts` (`seenQbEyes`, ZoneCoverage threat band, deep vertical test,
underneath lane pick).

## Question

The commit rule (CLAUDE.md) says a goal persists until a perceived change clears a margin, and
that re-scoring the same picture every tick is not a new decision.

On exact pictures, `scripts/diag/defense/zone-flips.mts` found zone defenders breaking that rule
in two choices:
- whom they read the QB looking at;
- which receiver they are playing.

Rates with the flag off:
- **Read of the QB's eyes:** changes 1.6–2.0 times a second. About a tenth of those reverse
  within 0.3 s.
- **Flat defender's target:** 27% of his changes reverse within 0.3 s.

## Rule as coached

A zone dropper reads the QB and plays the receiver who threatens his zone. He stays with that
read until he sees it change:
- the QB's head clearly turns to another receiver; or
- the receiver clearly leaves his zone; or
- another receiver clearly becomes the bigger threat.

He does not trade receivers back and forth while the picture is unchanged.

## The bits

Each bit is read alone. All margins are stated choices, read at three levels (bit 4 halves
them, bit 8 doubles them).

| Bit | Choice held | Enter | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (band) | A receiver's membership in the zone's lateral band, from his pictured position 0.7 s ahead | inside the band by 0.5 yd | outside it by 1.5 yd |
| 1 (band), deep zones | The vertical test | past 7.5 yd, or climbing faster than 1.5 yd/s | inside 6.5 yd and slower than 0.5 yd/s |
| 2 (eyes) | Whom he reads the QB looking at | another receiver's bearing beats the held one's by 4° | the QB's pictured head turns clear of the held cone by 6° |
| 16 (pick) | The underneath lane pick (receiver nearest his landmark, weighted toward the QB's eyes) | another receiver's lane score beats the held one's by 1.5 yd | — |

Under bit 16 the held receiver also stays eligible 1.5 yd past the zone radius.

Bit 16 was added after the first panel. Bit 1 alone did not change target reversals, because
the flips come from the per-tick argmin in the lane pick, not from band membership.

How to read these:
- **The eyes hold costs nothing on a real switch.** The 0.30 s is his perception delay: real
  head turns clear the margin at once.
- **Pick (16)** cuts the flat target reversals 0.27 → 0.08 and hook 0.12 → 0.05, at no delay.
- **Band (1)** keeps a leaving receiver about 0.2 s longer. Its only gain is on the hook
  defender (0.05 → 0.02 with pick on). That is a real cost for a small benefit.
- **Curl-flat target** stays at 0.10–0.14 at every level. Its flips come from the branches
  ahead of the lane pick (seam carry, curl-flat widen, flat expand), whose own entry
  thresholds are single-sided. That is the next place to look.

**Look-offs.** Measured as eyes on another receiver 0.3 s before the throw. They occur on only
12–13 plays without QB_LOOKOFF_RHYTHM. At every level, 5 of 20 underneath droppers are reading
the decoy at the throw, so no change can be read.

The point to test: a dropper who holds his read should be more movable by a real look-off, not
less, because a real look-off is a large, held head turn and clears the margin. This needs
re-checking once QB_LOOKOFF_RHYTHM is on (engine-70 card).

## Bits 32 and 64: the zone-settle reversal (2026-11-16)

**What Tools found.** Tools' zone-settle trace is `docs/reviews/2026-11-16-zone-settle-trace.md` on
claude/route-speed-diag. The settler's body matches the isolated body, so his slowing is in his own request: 45% of
settlers reverse it, and only those slow. Tools' candidate cause was the arrival brake against a moving target.

**Per-tick target trace.** `scripts/diag/defense/settle-flip.mts`.
- Population: every non-screen pass × cover2/3/3-match/4/6/tampa2 × two formations × both flips, seed 1.
- 1,389 underneath zone defenders, 2–9 yd off at the snap, on throws at 1.0 s or later.
- A reversal is two ticks whose request headings differ by 90° or more, in the last 0.5 s.
- Each reverser is classed by his first reversal. The table is at ZONE_HOLD=18; the speed change is the achieved
  speed change over the half second (p50 over every defender: −0.13).

| class | share | what moves | achieved speed change |
|---|---|---|---|
| technique switch | 53% | the label changes and the target jumps 4.6 yd | −1.03 |
| target jumped, same label | 30% | 2.8 yd lateral; the QB's eyes move from one receiver to another (106 of 153) | −0.63 |
| target crossed him (arrival brake) | 16% | 0.2 yd, at 0.5 yd/s | −0.43 |

Overall reversal rate: 38% (off), 36% (18), 35% (19). The existing holds barely change it. Tools' arrival brake is
the minor class.

**Bit 32 (lean).**
- The eyes-step class has two layers:
  - The settle target leaned toward whichever receiver the eyes were on.
  - The QB's head itself jumps: `qb.ts` sets `a.head` to the gaze point every tick, and HEAD_RATE only times
    decisions.
- With bit 32 the lean follows the QB's seen head line on a 1 yd leash. That is the taught "slide with the QB's
  eyes", read from the head itself rather than from the identity of a receiver.
- Within-settle jumps: n 102 → 85, step 2.86 → 2.27 yd, achieved change −0.58 → −0.34 (ZONE_HOLD=50 vs 18).
- The remaining step is the head snapping. That is A1, with the QB lane.

**Why carries end.** `scripts/diag/defense/carry-end.mts`, 169 carry ends in the last 0.5 s:

| why the carry ends | share | carryable again within 0.3 s |
|---|---|---|
| seen speed ≤ 4.5 (the route breaking down) | 67% | 7 of 113 |
| hand-off depth (carryTo) reached | 31% | 0 of 52 |
| receiver gone from his threats | 2% | — |

- These are perceived changes, so the seams are legitimate.
- The fault is the goal that follows. It is a lane spot or landmark shallower than he is, aimed at in one tick
  while he is gaining depth at 5–7 yd/s.

**Bit 64 (hand-off from motion).**
- On a hand-off, the new target may move anywhere across his motion.
- It may not come back against his motion short of his stopping point, `v²/(2·0.8·brake)` along the direction he was
  moving.
- The limit is continuous in his own speed and ends when he has stopped along it.
- The plant (landmark → settle on the seen QB set) is a decision and is excluded.

Measured, ZONE_HOLD=114 (18 + 32 + 64) against 50, seed 1:
- Reversals: 36% → 32%. Technique switches: 55% → 44%, with achieved change −0.97 → −0.75.
- Carry-seam → lane reversals: 37 → 5.
- Achieved change at a carry's end: speed-drop endings −0.56 → +0.09; hand-off-depth endings −1.19 → −0.93.

Walk-back, Integration's check: 110 hand-offs from a carry with 1.0+ s before the throw.
- At +1.0 s he is 1.2 yd deeper than at the hand-off (0.7 with bit 64 off). His depth velocity there is 0.07,
  against −0.92 off.
- Distance to his landmark grows 3.0 → 4.3 yd. Only 3 of 110 walk back.
- He settles where he stopped and does not return to the spot.
- He now settles about 1.2 yd deeper than the hand-off point. The underneath depth below is unchanged.

**Not fixed by bit 64.**
- lane → zone-settle: 20 reversals at −1.01. His lane motion was mostly lateral or upward, so the limit along his
  motion rarely binds.
- "Target crossed him": 0.17 → 0.22 of reversers. More defenders now arrive at their stop point at walking pace.
