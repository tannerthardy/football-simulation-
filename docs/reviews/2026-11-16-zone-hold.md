# ZONE_HOLD: commit margins on three zone-coverage choices (C14, 2026-11-16)

Defense lane. Flag `ZONE_HOLD`: a numeric bitmask, default 0 (off). The code is in
`src/sim/behaviors/defense.ts` (`seenQbEyes`, ZoneCoverage threat band, deep vertical test,
underneath lane pick).

## Question

The commit rule (CLAUDE.md) says a goal persists until a perceived change clears a margin, and
that re-scoring the same picture every tick is not a new decision.

On exact pictures, `scripts/diag/defense/zone-flips.mts` found zone defenders breaking that rule
in two choices:
- whom they read the QB looking at;
- which receiver they are playing.

Rates with the flag off:
- **Read of the QB's eyes:** changes 1.6–2.0 times a second. About a tenth of those reverse
  within 0.3 s.
- **Flat defender's target:** 27% of his changes reverse within 0.3 s.

## Rule as coached

A zone dropper reads the QB and plays the receiver who threatens his zone. He stays with that
read until he sees it change:
- the QB's head clearly turns to another receiver; or
- the receiver clearly leaves his zone; or
- another receiver clearly becomes the bigger threat.

He does not trade receivers back and forth while the picture is unchanged.

## The bits

Each bit is read alone. All margins are stated choices, read at three levels (bit 4 halves
them, bit 8 doubles them).

| Bit | Choice held | Enter | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (band) | A receiver's membership in the zone's lateral band, from his pictured position 0.7 s ahead | inside the band by 0.5 yd | outside it by 1.5 yd |
| 1 (band), deep zones | The vertical test | past 7.5 yd, or climbing faster than 1.5 yd/s | inside 6.5 yd and slower than 0.5 yd/s |
| 2 (eyes) | Whom he reads the QB looking at | another receiver's bearing beats the held one's by 4° | the QB's pictured head turns clear of the held cone by 6° |
| 16 (pick) | The underneath lane pick (receiver nearest his landmark, weighted toward the QB's eyes) | another receiver's lane score beats the held one's by 1.5 yd | — |

Under bit 16 the held receiver also stays eligible 1.5 yd past the zone radius.

Bit 16 was added after the first panel. Bit 1 alone did not change target reversals, because
the flips come from the per-tick argmin in the lane pick, not from band membership.

How to read these:
- **The eyes hold costs nothing on a real switch.** The 0.30 s is his perception delay: real
  head turns clear the margin at once.
- **Pick (16)** cuts the flat target reversals 0.27 → 0.08 and hook 0.12 → 0.05, at no delay.
- **Band (1)** keeps a leaving receiver about 0.2 s longer. Its only gain is on the hook
  defender (0.05 → 0.02 with pick on). That is a real cost for a small benefit.
- **Curl-flat target** stays at 0.10–0.14 at every level. Its flips come from the branches
  ahead of the lane pick (seam carry, curl-flat widen, flat expand), whose own entry
  thresholds are single-sided. That is the next place to look.

**Look-offs.** Measured as eyes on another receiver 0.3 s before the throw. They occur on only
12–13 plays without QB_LOOKOFF_RHYTHM. At every level, 5 of 20 underneath droppers are reading
the decoy at the throw, so no change can be read.

The point to test: a dropper who holds his read should be more movable by a real look-off, not
less, because a real look-off is a large, held head turn and clears the margin. This needs
re-checking once QB_LOOKOFF_RHYTHM is on (engine-70 card).

## Checks

- Dither budget with ZONE_HOLD=18: 2 of 2 passed.
- Flag off: every change is guarded by its bit, so ZONE_HOLD=0 leaves the off path unchanged.

## Recommendation

Bits 2 + 16 (ZONE_HOLD=18) for a card arm. Report bit 1 separately: on this panel it buys
little and delays the release of a receiver leaving the zone.
