Defense lane. Tools' information-guard refresh (#699, b5274f75) flagged four reads in `src/sim/behaviors/loose-seen.ts`.

## The four reads

| Read | What it does | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `recordLoose`: `sim.ball.kind !== 'loose'` | Stores the true flight of the loose ball each tick. Each player then samples it at his own observation delay, in his view and with a clear line (`sightLoose` → `sawAt`). | **Producer.** It is the store a player's own sight reads from. No player decides on it. Kept. |
| `looseSeenStart`: kind and `fumbledBy` | Records who lost the ball. That man recognizes the loose ball at once. | **Kept for the fumbler**, who felt it leave. But `lostBy` was also used for other players (next rows). |
| `LooseCue`: `if (sim.ball.kind === 'loose')` around his sighting and the call | Only opens his own sighting, which then decides. But it closes the moment the ball is truly recovered. | **Cheat (timing).** A player whose delayed picture still shows the ball loose stopped recognizing it the tick someone else picked it up. |
| `LooseScramble`: the same gate, plus `sim.see(a, s.lostBy)` for a called player with no sighting | A player who heard 'ball!' but had not seen the ball ran to where he saw the true fumbler. | **Cheat (knowledge).** A shout tells him a ball is loose and where the shout came from. It does not tell him who lost it. |

## The repair (LOOSE_OWN, off)

1. **The gates are lifted.** His own sighting decides recognition and re-sighting; the true state stays in the
   producer. A ball he still sees loose in his delayed picture is still loose to him. The recovery reaches him through
   PossessionCue, which wraps every player on the recovery: his picture of the holder carrying, or the call.
2. **A called player with no sighting runs toward the teammate whose call he heard.**
   - That teammate is `caller()`: the nearest one who recognized the loose ball by the listener's own observation
     delay, within CALL_RANGE.
   - The fumbler himself still goes to where he stands.
   - With no caller and no sighting, he holds (as before when nothing was known).
