## Withheld air-arrival experiment and new diagnostic findings

A separate archived experiment replaces the static late-air fallback with
bounded moving arrival using the same movement capacities. It turns the retained
Cover 2/right curl-flat TD from 70 to 22.40 yards, but changes the ordinary
PA post-dig/left tackle from 41.96 to a 70-yard TD. It is withheld. PA/right
remains a 70-yard TD; do not imply the experiment repairs every tail.

The enriched parent observer distinguishes raw behavior commands, coverage
containment and final applied World intents. Cover 2/right SS at 4.20/4.25 s
requests (+3.920,+6.866) / (+4.793,+6.850), while both applied intents are zero.
The stops come from spacing/refinement, not pursuit requesting a stop. At 2.75 s
the raw, contained and applied late-air command is the same (-6.883,-.009).
This separates an early static approach from a later pipeline override.

The PA mirror also exposes ambiguous airborne receiving windows. At the first
experimental fork, 3.35 s, LB_M selects an early window near the blocking TE Y:
the window is at 3.40 s, only .05 s away, while the later reachable catch spot
is at 4.60 s. Propagating that incidental candidate's motion disrupts a later
legitimate wrap. The right corner has no such window in the inspected air
fallbacks. An observed candidate is not the eventual catcher. A future repair
must preserve legitimate dump-offs, deflections and unknown windows without
peeking at the intended throw target.

Independent pre-catch review proves no safety roof bust in these two tails:
Cover 2's corner legitimately exchanges underneath while SS retains the deep
half, and PA's FS follows the installed run cap before throw reaction. Native
G1-P077 Power's two broken tackles are actual weak reaches that saturate and
slip. None of these observations justifies raising grip strength.
