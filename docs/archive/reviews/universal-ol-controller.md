# A common OL controller

## Direction

Keep one shared run-block controller and the reduced physical World. A run call
installs a responsibility and permitted responses; the lineman reads his own
picture, requests an attainable approach, and receives the actual physical
result. Improving that loop is more valuable now than expanding anatomy or
adding separate strength laws for every scheme.

The [work-exchange increment](ol-work-exchanges.md) repairs one violation of
that loop: changing the target must also classify the new job using the
recipient's own picture. The [zone-helper increment](zone-helper-check-and-climb.md)
adds original uncovered inside/split-zone check and climb, with personal optional
cadence and real keeper admissibility. Its rejected shared-clock candidate and
mixed final results are retained. The
[joined-attention increment](joined-combination-attention.md) now gives both
members personal confirmation and current self-departure proposals. Native
adverse traces expose plausible changed reads rather than hidden shared timing;
rushing efficiency remains mixed.

## What trained should mean

For the current engine, players are assumed to know the installed call. They
need an immediate responsibility, a permitted useful blocking surface, a
partner/backer relation where appropriate, and local priorities for slants and
penetration. Knowing a call does not confer extra force or guarantee yards.

`Player.familiarity` is position comfort, not practice in a call. Per-call
mastery is not implemented. A later management game can model practiced
installations through interpretation, communication, confidence and placement.
Do not relabel position familiarity or add a mastery multiplier to physical
strength in the current repair.

Existing recognition/awareness govern observation delay and decision cadence;
focus and stress can slow both. Consistency/readiness produce coherent execution
variation. Run-block skill contributes placement precision and sustain, while
hand technique affects the real hand fight. The body still constrains traction,
reach, acceleration, braking and support. First ensure those existing inputs
actually reach the relevant player's decisions.

## Minimum shared structure

| Layer | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Installation | Own a surface/threat, reserve a pull/kick/wrap role, or check/help a partner before the assigned backer |
| Personal observation | Read nearby DL, partner and backer through the player's own delayed picture and cadence |
| Job choice | Keep, collect, exchange, climb or recover for an identifiable football cue |
| Physical request | Choose a useful shoulder/path, controlled arrival, posture and push/seal direction |
| Achieved response | Use actual hands, support, force and displacement to determine what really happens |

This is a separation of responsibilities, not five new classes or a general
cognition framework. Keep the useful existing installation memory, classification,
zone reads, local work search, bounded fit functions and contact ownership.
Neither a job label nor an assigned target certifies useful contact or a lane.

The down threat comes first. An optional helper may leave only when his own
near responsibility is clear and the actual keeper can retain the physical job.
A sustained Duo/down double, OZ overtake and IZ check are different installed
relationships executing through the same controller and capacities. They should
not share a blanket departure exception simply because each can have two blockers.

## Repaired attention defect

The prior source-`ff641a8` ordinary panel, described in the
[work-exchange review](ol-work-exchanges.md#remaining-limits-and-next-work), has 190
combinations, each with only one scheduled reader; 96 suppress a partner with a
shorter initial decision interval. These are historical attention counts, not
counts of the new helper's private checks. Shared
`combo.nextRead` lets the first member in iteration do both people's reading.
This prevents a good guard's recognition from helping the pair.

The current repair uses two finite member states and a pre-command pair pass,
not a new engine-wide scheduler. A member proposes his own departure from his
own delayed picture; current physical ownership remains shared. Simultaneous
proposals have a stable resolver, tested with conflicting pictures and reversed
read order. Physical ineligibility can stop an attention attempt before eyes
consume anything. The six natural witnesses have no pair where both consume
visual surface pictures, despite seven pairs scheduling both members; controlled
good-fit tests cover that case. Reading more often never changes force caps.

## Highest-value remaining common defect

In Power/Under/left/S3, the backside RT reaches the instructed cutoff shoulder,
but the acquired contact frame treats that shoulder as severe lost leverage.
The nominal aim is almost tangent to that frame; tiny angle changes also switch
the approach between controlled recovery and the original sprint. Native hands
arrive, stay within shared caps, then shed for leverage. A successful inside-shade
cutoff shows why resetting every frame would hide real bad fits.

Start with the [pinned native diagnosis](../../../data/reviews/physics-world/combo-attention/cutoff-next-witness.md).
Separate installed useful contact surface from displacement intention only where
the contradiction is reproduced. Preserve genuine achieved geometry, normal
inside-shade and natural opposite-side controls. A better trained blocker must
be able to request a coherent shoulder and arrive there through the existing
body limits; the installation must not punish that shoulder as the wrong one.

## Subsequent work and acceptance

After the cutoff contradiction, diagnose recovery/collection and contextual engaged footwork.
Current offensive foot/hand traits emphasize pass-block ratings even during
run work. Review that through a reproduced specialist-control failure; do not
fix it by increasing strength or traction. Also preserve the distinction between
a stale picture, a wrong instruction and an unattainable physical request.

Accept improvements when the same ordinary profiles produce more coherent jobs,
useful attainable approaches and real loaded contact across concepts/fronts,
including hard and adverse cases. Hold bodies fixed when testing recognition;
better reads should reduce a reproduced missed opportunity, not guarantee a
better rushing gain in every seed. Use conditional NFL movement/path/contact
distributions as references and keep generated-roster variation visible.

Follow the [practical validation policy](practical-validation-and-stable-physics.md):
one diagnosed mechanism, focused regressions, immutable matched baseline reuse,
native observations, then one final frozen full suite. Physics remains the shared
foundation; coherent force accounting alone cannot certify correct OL decisions.
