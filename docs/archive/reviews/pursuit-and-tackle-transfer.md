# Pursuit, support and contact transfer

## Status and where to start

The most useful breakthrough in this increment is making the existing shared
physics execute a coherent standing tackle. An automatic technique request
was giving away a secured holder's footing. Removing that request makes the
same retained contact finish physically earlier and substantially improves
its local refinement stability. This supports the reduced-model direction;
it does not establish universal physics readiness or NFL calibration.

## Five repaired instructions

1. **Pursuit arrival:** the old incoming-heading classifier could change a
   requested speed by about 3.3 yd/s across a tiny bearing change. Near arrival
   now shares the body's actual fatigue/support braking budget. Early gathering
   requires the observed path to enter the contact corridor; distant oblique
   pursuit retains urgency. Known force/alley shoulders fade into contact.
2. **Waiting to grab:** only an affirmative forecast from the consumed picture
   and command actually held can justify waiting for better contact. An
   excluded free-body forecast no longer inherits an ideal current-state
   trajectory. Desperate reaches and weak physical holds remain possible.
3. **Late man matching:** an already-too-deep player no longer inherits his own
   depth as a retreat floor after a visible release. He closes toward the
   existing receiver-relative cushion. Release knowledge persists through a
   later plant; idle, press, roof and trail controls remain distinct.
4. **Traffic:** when a turn forecasts conflict, a half-speed request can replace
   a full stop if its achieved free movement stays upright and clears every
   consumed teammate picture. The existing zero fallback and contact/load
   exclusions remain. Already-moving bodies still collide when braking fails.
5. **Secured side/rear tackling:** the holder keeps running his feet into the
   hip rather than automatically diving when grip quality crosses .55. Explicit
   dives/lunges, extended grounded holds, grip slip and genuine falls remain.

Each change shapes intent or a contact choice. It does not move a body,
increase strength or prescribe final yards. The component reviews explain
[arrival](tackle-approach-controller-audit.md),
[independent acquisition](tackle-approach-independent-review.md),
[late matching](man-cushion-and-yac-audit.md),
[moving gathers](teammate-spacing-moving-gather.md) and
[standing tackle support](tackle-support-choice.md).

## Remaining work and decision rule

Keep this compact controller/World boundary. Reopen a physical law when a
feasible, correctly observed request has unexplained force, work or support;
trace the first divergence before increasing solver resolution.

- Diagnose full-game run tails by concept, installed fit and acquired pursuit
  cues before changing movement capacity. Ordinary panel mean and game mean
  describe different mixtures and roster populations.
- Separate physical support loss, forward-progress whistles, QB grasp and the
  inherited four-second hold cap. The cap is not physical tackle validation.
- Address conservative movement-forecast exclusions for residual lean only
  after an independently achievable held-command witness supports expansion.
- Man construction still reads live initial cushion. Coverage help/roof,
  visible possession and protection/private rush information remain debts.
- Grounded drag, recovery and rating-to-body mapping retain empirical
  uncertainty. Reduced tipping is not full vertical biomechanics; planar
  accounting does not measure hanging weight or actual human gaze.

The next improvements should be demonstrated across ordinary football and
adverse cases using the same physical capacities. Do not fit strength or
traction to rescue a bad assignment or desired league average.
