## Bounded repairs

### A coherent installed cutoff surface

For a solo backside cutoff against a clearly outside down threat, install the
intended inside shoulder separately from the displacement request. Approach it
from the blocker's own delayed picture. Initialize a new run contact in that
frame before grading the actual strike/arrival. Never infer a favorable frame
from where the blocker happened to arrive or rotate existing hands to erase a
lost shoulder. Clear the target-bound installation when the job changes.

Inside-shade cutoffs, doubles, pullers and pass protection retain their existing
frames. The narrow application follows the reproduced contradiction, rather
than adding a new rule to every block. Mass, traction, strength, drive and
support laws remain unchanged. Good containment can still produce a bad run;
the standalone Power probes have worse yards and later initial contact.

### Delayed QB body-direction inference: not promoted

The experiment removes `sim.qbEyes` and the selected receiver's true current
position from ZoneCoverage. It consumes a delayed QB picture and compares that broad direction
with receiver pictures it already consumed. A direction preference requires
observed movement above 1 yd/s or observed depth beyond the LOS. This is a
coarse movement/depth gate, not knowledge that the hidden route has released.
Underneath defenders squeeze
observed lanes; deep defenders may lean between verticals while keeping owned
vertical priority. Similar bearings remain ambiguous. An unavailable directional
cue preserves responsibility rather than guessing an exact target. Empty grass
does not become a target merely because the QB's torso points toward it.

This is a coarse **body-direction proxy**, not head or eye tracking. The
135-degree shoulder-glance sector and 35-degree directional cone are explicit
model assumptions, not NFL measurements. Gun drop and evade body poses can be
poor signals; the movement/depth threshold is also an effective assumption.
There is one added directional QB query reused across branches;
the common live-carrier checks may already query the QB too. No query acquires
another player's private intention or consumes a new random draw.

The first implementation (`f641f128…`) failed this applicability rule. Native
traces showed changed SS/LB instructions at 0.20 seconds with neutral QB facing,
unmoved delayed receiver pictures and no private QB read. Its body-ray fallback
steered the initial drop anyway. Four additional controls fail that candidate
and pass both C and the final rule. The rejected source and all fixed-panel
outcomes remain retained. Changing only the cue policy restores the large
FourVerts/Cover4 regression toward its C-like scramble/slide; other passing
regressions persist and are not concealed as coverage improvement.

The narrowed version still gives visible upright torso direction confidence one.
Receiver movement validates the receiver picture, not whether a QB's drop or
evade pose expresses his intended read. Native passing and matched game checks
worsened. Independent reviewers recommended withholding the whole proxy, rather
than fitting its cone or confidence to final yards. Production ZoneCoverage is
restored exactly to C; the private-read shortcut remains explicitly known debt.
Next isolate oracle removal from directional weighting on one retained adverse
pass, with owned-vertical and ordinary controls, before retrying promotion.

### Run-entry corridor prototype: not promoted

The bounded prototype checks an entry's approach against delayed blockers and
the runner's achievable short movement, rather than awarding an open lane from
its midpoint. A closed approach may call for gathering; visible penetration
must retain an escape. A near-face mouth waypoint preserves the existing Duo
directional read, but predicted versus executed shoulder squeeze, loaded-body
scope and the unsafe-patience escape need stronger proof. The prototype was
saved and production carrier code restored exactly to C. It is not an installed
RB read system or part of the final field comparison. Next test one pair's
mouth/speed rule before expanding the entry-selection policy.

## Movement: what the NFL evidence actually says

We have extracted 176,707 selected game paths and 6,310 combine paths, with
athlete-matched drill/game comparisons. This is useful movement-demand evidence,
but not complete all-22 run/contact tracking. The combine cohort omits RB, QB
and LB. Historical engine movement panels retain their original source labels.

An additional 18-path, 72-trial check at baseline C retained finite upright motion,
bounded traction and tiny work residuals. Several capacity/control differences
persist: the football capacity mapping cannot reproduce maximum-effort drill
paths, while a known-path controller overchases some turns and brakes differently
from the observed path. Same-path measured sprint fitting is a diagnostic,
not holdout predictive accuracy. Increasing global burst would mix those causes.

Before another physical parameter change, use the same filtering/differentiation
operator for NFL and engine paths, bin by role and task, and compare requested
pace/facing with achieved speed/braking/curvature. Use the already available
`o` and `dir` fields to distinguish backpedal, shuffle and hip turns. Later,
separate athletic capacity from chosen football effort at the command interface;
do not reduce every player's capacity because average game effort is lower.

## The next steps that generalize

1. Repair teammate traffic prediction with achieved short motion. Reuse the
   production movement preview only for free bodies near a material conflict;
   loaded/gripped bodies need conservative headway, not a free-space oracle.
   Keep genuine unavoidable collisions and clean-lane controls.
2. Pilot a small LB/safety attention contract: primary key, peripheral summary,
   last-seen memory and an actual reacquisition cost. Tie key changes to observed
   declarations, retain uncertainty, and keep touch/hand loads distinct from
   vision. Do not impose a global scan budget before one role is demonstrated.
3. Make pursuit gather/angle/force handoffs continuous, then limit new non-contact
   wraps to attainable shoulder/reach configurations. Actual impacts and existing
   grips remain physical/haptic interactions. Keep faster-runner escape controls.
4. Isolate the remaining coverage information shortcut from the unvalidated
   torso interpretation. Trace one adverse Cover3Match route through first
   responsibility, pace, hip turn and post-catch pursuit changes. Separately,
   build a consistently measured movement reference panel before changing
   capacity/effort mapping, and diagnose option-branch mismatches from native
   evidence with ordinary and adverse controls.

Underlying physics still has numerical debt at contact/support transitions and
empirical uncertainty in grip, recovery and rating-to-capacity mappings. The
stable laws justify these focused controller changes; they do not justify a
universal physics OK, perfect NFL calibration or a percentage of gains exhausted.
The owner can help most with source-labelled paired clips identifying the
expected first step, key, gather and acceptable mistake, rather than another
large generic motion dataset.

## What shipped and how it was checked

Only the installed cutoff surface shipped. Seventeen new controlled tests
cover actual mirrored movement, loaded hands, bad arrival, stale pictures,
acquired-frame preservation, pass compatibility and job cleanup. The pinned
baseline fails 13 feature cases, passes two controls, and deliberately reuses
cached evidence for two ordinary native controls.

The 160 ordinary run inputs plus two separate supplements match cached C's
players, profiles and common initial state. All recorded/plain controls match;
all finishes are natural. Four full native witnesses record the intentionally
new installation and actual hand/support loads. The two Power walls achieve
inside containment across 50/42 solved loaded samples; reacquisition remains
visible. The inside-shade Split controls preserve events and yards. Under
cutoff-only source their complete packed trajectories match cached C.

These mixed results, all six losses of at least two yards and all five gains
remain saved. Containment is the verified repair; neither efficiency nor NFL
accuracy is asserted. The old collector captures only five update-local climbs;
whole-play events count the other 109, rather than treating them as missing.

The final source passes **829 TS tests in 73 files**, type checking and build;
the unchanged Python data tools pass **22 tests**. The combined, unpromoted
QB candidate had passed 845 tests, yet worsened the native pass panel and two
matched game checks. This is why controller correctness requires football
witnesses beyond green unit tests.
