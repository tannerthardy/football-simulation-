# Draw presentation and individual run work

This bounded OL repair separates an installed initial presentation from the
lineman's combo, covered or second-level responsibility. It changes no shared
World law, movement capacity, strength, grip or block-win rule. The ordinary
zone and gap controllers keep their existing movement and contact.

## The reproduced instruction

The planner gave covered Draw owners a `draw` kind but deliberately retained
`combo` and `climb` kinds for other work. `RunBlocker.update` treated only the
`draw` kind as a pass presentation. Its helpers therefore fired low while
their covered partners retreated high. An uncovered center could immediately
charge the linebacker before the rest of the line finished showing pass.

On source `8bc3eec8…`, native Gun Doubles Draw against Over/Cover3, roster
seeds31/32, strength.55, snap seed3, LOS40 and both flips reproduce this:

- Right: at .05s LG requests (+.77,−1.21) yd/s low while keeper C requests
  (−1.99,0) high. At .30s LG requests +7.59 yd/s downfield while C still
  retreats at −1.85. LG makes actual run contact at .60s.
- Left: at .05s uncovered C requests (+6.09,+.63) yd/s low while covered
  owners retreat. At .90s C has crossed LOS to x40.66, while LT is at x38.96.

These are contradictions within this engine's installed one-second Draw
presentation. They do not establish that every NFL Draw lineman should wait
exactly one second; some installations deliberately release a blocker sooner.
The existing phase is an authored motor/presentation law, not a visual read
or newly calibrated NFL duration.

## The repair

`RunBlockPlan.presentation` now records the installed Draw presentation
independently of `kind`. Original helpers and climbers keep their target and
eventual responsibility while showing the same initial pass presentation.
Existing `draw` owners retain their exact `goTo` formula; standalone manually
installed `draw` jobs remain compatible. The normal snap response still wins.

Unavailable original work can still cancel a double and choose ordinary new
work. The presentation survives that job change. A personally observed body
penetrating the player's existing near-gap collection corridor can instead
trigger the normal attack and real contact before the phase ends. The cue is
held through his ordinary attention beat. An unseen live penetration cannot
trigger it, and an old reachable picture cannot create hands on a remote body.
Acquired hands remain physical; the defender can win them. The repair does
not grant an emergency block success.

While presenting, a joined player cannot abandon the surface for his assigned
backer merely because a sustained combo read qualifies. At phase end, the
existing personal reads and physical departure eligibility resume. This keeps
the installation distinct from an unconditional mandatory wait that disables
collisions or immediate penetration collection.

## What remains

Draw currently uses a simple high presentation and set landmark. It is not a
complete pass-to-run footwork or pass-hand transition model. One-second timing,
public reach laws and the usual private availability shortcuts remain
assumptions/debt; this change neither calibrates them nor removes them all.

The three retained IZ/Split entry cases were also audited read-only. All 14
actual entry decisions have upright live and delayed defender poses, so the
remaining `runReadAim` live-fall shortcut has no demonstrated fork there.
Healthy-target OL abandonment is likewise unproven. Do not add automatic
climbs, force bonuses or generic lane guarantees from either hypothesis.

The coaching distinction is documented in
[offensive-line technique](offensive-line-run-technique.md),
[the common OL controller](universal-ol-controller.md) and
[the trench handbook](../../football-handbook/trenches.md). They distinguish
installation, acquired evidence, requested action and achieved physical work;
generated textbook aids are not universal NFL coaching authorities.
