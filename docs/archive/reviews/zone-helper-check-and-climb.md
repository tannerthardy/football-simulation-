## Installed rule and execution

Inside/split zone installs an optional check policy for the original uncovered
member, including his original gap and side of the covered partner. Other zone,
gap, Duo and Draw combinations retain their sustained-double policy.

The helper reads his own delayed owner and DL pictures. The owner must appear
upright and in a plausible visible contact envelope; the DL must remain on the
owner's far half with useful leverage. The helper checks his original/current
gap for replacing near-level threats. He then reads the assigned backer and RB:
a filling backer or arriving runner supplies the football trigger. The observed
DL must clear the approach corridor now and over the next decision interval.
This short visual forecast is not a physical reachability certificate.

Physical admissibility is separate from knowledge. The owner must actually
retain genuine hands, support and useful fit. The helper must actually be free
of engagement and foreign restraints. The existing control, leverage and finite
stability gates remain. Exact owner control/support are not information handed
to the helper as a perfect visual read.

The transition preserves the owner's contact object, acquired frame, control,
stored angle and penalties. It installs the existing vertical second-level
approach and records `check` in the climb event. World determines the achieved
motion, contact and force. Joined doubles keep their ordinary release behavior.

## A rejected candidate exposed a timing bug

The first candidate reused the double's shared read clock for the optional
helper. It passed 790 tests and produced five sensible check climbs, but native
review found an unrelated joined release shifted earlier. Split Zone/Bear/right/
S11 fell 5.12→3.00 yd with no check-climb event. Optional reads had changed the
normal double's cadence before the helper joined.

The final rule gives the optional helper his own `checkNextRead` and confirmation
state. The original shared schedule continues its ordinary unjoined bookkeeping;
it never inherits the helper's optional phase. A native regression compares the
joined reads/contact and earned release against the same World with optional
policy disabled. The rejected candidate fails that control. Final Bear results
and events return exactly to baseline, including releases at 1.65/2.85s.
Its entire final signature still differs, so this is not a claim that every
tag/private state is identical. The [unpromoted evidence](../../../data/reviews/physics-world/zone-helper-check/pre-cadence/README.md)
retains the diagnosis, source delta, tests and adverse result.

## Limits to keep visible

The unchanged collector omits private helper clocks, the entire pre-update
World-joint list, and TE-only body impulse rows. Recorded owner/DL/backer/RB
images and present corridor geometry are native evidence; exact private dwell,
future horizon and foreign-grip rejection are source/controlled-test evidence.
A missing TE impulse is not zero force.

Replay positions are recorded after physics with tick-start timestamps. The
native inspector separately requires `completedAt <= visual time`, so it can
trail displayed bodies by one tick; the final +.05s solve lies outside the seek
range. Neither phase was silently retimed. Correct the shared recording stamp
separately before claiming precise frame/contact synchronization.

Joined combinations still share one reader and can select another member's
departure using that reader's picture. Per-call learning/mastery is absent;
generated players are assumed to know their installation. Existing familiarity
means position comfort. Fix personal attention and conflicting proposals next,
then diagnose recovery/collection and contextual run footwork using the same
physical capabilities and matched ordinary/adverse inputs.
