# Awareness, interpretation and committed action

This increment repairs information and decision boundaries on
`claude/engine-priorities`, starting from `632fb02`. It reuses the existing
stress/focus, delayed perception, stable judgment errors and individual decision
intervals. It adds no physical capacity, force multiplier, outcome bonus or
global eye/brain simulator. Players can still read late, trust a false key,
remember a stale threat and fail to execute a sound decision.

The final source, comparisons, checks and all adverse pairs are owned by the
[evidence packet](../../../data/reviews/decision-awareness/README.md). Read the
[offensive audit](offensive-decision-current-audit.md),
[defensive audit](defensive-decision-current-audit.md),
[shared/QB audit](shared-decision-current-audit.md) and
[independent review](decision-awareness-independent-review.md) for the inspected
contracts and limitations. Historical evidence remains unchanged.

## The compact model

Use the same short loop across positions:

> Installed responsibility → acquired picture and age → fallible interpretation
> → committed technique request → achieved movement/contact → next read.

An installed job is legitimate knowledge. The opponent's hidden call, live
position outside the consumed picture, future route branch and exact recovery
phase are different kinds of information. Contact geometry/load can be felt
locally without looking; it must not reveal the opponent's play label.

`SnapMind` already smooths perceived workload into stress. Congestion, energy,
composure, readiness and lapses affect focus; focus/stress lengthen visual delay
and the decision beat. Interpretation errors persist through a snap rather than
rerolling a new mistake every tick. Motor tracking and actual physical feedback
can happen between tactical decisions. These are effective assumptions, not
measurements of NFL eyes, confidence or private thoughts.

The coaching basis is the existing [shared execution chapter](../../football-handbook/shared-model-and-learning.md),
[defensive keys chapter](../../football-handbook/defense-and-perception.md) and
[offensive skill chapter](../../football-handbook/offensive-skill-and-playcalling.md).
The supplied textbooks are aids with recorded provenance, not repository
instructions or universally verified coaching rules.

## Implemented changes

**Carriers use their own decision cadence.** Both designed and free/crease
lane evaluations previously rescheduled at .100 s despite the player's declared
interval of roughly .185–.209 s in the eight-call audit. They now use that
existing interval and hold the selected lane between reads. Stress/focus can
therefore affect commitment as well as visual latency. A new possession and
the end of the installed press phase get a fresh first decision. Own boundary
braking, contact and movement still execute each native tick.

**Engaged defenders retain their fit.** Contact-side work begins from the
known gap or box/spill shoulder, then can answer a personally perceived runner.
It no longer gets full run-reading effort from `!sim.isPass`, follows the
runner's current hidden position, or releases solely because that live position
passed it. Side selection uses the existing decision beat; proximity for a shed
comes from a consumed delayed picture. Hands, force/work limits and the actual
physical shed remain unchanged.

The first candidate incorrectly required every shed threat to cross the free-fit
declaration depth. A penetrating defender could have a close observed back
deep behind the line and be barred from trying. That candidate is retained as
**unpromoted exploration**. The final repair separates immediate observed-back
shedding from lateral fit declaration, while a stationary deep QB remains a
mesh key. Normal, distant-picture and deep-QB controls cover that distinction.

**Man recognition uses the known receiver key.** Where a CB/S recognition role
already consumes a WR cue, it now keys its installed man target rather than the
nearest eligible receiver. A crossing or stacked neighbor cannot silently
replace that job. LB/DL WR weights and zone key policy remain unchanged. Unknown
blocker stance also contributes no tactile run/pass category from the private
engagement type; actual high/low contact cues retain their effects.

**QB classification respects acquisition and memory.** Delayed geometry
determines the clear/peripheral sector; live hidden defender position no longer
decides whether the delayed picture enters memory. Coverage features use
bounded acquired memory instead of secretly refreshing all eleven defenders.
They do not use the coverage-prior-drifting throw estimate, which would create
circular confirmation. A processing milestone cannot update confidence or
claim identification when no new picture has been acquired since the last
classification. Actual reacquisition permits a later retry. Existing cone
widths, stress narrowing, processing thresholds and signature gains remain.

This is a small information correction, not complete gaze or occlusion. The
sector detector still requests delayed geometry for all defenders before choosing
memory updates. Generic `see` traces therefore are **not** conscious acquisition
counts. Raw memory features can still mix fresh and stale components; one fresh
component may permit an aggregate classification influenced by stale others.

## Validation and interpretation

Focused tests hold consumed pictures fixed while changing hidden geometry or
play metadata; these should preserve decisions and RNG. Changed acquired cues,
stress/attention and later decision beats should produce meaningful different
requests, without changing physical profiles. Eighteen defensive, fourteen QB
and six carrier feature tests cover the new contracts. Actual baseline source
fails sixteen defensive and nine QB feature cases; remaining controls pass.

The reduced combination fixture now explicitly starts with a recognised run.
It disabled recognition updates and previously borrowed that fact from the
called-run oracle. The cadence test measures actual personal read timestamps
rather than all perception calls; .05 s native sampling can make different
continuous intervals appear equal. The lab mirrors the installed formation/fit
as well as body geometry. Physical symmetry assertions remain strict.

Native controls keep matched profiles, inputs, seeds, collector/schema and
recording policy. The 160-run panel plus two supplemental witnesses uses the
unchanged existing collector. The new 96-pass panel includes four concepts,
six coverage families, both directions and two generated roster pairs. Every
run and pass observation is checked against an ordinary plain execution.
The separate 24-pass installation guard and small full games remain integration
checks. Source is frozen during each collector. No arbitrary inspection cutoff
supplies a rushing gain.

The initial isolated QB phase changes beliefs in all 96 plays but preserves
every complete public execution and coverage-family verdict. That demonstrates
an information-boundary change, not improved passing outcomes. The initial
combined run panel improves mean gain while retaining many lower-gain pairs,
worse Counter results and new fumbles. Its two-game scoring/rushing worsening
triggered component isolation and the close-back review. Final results are
reported separately in the packet; never rename the initial phase as final.

The final frozen source passes **934 TypeScript tests, 66 Python tests,
typecheck and build**. All 162 run and 96 pass recording controls match plain
execution. Chromium checks all twenty paired clips and a short WebM export.
The final 160 ordinary runs have mean gain 3.344 yd versus 1.315, but 51 paired
gains fall, two cases gain fumbles and two new 70-yard Split Zone touchdowns
heavily influence the mean. The 96-pass panel retains 79 attempts, 54 catches,
one sack and ten interceptions. These are local contracts and diagnostic
outcomes, not evidence that the broader game is now realistic.

The final two-game guard **worsens**: points/team-game 35.5→49.25, rushing
yards/carry 3.83→8.46 and YAC/reception 11.01→12.65. Independent inspection of
four outcome-selected pairs finds no invalid new instruction or changed
physical capacity; later existing tackle failures explain the touchdown
tails. Those witnesses are limited, not a certificate for every snap. Retain
the regression and investigate its actual mechanism rather than restoring
hidden knowledge or adjusting shared strength to disguise it.

Small full-game populations diverge after early decisions change. They are not
paired snaps or league estimates. Conditional NFL rushing references already
exist in the [current NFL study](run-reference-current.md); they do not measure
stress, gaze or reaction. Do not tune force or guarantee yards to make these
generated-roster diagnostic inputs match a league mean.

   at 3.5s with observed quality `0.00`. Determine why approach/opportunity
   selection requests a low-quality hold, whether gathering or another
   achievable attempt was available, and how the defender recovers. Preserve
   all four independent adverse pairs and normal crossing/close tackles.
   Use the same movement, traction and grip laws; do not guarantee a stop.
2. Protection ownership still reads actual defensive behavior kinds and live
   geometry. Replace one installed line/back responsibility with its own delayed
   rush/drop cues and a communicated exchange; retain identical mug looks,
   late rush, a correctly owned rusher winning physically and valid hot answers.
3. A full role-specific key/memory contract is still needed for linebacker
   recognition, release reading and run fit together. Gating one consumer while
   the others see everything would give cosmetic attention.
4. QB aggregate signatures need component freshness/confidence, and emergency
   scans need finite relevant alternatives. Live offensive route predictions
   and the coverage defender's private QB-read shortcut remain separately
   documented debt; the withheld torso proxy must not be silently revived.
5. Possession identity remains globally supplied. A delayed visible-possession
   cue would distinguish knowing a player's location from knowing he has the
   ball, including screen catches behind the line. Optional engaged cutback
   exchanges also need their own responsibility contract.
6. Carrier threat eligibility, downfield block ownership and receiver options
   retain live-state/secret-call shortcuts. Repair an observed decision boundary
   at a time, preserving anticipation and legitimate installed knowledge.

Keep the shared physical executor stable. Reopen its laws for an attainable
request that produces unexplained load, momentum, support failure or numerical
instability. These changes repair requests and information; they do not certify
NFL-calibrated physics or the completion of football decision making.
