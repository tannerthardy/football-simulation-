## Problem and change

Adjacent unengaged linemen already exchange crossing defenders when both
lateral assignments improve. The exchange changed target identity without
changing technique. A former climber could inherit a near-level defensive
lineman yet continue using a second-level approach; another blocker could
inherit a deeper threat while retaining first-level work.

Both recipients now classify the new job from their own consumed delayed
picture. A threat seen more than two yards beyond the line calls for a climb;
a shallower threat restores the blocker’s installed solo technique and drive
direction. The roster's position label does not decide. A recipient who has
not updated yet first remembers his original installation. Pull, lead, stalk
and draw responsibilities are protected symmetrically from this exchange.
Existing live-contact, combination and mutually useful crossing guards remain.

The decision changes plans, not bodies or physical joints. World then executes
the bounded movement/contact request. No force multiplier, solver-resolution
change, new anatomical detail or outcome adjustment is involved.

## Remaining limits and next work

The exchange proposal still comes from the initiating player's crossing
picture. The recipient independently classifies the job but does not yet
independently accept the proposed exchange. Per-call mastery and trained
communication are not implemented; current player familiarity means position
comfort. Ratings must affect reading/execution, not guarantee a block or yards.

The next reproduced defect is a mandatory sustained double: the original
uncovered IZ/split-IZ helper can keep chasing a defender already held securely
by the covered owner instead of checking the local threat and climbing. Start
with Split Zone/Tite/right/S3. Require the helper's own delayed owner/DL/backer
picture, no replacing penetration, and actual admissible keeper contact. Do
not turn the keeper's exact support/control into free visual knowledge. Preserve
the real owner's hand object and use existing second-level movement.
Scope the first rule to original IZ/split-IZ helpers; Duo/gap/OZ need their own
responsibility proof, not a blanket exemption from double-team requirements.

A separate limitation is shared combination attention: the first member in
iteration advances `combo.nextRead` for both. The saved ordinary panel has 190
combination groups and every one has only one scheduled observer. Independent
member reads should be an isolated later change, with order/cadence controls.

These repairs make a common controller more useful without expanding physical
detail. The long-term pattern is installation → personal observation → job
choice → achievable approach/contact → achieved physical response. Keep those
layers distinct and retain normal, difficult and adverse football inputs.
